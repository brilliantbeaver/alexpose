## Gait Dataset for Knee Osteoarthritis and Parkinson's Disease Analysis With Severity Levels
Published: 24 December 2020 | Version 1
DOI: 10.17632/44pfnysy89.1
Contributors: Navleen Kour, Sunanda Gupta, Sakshi Arora
Institutions: Shri Mata Vaishno Devi University
Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/44pfnysy89/1

Thanks for your interest in our dataset. This dataset is provided for research purpose only, not for any commercial purposes. 
KOA-PD-NM Gait video dataset (in folder KOA-PD-NM) comprises of 96 subjects which involves 50 Knee Osteoarthritis (KOA), 16 Parkinson's Disease (PD) and 30 Normal/Healthy (NM) subjects with 2 sequences(left to right and right to left) for each subject in frontal/saggital plane.The dataset (each video in MOV format) is collected using a single NIKON DSLR 5300 camera placed 8m away from the walking mat in the hospital area and a set of 6 red-colored passive reflective markers has been attached to the subject’s body joints.

* Sub-Folder KOA includes KOA Gait video files of all sequences. 
Total video files (KOA): 100 also including three disease severity levels i.e.Early (EL), Moderate (MD) and Severe (SV) as
Total video files (KOA_EL): 30
Total video files (KOA_MD): 40
Total video files (KOA_SV): 30

* Sub-Folder  PD includes PD Gait video files of all sequences. 
Total video files (PD): 31 also including three disease severity levels i.e.Mild (ML), Moderate (MD) and Severe (SV) as
Total video files (KOA_ML): 12
Total video files (KOA_MD): 14
Total video files (KOA_SV): 5

* Sub-Folder  NM includes NM Gait video files of all sequences. 
Total video files (PD): 60

* The nomenclature to represent each video file includes the information as under:-

1)Knee Osteoarthritis dataset (KOA) dataset:- 

                                             Subject ID_KOA_Sequence No._Severity level

For example:- The fields of video format ‘001_KOA_01_EL’ indicates following:
•	001_KOA represents first KOA subject
•	01 means sagittal/lateral view from left to right
•	EL shows the severity level of KOA (Group: 1-2)
The identical pattern is used to represent video detail of moderate (MD) and severe (SV) KOA patients.

2)	Parkinson’s disease (PD) dataset:- 

                                          Subject ID_PD_Sequence No._Severity level

For example:- The fields of video format ‘001_PD_01_ML’ indicates following:
•	001_PD represents first PD subject
•	01 means sagittal/lateral view from left to right
•	ML shows the severity level of PD
Alike, The meaning of all the fields remain same (i.e. MD implies moderate PD and SV means severe PD).

3)	Normal/healthy (NM) dataset:- 

                              Subject ID_NM_Sequence No.

For example:- The fields of video format ‘001_NM_01’ indicates following:
•	001_NM represents first NM subject
•	01 means sequence no. i.e. sagittal/lateral view from left to right
•	Here, the severity level is not present since it includes the subject’s gait videos having no disorder.
