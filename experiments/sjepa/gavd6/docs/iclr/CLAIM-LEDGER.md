# Claim and evidence ledger

The manuscript describes completed synthetic development experiments. Every version uses the same source study; revisions and post hoc reanalyses are not new training experiments. Scientific figures read retained exports or are explicitly marked algebraic/implementation schematics. Earlier versions retain their review-documented deficiencies. The final manuscript is `versions/v07/paper-v07-final.tex` / `versions/v07/paper-v07-final.pdf`.

The [independent audit](evidence/independent_evidence_audit.py) checks all69 transferred file hashes and reconstructs16 saved primary/secondary comparisons. That verifies the transferred packet and summary calculations, not raw-data training or clinical validity. The original `outputs/gait-fidelity` reports were inspected; its generated tutorial fixture results are excluded. Notebook07 reads `outputs/iclr`; the other notebooks explain operations and unexecuted options as well as completed work.

References were checked against primary papers and official publisher/project sources; the [literature review](reviews/literature-initial.md) records the claim map. The final paper deliberately does not imply reproduction or empirical superiority over cited methods that were not run.
