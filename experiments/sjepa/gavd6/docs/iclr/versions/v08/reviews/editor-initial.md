# v08 initial independent editorial review

Scope: the user's full revision request; v07 manuscript, source, and prior editorial reviews; the current research overview and organization review; authoritative visual precedents; and current official submission instructions. This review does not author the manuscript or figures. Evidence recomputation is a separate review stream.

## Recommended central claim

**In this controlled pose-restoration study, improvements over noisy observations do not establish useful recovery of movement changes: the pooled response score favors zero prediction over every neural variant, while the apparent value of predictive features changes with the readout objective and coexists with trajectory and anatomical-assignment failures. The completed comparisons expose these interpretation limits without resolving an incremental feature-prediction benefit.**

This is a claim about an executed evaluation and the resulting comparisons. It is stronger than observing that one scalar discards information, but narrower than proposing a universally superior objective or declaring predictive representations ineffective. The initial question concerns paired feature supervision; the post hoc assignment analysis asks whether its response ranking agrees with geometric side fidelity. Keep those temporal/evidential roles explicit.

Three supporting contributions should organize the paper:

1. A controlled, paired restoration evaluation that measures response, trajectory, and assignment on projected references while distinguishing physical interventions from observation corruption.
2. An empirical comparison showing why response gains cannot be read as representation gains: the zero-response benchmark, uncertain and readout-dependent feature contrast, and unequal initial auxiliary influence jointly constrain interpretation.
3. A transparent supervision follow-up and post hoc laterality audit: the tested compound objective damages waveform accuracy, scalar downweighting accounts for most of the mean repair gain, and global naming swaps expose assignment failures that response alone does not summarize.

## Proposed question-led sequence

Introduction: why representation learning for restoration must preserve movement and side identity; what closely related approaches optimize; the controlled question and three supported contributions. Then define movement states/reference geometry and corruption before objectives. A compact design section should explain the comparison table and distinguish joint training from frozen readouts. Organize results as: (1) does coordinate restoration recover useful movement changes, including zero prediction; (2) is there an incremental feature-prediction benefit, including readout reversal and calibration; (3) what changes when supervision is repaired; and (4) does response ranking agree with side assignment. Close with the supported implication and limitations that require new evidence. The chronological sequence should remain a brief design disclosure.
