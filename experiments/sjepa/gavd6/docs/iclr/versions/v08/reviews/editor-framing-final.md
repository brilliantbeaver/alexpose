## Literature and framing assessment

The primary-source audit is retained in `framing-literature-and-guidelines.md`. The manuscript adds four focused references, bringing the cited-key count to 18; every key resolves. There is no broad citation padding or unsupported priority claim.

- [Padmanabhan et al. (2020)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12984-020-00732-z) supports the specific observation that step-length symmetry can improve while joint mechanics remain asymmetric after stroke. The paper does not generalize that result to every patient or equate the synthetic knee intervention with stroke.
- [Seuthe et al. (2024)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00415-024-12379-0) supports the distinction between measured gait asymmetry and clinical symptom laterality in Parkinson's disease. The manuscript's “need not identify” wording respects that distinction.
- [Creaby et al. (2012)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22385873/) supports different biomechanical asymmetry findings for unilateral and bilateral knee osteoarthritis. The current sentence is appropriately about that relationship, not a general disease classifier. This source audit used the primary abstract; it did not independently inspect inaccessible full text.
- [LeCun (2022)](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=BZ5a1r-kVsf) is correctly used as a position-paper proposal connecting hierarchical JEPA and world models. I-JEPA and video JEPA support the representation-prediction background; neither citation is used to claim that this study performs temporal world modeling. The earlier audit records the access limitation for the full LeCun PDF and its corroborating primary author talk.

The existing S-JEPA/GFP/PoseBERT/MotionBERT/SmoothNet positioning remains accurate and narrow. The paper identifies its privileged reference and deployed-encoder differences and says the external restoration methods were not fitted here. It does not use the uncertain feature contrast as a general result against JEPA or self-supervised learning. Clinical measurement motivation is relevant because the outcomes depend on named anatomical geometry, but it supplies no additional empirical validation.

The revised research journey gives a coherent reason for the sequence of comparisons: initial reference-feature prediction, paired auxiliary comparison, supervision repair, and subsequent naming diagnosis. Table 11 preserves the correct comparator for each stage and separates the crossed bootstrap from the repair's conditional person-t interval. This improves navigation without reclassifying adaptive follow-up as independent confirmation.
