# Scientific argument before rewriting

## Central claim

In this controlled, paired synthetic restoration study, reference-feature prediction does not establish recovery of movement change beyond the tested controls: pooled response error favors a zero-change prediction, the delta–endpoint contrast is uncertain with its point-estimate ordering reversed under a coordinate-only readout, and a lower response point estimate can coexist with worse geometric side assignment. The informative result is the empirical relationship between representation comparisons, downstream supervision, and explicit failure costs—not the elementary fact that summaries discard information.

## Supporting contributions

1. A controlled evaluation links a paired, side-sensitive movement response to coordinate, waveform, and geometric naming fidelity, while separating physical movement transformations from observation-label errors.
2. A complete comparison of the implemented representation and readout families shows where coordinate restoration helps, where zero response remains competitive, and why the tested feature-difference benefit is unresolved under unequal initial auxiliary influence.
3. A targeted readout follow-up and exploratory reliability analysis distinguish absolute waveform improvements, rare costly measurement failures, and post hoc naming errors. They constrain the interpretation of apparent gains without assigning a unique mechanism.

The original research questions concern reference-feature prediction and change supervision. Zero-baseline stratification, penalty sensitivity, and the geometric naming diagnostic are retrospective analyses of completed development exports. No independent confirmation, new training, preregistration, or general failure of predictive representations is claimed.

## Narrative questions

- Does better restoration of noisy coordinates recover movement changes?
- Does predicting feature differences improve on predicting endpoints?
- What can failure accounting explain about the reported contrast?
- Which downstream supervision changes improve trajectories?
- Does response ranking agree with geometric anatomical naming?

Related work precedes the method. Detailed implementation and complete inventories move to the appendix. The original sequence and declared primary comparisons remain in a compact design table and analysis-status statement.
