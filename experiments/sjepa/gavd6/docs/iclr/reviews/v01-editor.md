# Version 01 — independent scope, literature, and natural-writing review

Reviewed `docs/iclr/paper-v01.tex` and its five current figure PNGs. This reviewer did not write the manuscript. The review uses the same eight-dimension rubric for every version. Source-level and rendered-layout observations are distinguished; no PDF page-fit claim is made before compilation.

The paper has a clear task, valuable controls, and unusually candid handling of failed measurements and the zero-response baseline. Its present framing nevertheless omits the closest skeletal feature-prediction work and overstates the relationship between the idealized reflection illustration and the executed 3D manipulation. The main revision should improve that scientific precision before polishing its tone.
