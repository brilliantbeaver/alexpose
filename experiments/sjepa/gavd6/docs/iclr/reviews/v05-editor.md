## Precise language for v06

An abstract sentence can state:

> In a post hoc geometric laterality analysis, delta JEPA's lower response-error point estimate coexists with slightly higher assignment failure (50.46% versus 49.69%), and both feature readouts retain large failures under global left–right renaming.

It is preferable to a claim that “JEPA loses laterality” or “response gains conceal identity failure,” because the architecture comparison is conditional, the response contrast is uncertain, and the assignment difference is exploratory. It also distinguishes a diagnostic of geometrically correct naming from clinically identifying the affected limb.

The introduction can connect the outcomes through this question:

> Does reference-feature prediction improve the signed movement response, and do apparent gains coincide with accurate trajectories and anatomical labeling?

Immediately explain that response contrasts were the declared primary comparisons and the naming-condition analysis is a subsequent diagnostic on the same completed exports. The abstract should retain the zero-response baseline, the primary feature interval, the repeated 14-person development population, and the fact that the weighting result is a ratio of mean gains. Those details determine the interpretation and should take priority over an extra catalog of baseline means.
