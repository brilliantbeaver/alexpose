The recommended direction is **selecting future teacher information that skeleton history can predict beyond a declared current-state reference, then testing whether transferring it improves an independent motion task**. Reflection supplies a precise anatomical diagnostic; time reversal and matched-current-pose trajectories expose whether the representation retains direction. Their combination is useful only when student prediction improves.

The 43-source cached experiment completed successfully but did not establish that benefit. Its primary real-history minus validity-only increment is **+0.001994 R²**, with a conditional 95% source interval **[−0.006673, +0.012608]**. The original direct-v3 development STOP remains unchanged. The paper is an evidence-grounded research draft; successful selective student training and independent confirmation remain necessary for a strong method claim.

The latest source-learning curve, retained in `notebook_runs/haic-run-02`, expands evaluation to **290 recordings and 1,403 windows**. RGB predictive R² rises from **0.451045 to 0.662830**, while the largest-endpoint real-skeleton gain is only **+0.000357099**, below the frozen 0.05 requirement. This completed **development STOP** measures skeleton complementarity after RGB; it does not settle skeleton-only accessibility. The [new research strategy](../scaling/research-strategy.md) develops the result into a conditional target-selection method, controlled mechanism tests, a prior-work comparison, and staged student experiments. The immediate next measurement is a separately frozen, confidence-matched skeleton-only panel on the expanded cache.
