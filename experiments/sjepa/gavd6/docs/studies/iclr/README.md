# A research bridge from laterality to future distillation

The recommended direction is **selecting future teacher information that skeleton history can predict beyond a declared current-state reference, then testing whether transferring it improves an independent motion task**. Reflection supplies a precise anatomical diagnostic; time reversal and matched-current-pose trajectories expose whether the representation retains direction. Their combination is useful only when student prediction improves.

The new cached experiment completed successfully but did not establish that benefit. Its primary real-history minus validity-only increment is **+0.001994 R²**, with a conditional 95% source interval **[−0.006673, +0.012608]**. The original direct-v3 development STOP remains unchanged. The paper is an evidence-grounded research draft; successful selective student training and independent confirmation remain necessary for a strong method claim.
