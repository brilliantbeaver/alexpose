## Decision summary

Keep the first implementation focused on E0 observation/split validation, E1 controlled time-support preservation, and E2 past-only observable forecasting. The literature does not justify replacing these with a large collection of new losses. The consequential corrections are:

1. GaitForeMer uses supervised action labels during pretraining as well as forecasting. It is mixed-supervision transfer evidence, not a pure-SSL clinical success claim.
2. Lower SSL loss, high feature similarity, and a noncollapsed aggregate encoder are insufficient success criteria. Probe the particular branch and the actual predicted future representation.
3. Dense JEPA loss alone has documented global-task costs; compact skeleton tokenization also has explicit high-frequency limitations. Treat both as hypotheses, not defaults.
4. Native video-model sampling is different across generations. In particular, original V-JEPA's frame stride of four is not V-JEPA 2/2.1's four frames per second.
5. Learned equivariance, temporal augmentation invariance, and clinical measurement fidelity are different properties. Neither action recognition nor identity retrieval establishes preserved signed gait dynamics.

## Verified core evidence

The entries distinguish **model/loss/teacher**, **sampling and scale**, and **evaluation/limits**. The implications in the next section are our research decisions, not published GAVD findings. Publication status follows a proceedings/journal record where verified; otherwise the inspected work is labeled a preprint. ArXiv submission history, not a search engine's relative date or dynamic HTML render date, identifies the inspected version.

### Image and video JEPA

**I-JEPA — CVPR 2023; arXiv v3, 2023-04-13, first posted 2023-01-19.** ViT context/predictor learns squared-L2 target-feature regression with a stop-gradient EMA teacher. Four target blocks typically cover 15–20% each; context covers 85–100% before removing overlap. ImageNet pretraining and low-shot/frozen evaluations support semantic representation learning. Its controlled masking ablations require sufficiently informative context and sizeable targets. There is no physical-time, gait, or future-observation experiment. [Method and ablations](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.08243v3), [verified venue listing](https://www.midoassran.ca/).

**V-JEPA — TMLR 2024; arXiv v1, 2024-02-15.** Video ViTs use L1 latent regression, a predictor, and stop-gradient EMA targets. VideoMix2M pretraining samples 16 frames at **stride four native frames**, using approximately 90% multi-block masking. Frozen action evaluation uses multiple clips and attentive probes; reported SSv2 accuracy is 72.2%. Clip duration consequently depends on source FPS. Recognition, including stronger temporal coverage at evaluation, is not evidence of past-only coordinate forecasting. [Method, §4 and appendices](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.08471v1), [venue listing](https://www.midoassran.ca/).

**V-JEPA 2 — inspected preprint v1, 2025-06-11.** L1/EMA masked-latent pretraining scales video/image data and ViTs to 1B parameters. Training progresses from 16 to 64 frames at **4 FPS**; VideoMix22M contains over one million video hours. Frozen recognition/anticipation and separately trained action-conditioned robotics are different evaluations. Longer clips helped tested tasks, but 128/256 frames gave no further improvement. Planning requires robot interaction post-training, not merely the observational encoder. [Method, temporal ablations and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.09985v1), [official code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2).

**V-JEPA 2.1 — inspected preprint v3, 2026-06-11; first posted 2026-03-15.** Dense L1 predicts visible and masked EMA targets; four-level supervision complements it. VisionMix163M image/video training uses 16→64 frames at 4 FPS, 135k+12k updates, EMA 0.99925. Table 1's baseline→context→multi-level results are IN1K **82.2→72.6→80.8**, SSv2 **72.8→62.5→72.1**, NYU RMSE **.682→.474→.463**, ADE20K mIoU **22.2→33.8→38.6**. Thus context loss is not an unqualified improvement. Final models also change scale/data; distilled models use a frozen teacher. [Table 1 and Appendices A/B](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14482v3).

### Skeleton masked prediction

**S-JEPA — ECCV 2024 proceedings.** ViT encoder/predictor learns centered, sharpened target-distribution cross-entropy against an EMA teacher. The published recipe uses 120 temporally resized frames, temporal patches of four, 90% motion-aware masking, an eight-layer/256-wide encoder, and 1,200 epochs with batch 256. NTU60/120 and PKU-MMD evaluations use action cross-subject/view/setup protocols, not clinical targets. Relative-time resizing and action-preserving transformations cannot be assumed to preserve cadence or signed laterality. Local CE+VICReg training is an adaptation, not an exact reproduction. [Official method and implementation details](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/04755.pdf).

**MAMP — ICCV 2023; arXiv v1, 2023-08-14.** A masked ViT encoder/decoder reconstructs normalized joint-motion targets with MSE; this is not an EMA latent-teacher objective. Continuous segments are resized to 120 frames; 90% motion-aware masking, 400 epochs, batch 128. NTU60 cross-subject linear accuracy is 84.9%; evaluation also covers NTU120 and PKU-MMD. The target is finite difference in the resized sequence, not automatically physical velocity. Motion-aware masking and motion prediction are established baselines, not new contributions here. [Method, processing and results](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.07092v1).

**SLiM — inspected preprint v3, 2026-08-03; first posted 2026-03-11.** Decoder-free prototype CE/EMA and global-local contrastive learning use a 64-frame, 8×25-token grid, token-index RoPE, 150 epochs/batch 768. NTU60 linear accuracy is 87.9/93.2% cross-subject/view; 7.89× concerns downstream encoder GFLOPs, not total training or latency. Appendix E shows direct feature-MSE branch degeneration despite useful shared-encoder features. Declared missingness beats unflagged filling. High-frequency timing, 2D pose-estimator transfer, and multiple pretraining seeds remain limitations. [Method, controlled ablations and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.10648v3), [official model card](https://huggingface.co/JeonghyeokDo/SLiM).

### Identity-oriented versus clinical gait learning

**GaitSSB — IEEE TPAMI 45(12), 2023; inspected arXiv v2, 2023-09-04.** A silhouette CNN/part-pooling Siamese encoder uses contrastive matching, predictor and stop-gradient, without a momentum encoder. Pretraining uses 16-frame clips from 1.02M unlabeled walks; transfer uses 30 frames and supervised identity objectives. CASIA-B, OU-MVLP, GREW and Gait3D use identity-oriented gallery/probe protocols. The evidence supports identity transfer, not a clinical forecasting endpoint. Silhouette normalization and view-invariant augmentations address a different target. [Method and evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.13964v2), [journal citation](https://github.com/ShiqiYu/OpenGait/blob/master/datasets/GaitLU-1M/README.md).

**On Model and Data Scaling for Skeleton-based Self-Supervised Gait Recognition — AAAI 2026, published 2026-03-14; inspected arXiv v1, 2025-04-10.** GaitPTv2/GaitFormer use SimCLR plus .01 KoLeo, not an EMA teacher. Private pretraining covers 2.7M skeleton sequences; fixed-epoch comparisons use 25 epochs, batch 256. Cropping, pace changes, mirroring and smoothing are identity augmentations. Zero-shot identity retrieval is evaluated on CASIA-B, PsyMo, GREW and Gait3D. Scaling behavior does not establish a clinical scaling law or independent participant count. [Method and protocols](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.07598v1), [AAAI record](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/37340).

**GaitForeMer — MICCAI 2022; arXiv v1, 2022-06-30.** Transformer forecasting uses layerwise L1 coordinate prediction; **NTU pretraining also uses supervised activity cross-entropy**, jointly for 100 epochs. Clinical video-derived VIBE skeletons form 100-frame clips; 54 participants are assessed using subject-level leave-one-out validation. Macro-F1 is .76 versus .60 without pretraining. There is no EMA teacher. These results support mixed-supervision transfer, not isolated superiority of pure SSL or latent forecasting. Preserve the distinction despite “Self-Supervised” in the title. [§2.1, §3 and §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.00106v1).

**Self-Supervised Learning of Gait-Based Biomarkers — MICCAI Ambient Intelligence workshop 2023; arXiv v1, 2023-07-30.** A causal GPT-like transformer uses same-trial contrastive crops and optional next-step MSE, without EMA; 90 frames/3 seconds, 1,000 epochs, batch 32. Multiview markerless joint-angle data cover 75 people/712 trials. Diagnosis/laterality use subject-held-out threefold evaluation with inner readout tuning. Lower SSL loss did not predict downstream performance; FCM/noise often worsened it. The longitudinal 11-person illustration includes training subjects and is not an independent response validation. [Methods, results and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.16321v1).

### Equivariance and newer boundary evidence

**seq-JEPA — NeurIPS 2025; inspected arXiv v3, 2026-01-08; first posted 2025-05-06.** An image encoder, sequence aggregator and action-conditioned predictor maximize cosine agreement with a stop-gradient EMA next-view target. Actions are known transformations/crop displacements. Training/evaluation use rendered 3DIEBench and image datasets, including variable observation counts, not continuous gait time. Action conditioning benefits in-distribution equivariance; the disjoint-rotation test fails rotation decoding even when classification degrades less. Equivariance is not guaranteed extrapolation or intervention modeling. [Method and OOD Appendix B.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.03176v3), [official project/venue](https://hafezgh.github.io/seq-jepa/).

**Latent Video Prediction Learns Better World Models — inspected preprint v1, 2026-05-15.** A comparison of released video encoders examines corruption, occlusion, recognition, and temporal direction. Its design distinguishes feature stability from useful predictive discrimination, and explicitly does not equalize historical pretraining data/compute. This is evaluation evidence, not a new clinical training recipe or proof that objective choice causes every model difference. [Primary comparison](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15618v1).

**From Pixels to Newtons — inspected preprint v1, 2026-06-04.** Supervised force regression combines mesh kinematics with frozen V-JEPA 2 features. The 26-patient study reports LOSO assessment; its modality ablations instead use an 85/15 patient-stratified validation split. Video-without-text reduces validation nRMSE from 16.3% to 12.9% versus kinematics+shape. Velocities use elapsed time; acceleration did not improve validation error. It supports testing a video reference, not forecasting or therapeutic efficacy: preprocessing is retrospective, and external evaluation disables the unavailable video pathway. [Methods and modality ablation](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06631v1).

Additional update leads were screened, not promoted to implementation prerequisites: [GaitEncoder](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.07.26357479v1.full), July 2026, is a weakly supervised kinematic VAE, with full-text retrieval incomplete in this review; [Capturing Uncertainty in Human Motion for Representation Learning in Soccer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11203), August 2026, is a future-motion preprint outside clinical gait, inspected at abstract level only. A [2026 ACM healthcare SSL study](https://ghasemzadeh.com/publication/2026-lift-pd/) uses a waist accelerometer, and a [2026 IEEE Access masked-gait study](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3697731) uses ground-reaction-force signals; these do not establish video transfer. This screening avoids conflating modalities or treating search hits as verified methods.

## Implementation consequences and falsification tests

The following are original study-design decisions inferred from the evidence above. They are not guarantees of improvement and do not replace the exact endpoint/split contract in the experiment specification.

### E0: measurement, provenance and independent units first

- Retain original timestamps and observed/imputed/invalid distinctions. A fixed grid is a query system, not evidence of new observations. Confidence must remain an input-quality signal, not a claimed probability of anatomical accuracy.
- Separate structural token padding, declared observation missingness, and deliberate SSL masking. Do not silently present a filled joint as an observed joint. Test all-invalid tokens and examples explicitly.
- Split connected known-person/duplicate/source groups before window sampling, pretraining and target fitting. Windows, mirrors and optimization seeds do not increase the number of independent clinical subjects.
- Preserve the distinction between source-video grouping and verified participant grouping in every report. Labelblind SSL eligibility must not inherit clinical-label selection.

### E1: isolate information support before increasing model complexity

- Compare physical-time support against historical normalized-time support on identical underlying windows, with the same coordinates, masks, four-sample patch, model capacity and optimization exposure. Clock encoding and two-sample patches are subsequent separate contrasts.
- Retain the published-inspired centered CE arm as the anchor. L1/Huber regression, VICReg changes, and extra motion losses are separately named adaptations; do not bundle them and attribute a gain to time fidelity.
- Log effective rank/variance separately for encoder features, predicted masked features, targets and projected prototype logits where relevant. A useful shared representation can coexist with a degenerate prediction branch.
- Use directly computed prefix observables and random/supervised controls to localize loss of information. A lower proxy-recovery error is evidence about that proxy, not affected side or disease severity.

### E2: define prediction by the information boundary

- The implemented prefix is `[b−2.56,b)` sampled at 25 Hz. Preprocessing, root/body scale, motion masks, velocity fitting and every predictor covariate must depend only on observations strictly before `b`.
- The primary outcome remains original-observation lower-limb position at the requested **0.50 s** horizon, with the contract's nearest-observation tolerance, validity and bilateral-pair rule. The two-query future latent interval is a distinct training target, not permission to average away the primary endpoint.
- Future observation timestamps, target availability and interpolation decisions must not shape context inputs or predictor queries. Mutate future content and move future timestamps across the boundary in tests; the latter must trigger reclassification/rejection, not silent leakage.
- Persistence, constant velocity, prefix-fitted periodic/AR prediction, direct supervised pose forecasting and latent methods must share eligible examples, target support, body scale and training-source endpoints. Report the actual strongest baseline selected on development, not whichever yields the largest reported skill.
- Read out both the frozen context and the **predicted** future latent. Context decodability alone cannot validate the JEPA predictor. Exclude the future teacher from inference. Use mismatched-future controls without changing marginal exposure or source-group rules.
- The implemented `future_wrong_time` arm is precisely **wrong_horizon_order**: encode each window's true future intervals independently, circularly permute the complete target embeddings and their validity by one horizon, and leave predictor queries unchanged. With horizons `[.25,.50,1.00]`, queries receive target indices `[2,0,1]`. This preserves each example's target multiset and usable-target count even in short bouts. It is not a disjoint-future-window or shuffled-continuous-time experiment. `future_wrong_source` instead samples a different connected group, using source→bout→window draws; its support differences must be reported.
- Lower point error may reflect conditional averaging. Do not claim multimodal uncertainty, causal intervention, gait-event validity or clinical utility unless those additional outcomes are implemented and independently evaluated.

### Later gated experiments, not first-run requirements

For dense/deep supervision, run the complete base/dense-only/deep-only/both comparison under fixed updates and data. Normalize masked and visible losses independently; predefine joint/time distance and its scaling for context weighting. Track both local dynamics and global utility. Do not import a 2B-parameter video recipe as a small-cohort skeleton optimum.

For a frozen RGB control, record checkpoint checksum, code revision, original pretraining regime, native sampling, extraction support, spatial crop and readout budget. Sixteen frames at 4 FPS span 3.75 seconds first-to-last and cannot fit unchanged inside a 2.56-second prefix. Either resample the allowed prefix, explicitly pad past frames, or preregister a longer-prefix comparison; never borrow future frames. Treat unknown internet-pretraining overlap as unresolved contamination risk. Repeated/still, shuffled, person-only and background controls test where utility comes from; they do not fully identify the effect of the pretraining objective.

SLiM's compact tokens and global/local scale invariance are optional compute comparators after preservation is measured. Eight-frame patches and pace-changing identity augmentations can erase the very timing under study. Similarly, reflection tests must specify anatomical relabeling, image-coordinate reflection, and target sign separately. A lower equivariance residual with weaker signed decoding is a negative result.

## Reporting gate

Release claims require saved configuration, split and observation receipts, checkpoint hashes, per-window predictions, source-video aggregation and clustered uncertainty. Report all failed/null arms and distinguish pretraining-seed, readout-seed, corruption-seed and source-sampling variation. Development comparisons may guide choices but do not become untouched final-test evidence. The strongest defensible contribution remains a controlled explanation of information preservation under noisy temporal observation, followed by a remedy only if the held-out evidence supports it.
