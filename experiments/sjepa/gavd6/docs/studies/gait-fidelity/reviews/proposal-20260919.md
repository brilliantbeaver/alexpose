# Independent adversarial review and disposition

19 September 2026. Three independent agents investigated different parts of the problem, wrote their own evidence audits, then challenged the integrated proposal. The root revised the synthesis and the reviewers reread it. This is review of a prospective research plan, not peer review of completed scientific results.

## Scope of the independent work

- The clinical-data reviewer investigated practical laterality applications, pathological gait sources, access and licensing, reference modalities, and clinical interpretation.
- The method reviewer searched close prior art, inspected predecessor experiments, ranked competing hypotheses, and challenged whether the proposed learning objective adds anything beyond direct task supervision.
- The measurement reviewer defined intervention-response estimands, ambiguity limits, event/reference requirements, statistical units, uncertainty, and failure policies.

All reviews used the same dimensions: conference relevance/contribution (20%), claim accuracy/evidence (20%), evaluation/statistics (15%), scientific insight/prior work (15%), reproducibility (10%), clarity (10%), figures (5%), and submission fit (5%). Two reviewers supplied optional plan-only scores, 75.5 and 87/100; the third declined a numerical paper-quality score without experiments. These judgments are not averaged or interpreted as acceptance probabilities. The substantive findings below determine revisions.

## Changes made in response

| Concern | Disposition in the final synthesis |
| --- | --- |
| Synthetic clinical gait learning and split-belt asymmetry evaluation are already published or described in close preprints | Yamada 2025, Stenum 2024 and DeepGaitLab 2026 are explicit nearest precedents; the proposed contribution is a narrower response-preservation mechanism and useful evidence boundary |
| A pair loss could merely rename ordinary measurement supervision | Added direct per-example measurement supervision, equal target exposures/batch structure, matched-difference shuffled pairs, and direct quantity prediction; full constraint-by-objective interaction additionally retains unconstrained JEPA on the same new data |
| Failure during total occlusion may be unavoidable rather than a learned defect | Separate recoverable measurements from controlled input-equivalent ambiguity; score distributions/abstention or added information in the latter case |
| A side anchor does not reveal hidden coordinates | Anatomical assignment and hidden-motion uncertainty are separated explicitly |
| Mirrored motions alone do not test biological severity or graded change | Restrict mirror pairs to sign/transformation controls; require an independently measured or reference-verified graded change |
| The actionable public clinical sources were buried in a supporting audit | Added explicit LIVE-GaitNeuroKids and stroke-mocap rows, first-day feasibility checks, and a declared engineering-only fallback |
| Synthetic naming errors could repeat historical shortcut failures | Require sequence-level paths before windowing, consistent confidence/validity/metadata permutation, independent missingness, leakage probes, and continuity headroom |
| Missing detected events could create deceptively good step-time results | Define signed step time, initial contact, pairing, reference eligibility, failures and coverage before prediction; retain missed/extra events |
| Oracle contacts supply the step-time answer | Limit oracle-event coordinate diagnostics and keep them separate from practical timing recovery |
| Rejecting foot sliding could remove genuine pathological drag | QC now rejects retargeting-induced artifacts while preserving reference-observed drag |
| Canonicalized gait representations might only need their retained transformation metadata | Add canonical features plus inverse side/phase mapping as a baseline |
| A supervised scalar gain may not generalize | Require an untrained outcome/intervention, whole-trajectory guardrails, credible refiners and independent real-reference evidence for a strong claim |
| Existing adapted baselines might be mistaken for official reproductions | Require compatible recent refiners for broad comparisons and label bounded adaptations |

The reviewers reported no remaining critical wording issue after the substantive changes. Their last narrow corrections concerning oracle event timing and foot drag were applied exactly as recommended. This does not establish that the proposed datasets import correctly, that the new objective works, or that clinical validity is demonstrated.

## What remains unresolved

Natural laterality-error prevalence, access and usable clinical references, video-to-sensor synchronization, faithful motion retargeting, precise model/loss implementation, realistic compute cost, new-endpoint variance and sample size, repeated-seed results, and incremental JEPA value remain unmeasured. The proposed synchronized raw-video viewer is not implemented. The existing linked gallery contains only previously retained diagnostic figures.

The final delivery contains documentation and source-grounded hypotheses. It neither launches experiments nor changes previous results or abstract drafts. A statistically significant result, substantial practical improvement, novelty priority, and conference acceptance cannot be inferred from this review.
