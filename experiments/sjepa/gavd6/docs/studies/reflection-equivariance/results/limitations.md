# Limits of the fixed-reflection study

Fixed chiral equivariance for human pose is direct prior art. Chirality Nets
already use coordinate reflection plus left/right label exchange, shared
parameters, odd/even symmetry, and pose-sequence tasks. Skeleton JEPA and
invariant/equivariant latent representations are likewise established method
families.

Accordingly, this study may support conclusions such as:

- a repaired known-reflection constraint helps or does not help this transfer
  task;
- output repair or unconstrained fusion is sufficient; or
- a purported gain was explained by missingness, static pose, or leakage.

It may not support a claim of a new general representation-learning mechanism.
Its scientific role is to be the toughest fixed-symmetry baseline for Latent
Laterality.
