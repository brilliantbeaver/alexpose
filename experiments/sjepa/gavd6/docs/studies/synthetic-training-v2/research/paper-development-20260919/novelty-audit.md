# Contribution and method audit

19 September 2026. This is a research plan based on the completed seed-17 development run, source inspection, and the primary literature linked below. Proposed experiments are not completed results. This audit changes no training code or retained experiment artifacts.

## Recommended paper direction

The strongest feasible direction is a controlled study of **when paired feature prediction helps restore measured movement**, using synthetic data to isolate error sources and independent real video with motion-capture references to test transfer. The existing results motivate that question but do not answer it generally. Direct supervision improves position and displacement beyond simple calibration, paired JEPA nearly matches random-feature and shuffled-pairing controls, and several methods retain distorted ankle trajectories. A larger repetition of the same treadmill panel would increase precision without resolving the narrow motion distribution, reference conventions, detector inputs, or lack of real evaluation.

The paper should distinguish three possible outcomes before running the confirmation experiment. A repeatable latent-learning advantage beyond a matched direct coordinate-and-motion learner would support a method contribution. A narrow, well-measured boundary on latent-learning benefit could support an empirical contribution if it is reproduced with credible recipes and independent data. An unresolved confidence interval would remain inconclusive; it should not become a negative claim about JEPA.

### Ranked directions

| Rank | Direction | What would make it substantive | Main risk and scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | An audited comparison of feature prediction and direct supervision for motion restoration | Establish which gains arise from spatial calibration, which require temporal information, and which transfer to new people, a held-out estimator, and real videos. Release aligned input/reference/prediction inspection tools and person-level evidence. | Smoothness versus position accuracy is already a known issue. Novelty must come from the controlled representation-learning question and new external evidence, not that observation alone. |
| 2 | Paired feature prediction with explicit motion targets | Show that a precisely specified change to the pretraining targets improves motion information and final restoration beyond equally supervised coordinate-and-motion controls, at comparable tuning effort and compute. | Predicting motion and adding velocity/acceleration losses are established ideas. Any contribution lies in the demonstrated causal comparison and useful transfer, not a new name for a finite difference. |
| 3 | Uncertainty-aware restoration during long occlusion | Return uncertainty when the visible sequence admits several plausible hidden motions, and demonstrate useful error/coverage calibration on real references. | This adds a different modeling and evaluation problem, and is unlikely to be the best one-week route. A deterministic point estimator remains the stronger near-term baseline. |

Rank 1 is the minimum credible paper program. Rank 2 is a bounded extension inside it, contingent on a successful diagnostic. Rank 3 is a later project unless suitable uncertainty code, annotations, and expertise already exist.

## Source-level findings that matter before adding complexity

The [complete seed-17 analysis](../../results/seed17-complete-analysis-20260919/README.md) reports finished fits and no obvious constant-feature collapse. This removes an easy explanation for the weak paired effect. Several other explanations remain testable, and none is yet an established mechanism.

1. **The deployed feature extractor is the online encoder.** `RestorationModel.forward` uses `model.encoder`; the momentum `model.teacher` supplies pretraining targets. The original S-JEPA paper uses its target encoder downstream. Fit identical coordinate probes to the retained online and EMA encoders, using only training labels and development selection. This is a recipe diagnostic, not a new algorithm or a declaration that current code is incorrect.
2. **Observed coordinates bypass the representation.** The final output adds the learned correction to the observed coordinates. A nonlinear coordinate head can learn simple calibration from random features while much of the input trajectory passes through unchanged. Measure what the representation supplies beyond that bypass, rather than inferring useful temporal learning from a lower coordinate loss.
3. **The current pairing control is weak for repetitive treadmill motion.** Shuffled donors are different windows within the same person/condition/extractor strata. They can preserve similar gait content. Add a phase-disrupted same-sequence donor and a different-sequence donor matched on broad motion and scale; retain both so that changing target difficulty does not masquerade as a pairing result.
4. **A static control is not yet a clean temporal ablation.** It retains whole-window auxiliary information and differs in architecture. Add an attention restriction that prevents access to neighboring coordinate tokens while holding tokenization, auxiliary inputs, readout, and normalization fixed. Since a four-frame token already contains temporal information, call this a cross-patch control. A strict frame-only comparison requires one-frame tokens in both arms or a carefully matched framewise architecture.
5. **Feature rank is not a motion test.** Train small frozen probes for reference displacement, signed ankle separation, and position on the same training people. Report random, coordinate-pretrained, online-JEPA, and EMA-JEPA features with both a linear head and the existing nonlinear head. Do not provide the probes a raw-coordinate residual skip. Destroy neighboring coordinate order while preserving the target-frame coordinates and the original normalization to test dependence on temporal input. This perturbation is diagnostic, not a substitute for an in-distribution controlled training arm.
6. **Direct supervision currently receives more coordinate optimization.** Direct uses 4,000 coordinate updates; the pretraining arms have 2,000 pretraining and 2,000 readout updates. Provide both matched downstream-coordinate budgets and matched measured-total-compute comparisons. Use a matched end-to-end fine-tuning comparison only as a separate question; it changes the interpretation of frozen-feature quality.
7. **Rendering and evaluation choices need external checks.** Input-only normalization prevents reference leakage into the model, but the current metrics also use reference-box normalization; framewise scale changes affect operational peak counts. Synthetic foreground boxes are more informative than detector-generated boxes. A real-video claim therefore needs synchronized image/reference inspection, an audited joint mapping, and a deployment-like detector/tracker crop condition.

Source files inspected: [models.py](../../../../../src/gavd6_sjepa/research_directions/synthetic_training_v2/models.py), [training.py](../../../../../src/gavd6_sjepa/research_directions/synthetic_training_v2/training.py), and [data.py](../../../../../src/gavd6_sjepa/research_directions/synthetic_training_v2/data.py). In particular, the current mask hides observed patch/joint tokens, teacher inputs for paired learning use valid synthetic reference coordinates, and the final coordinate stage freezes the encoder. Privileged references must remain confined to fitting and evaluation.

## A bounded representation-learning intervention

The hypothesis is that feature prediction may preserve easily predicted body layout while failing to retain the smaller, time-specific changes required for motion restoration. The present evidence is compatible with this hypothesis but does not establish it. Test the probes above before interpreting this as the reason JEPA underperforms.

A practical intervention is to add a separate target stream encoding **reference displacement at fixed physical time intervals**, alongside the current reference-position features. Displacement is the reference joint position at a later time minus its position now. Use a small preregistered set such as 0.04, 0.20, and 0.40 seconds on a 25 Hz grid; timestamps and target validity must determine support. Use one input-derived scale per window rather than normalizing each displacement sequence to unit variance, which would discard the amplitude information being tested. A momentum encoder can produce the displacement targets, and a separate predictor can estimate them from the same partially observed input. Neither reference displacements nor reference support enter inference.

Start with the target change alone. The existing mask already uses time blocks, so characterize its realized durations before describing a contiguous-gap mask as new. Only add a second masking experiment if probes show that visible neighboring samples make the target trivial. If used, evaluate missing intervals at fixed durations and use the same intervals for coordinate-pretraining controls. Artificial hiding and true estimator missingness remain separate recorded variables.

This intervention has scientific value only if it answers the target-design question. Mandatory controls are an equally sized coordinate-and-displacement prediction arm, a direct supervised model with the same reference motion losses, a shuffled-displacement target, and the original position-feature arm. MAMP and motion-aware pose refiners already motivate the generic ingredients. Do not present the composite loss itself as established novelty, and do not interpret a gain over the weak original JEPA arm as sufficient.

### Compact matrix

| Stage | Methods | Data and repetitions | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recipe check | Random features; coordinate pretraining; current JEPA online; current JEPA EMA | Retained training split; audited development split; matched linear/nonlinear readouts | Identify whether feature choice, readout, or coordinate bypass changes the interpretation. |
| Development screen | Direct position; direct position+motion; original paired JEPA; motion-target paired JEPA; shuffled motion-target JEPA | Broader training movements; the same development people and corruptions; one declared seed and identical selection allowance | Select at most one candidate and one strong direct comparator. A failed screen is reported and closes the method branch. |
| Frozen confirmation | Unchanged; affine calibration; development-selected filter; verified SmoothNet; one feasible recent refiner; direct comparator; selected paired candidate; matched random/shuffled controls | Locked unseen people and an external real-reference set; initially 3, preferably 5 predeclared training seeds if affordable | Estimate the prespecified primary contrast and preserve all runs, including failures. |
| Mechanism and stress tests | Selected candidate and strongest direct/filter controls | Held estimator, controlled occlusion duration, camera/crop changes, and untouched corruption combinations | Establish the conditions in which a gain persists or fails. Keep these secondary to the declared primary contrast. |

“Verified SmoothNet” means checking the author implementation, adapting the input/output schema explicitly, and giving it comparable development tuning. A SynSP or PS-Mamba adaptation should likewise be labeled as adapted when the published dimensionality, joint set, objective, or recipe changes. Using released weights with an incompatible joint schema is not a fair shortcut. Prefer one carefully reproduced recent baseline over several unreliable ports; an unavailable recent baseline remains a limitation, not a license to claim state of the art.

## Primary comparison and a defensible success rule

For a **method paper**, lock the candidate before external evaluation and compare it primarily against the direct coordinate-and-motion model on person-balanced displacement error over 0.20 seconds in the independent real-reference set. This tests whether latent prediction adds value beyond receiving the same motion supervision. Require a position-accuracy guardrail with a practical degradation margin chosen from development data and reference uncertainty, before observing test results. Report effect sizes and intervals over people and training runs, not the number of rendered tracks or frames. The parent statistical plan should set the sample size and family of confirmatory tests.

Amplitude, event precision/recall, left/right geometry, and missing-joint coverage remain prespecified secondary outcomes. Good displacement error is insufficient evidence that all movement is preserved. Operational ankle-separation peaks should remain operational peaks until validated against independently annotated gait events.

For an **empirical paper**, replace an assumed method win with a declared question about the practically meaningful incremental benefit of paired pretraining over random features and direct supervision. A confidence interval that includes zero does not establish equivalence. Define a useful effect margin before confirmation and collect enough independent people to distinguish small effects from inconclusive estimates. The scientific contribution would be a reproducible account of when and why the objectives differ, supported by the intervention/probe results, rather than a broad statement that JEPA fails.

## Minimum successful paper versus a longer program

Within a week, prioritize a reviewed reference adapter and new held people, a real-reference feasibility test, completed fixed-seed comparisons, the online/EMA diagnostic, and trustworthy baselines. The existing eight-person panel has already influenced decisions and belongs to development. More windows from those people and more training seeds cannot turn it into an untouched test set.

MoVi is a promising external candidate under investigation by the separate dataset audit. Excluding its AMASS counterpart BMLmovi prevents direct reuse of those recordings but does not establish that its people never appear in another BioMotionLab collection. No verified cross-study identity map has been established here for MoVi versus BioMotionLab_NTroje/BMLrub. For the strongest fresh experiment, exclude all potentially overlapping BioMotionLab collections from synthetic training unless a reliable identity map establishes separation, and retain the current BML-trained experiment as development. Track original people and recordings across representations; corpus names alone do not prove independence. Verify access to the particular synchronized video, calibration, and joint/reference subset before promising its advertised participant count as the eligible test population. Human3.6M can supply an established protocol check, but its standard small held-person population does not solve subject-level power. GAVD can support qualitative or application-specific work; it cannot establish continuous coordinate or gait-timing accuracy without appropriate independent reference annotations.

A minimally convincing empirical paper needs replication on new people, a credible real-reference result, fair representation controls, and an explanation that survives normalization, visibility, and crop checks. A successful new JEPA method additionally needs a useful advantage over direct motion supervision and current refiners, with ablations linking that gain to the claimed mechanism. A larger uncertainty-aware model, multiple external benchmarks, controlled clinical laterality, and a full standardized motion-preservation benchmark are a longer program.

If independent real-reference data cannot be prepared and reviewed in time, a carefully bounded synthetic study may still be worth writing, but the current evidence does not justify promising an ICLR-level contribution or a statistically significant JEPA improvement. A strong data and evaluation artifact helps the paper only when it enables a clear, repeatable scientific finding.
