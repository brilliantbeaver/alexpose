# Developing the synthetic pose study into a substantive paper

19 September 2026. This plan uses the completed 2,000-update-per-phase, seed-17 results. It supersedes the earlier two-person planning assumptions while preserving the previous study, its records and its scientific decisions. Proposed experiments below have not been run. Dataset access and available HAIC time remain to be confirmed.

**Recommendation.** Build a controlled study of whether paired feature prediction supplies useful movement information beyond direct coordinate-and-motion supervision, and test its conclusions on independently referenced real video. Use the current AMASS experiment to diagnose the method, then select at most one revised candidate before a fresh evaluation. The most promising real-data route is BioMotionLab MoVi, subject to access, reference checks and a conservative training-overlap audit. A larger version of the same eight-person treadmill experiment would leave several central questions unresolved.

This program can produce a positive method result or a useful, bounded empirical result. Statistical significance cannot be guaranteed, and it would not make a negligible improvement novel. The aim is a practically useful finding with adequate uncertainty and an explanation that survives strong controls. A repeatable null for this small local recipe, together with corrections to its evaluation protocol, would still be insufficient by itself. The empirical route needs a useful new boundary or explanation, such as a controlled target change that alters the motion information accessible in features and predicts downstream behavior across independent data and credible alternative recipes. Otherwise the appropriate outcome may be a preliminary study or a longer project.

Open the [data-review page](data-review.html) to inspect the retained trajectories and fitting histories now. The detailed supporting investigations are the [dataset audit](dataset-audit.md), [contribution audit](novelty-audit.md), and [statistical design](stats-audit.md).

**What the latest experiment establishes and what it leaves open**

The [complete analysis](../../results/seed17-complete-analysis-20260919/README.md) supports a concrete starting point. Direct training reduces visible-position error by 7.15–8.51% and 0.20-second displacement error by 6.17–8.31% relative to the affine calibration control across the three extractors. A five-frame filter performs better on displacement for HRNet overall and for all three extractors under combined blur and obstruction. Paired JEPA nearly matches initialized-feature and shuffled-target controls. Valid synthetic references that include hidden joints reveal exaggerated ankle-separation variation and extra predicted peaks even when coordinate errors improve.

These are development findings from eight people, each represented by normal treadmill walking from one corpus, with one neural training seed. The 384 track records repeat those people under windows, renderings and estimators. Current coverage failures were caused by a reference-visibility rule; valid motion coordinates were still available. The data source should therefore not be dismissed as intrinsically poor, but its present selection and measurement protocol are too narrow for broad conclusions.

Recent prior work makes the contribution question demanding. [SmoothNet](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136650615.pdf) studies temporal refinement, [SynSP](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Wang_SynSP_Synergy_of_Smoothness_and_Precision_in_Pose_Sequences_Refinement_CVPR_2024_paper.html) explicitly addresses precision and smoothness, and [PS-Mamba](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Dong_PS-Mamba_Spatial-Temporal_Graph_Mamba_for_Pose_Sequence_Refinement_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf) is a newer refinement model. Showing that a position score and a motion score differ is insufficient by itself. The stronger contribution would explain what paired learned features add, when they fail, and whether that conclusion survives independent references and real imagery.

**A focused contribution with falsifiable questions**

A suitable working paper question is: *When does predicting reference features improve pose restoration beyond learning coordinates and motion directly?* This stays close to the current abstract's scope while making the comparison more informative.

| Question | Experiment that could answer it | Result that would limit the claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the learned representation contain motion information beyond an untrained network? | Fit identical small probes to initialized, coordinate-pretrained, online-JEPA and momentum-teacher features. Predict positions and 0.20-second displacements without a raw-coordinate shortcut. | Comparable probes would leave feature-learning utility unestablished even if a larger output network restores positions. |
| Is the target representation emphasizing body layout more than time-specific movement? | Compare the current position-feature objective with a single additional reference-displacement feature stream, plus equally supervised direct and coordinate-target controls. | A direct coordinate-and-motion model matching the gain would show that extra motion supervision, rather than feature prediction, is sufficient. |
| Does the practical benefit survive new people and real image errors? | Freeze methods, inputs and metrics; evaluate fresh people using a fixed extractor family and synchronized real-video references. | A gain confined to synthetic body-model references or oracle person crops supports a narrower conclusion. |
| Does a motion-preservation claim survive how references are scored? | Retain fixed-scale coordinate/displacement measurements, visibility/support counts, and separately named anatomical-reference sensitivity analyses. | A finding that disappears under a credible reference convention or depends on a changing scored population needs explanation before a preservation claim. |

These questions are proposed tests, not established explanations of the existing result. Synthetic experiments provide control over corruption and viewpoint; the real experiment checks whether the resulting explanation is useful outside the renderer.

**First diagnose the current recipe with inexpensive comparisons**

Recover the original source bundle, saved model checkpoints and raw predictions from HAIC before adding complexity. The local diagnostic download contains detailed tables and calibration predictions, but lacks the original neural arrays and weights. Its recorded HAIC verification and locally reconstructed aggregates remain useful; they do not make those missing assets available locally.

The source inspection identifies several specific tests:

1. Compare frozen features from the online encoder with those from its momentum teacher, the gradually updated network that supplied training targets. The [original S-JEPA paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/04755.pdf) uses the target encoder downstream; this local restoration adaptation uses the online encoder. This is a recipe comparison, not a newly invented method or proof of a coding defect. It requires retained teacher weights or a new development fit.
2. Fit both a linear probe and the current nonlinear output network to each representation. For this diagnostic, remove the direct addition of input coordinates to outputs. The ordinary restoration path can keep that useful residual connection; the probe asks what information the learned features themselves supply.
3. Use stronger pairing controls. Current shuffled targets come from another window of the same person's repeated treadmill movement. Add a declared phase shift and a different recording matched on broad action, with the same training budget. Preserve the original shuffled control. A harder donor can change task difficulty, so it does not isolate correspondence on its own.
4. Measure dependence on temporal input. A parameter-matched attention restriction can isolate access across patches. Current patches contain four frames, so call that a cross-patch control; a truly frame-only control requires one-frame tokenization in both arms. Keep normalization and auxiliary inputs matched.
5. Compare both equal downstream-coordinate updates and measured total compute. Direct receives 4,000 coordinate updates; the current feature methods receive 2,000 pretraining and 2,000 coordinate-output updates. Equal total steps do not equalize the supervised objective or total computation.

Complete these probes on development data. If matched probes recover reference motion more accurately from learned features than from random features, but the restoration head does not improve, test the output stage under the same feature access and coordinate budget. Probe success identifies accessible information; it does not by itself identify why restoration failed. If the teacher or representation has no useful advantage in these tests, more identical training runs will measure that limitation more precisely.

**One bounded candidate intervention**

If the probes indicate weak motion information, test a paired JEPA with separate targets for body position and reference displacement. Displacement is the vector from a joint's position now to its position after a specified interval. Candidate intervals such as 0.04, 0.20 and 0.40 seconds are design choices to freeze on development data, not new observations. Predict learned numerical features of those displacements from the same partially observed input. Use one input-derived scale per window; normalizing each displacement series to unit variance would remove the amplitude information being tested. Reference coordinates and reference-validity masks remain training/scoring information and never become inference inputs.

The novelty claim must concern the supported effect of this target choice. [MAMP](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Mao_Masked_Motion_Predictors_are_Strong_3D_Action_Representation_Learners_ICCV_2023_paper.html), [PoseBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.10211), [MotionBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06551) and temporal refiners already use related motion or masked-recovery ideas. Adding a velocity loss or hiding more frames would not establish novelty.

The decisive comparator is a direct model given the **same position and displacement supervision**, tuning allowance and input data. Retain a shuffled-displacement target, the original paired objective, and initialized-feature controls. Begin with the target change alone. Add a masking change only if a specific diagnostic justifies it, and give coordinate-learning controls the same mask. Select at most one final candidate; preserve all unsuccessful development variants.

This candidate is not yet an implementation-ready method. Before screening, specify how the displacement teacher is updated, how frame-level targets align with four-frame tokens, how boundaries and missing references are masked, and how added capacity and compute are matched in controls. At 25 Hz, a 0.20-second displacement spans five frames; it cannot silently become a one-token difference. These decisions must be resolved before the candidate enters the frozen comparison.

**Prevent leakage before scaling up**

AMASS includes BMLmovi, the body-model version of MoVi. Training on those motions and testing their real videos would not be an independent external-motion test. In addition, no verified identity crosswalk was found between MoVi and the BioMotionLab_NTroje/BMLrub collections. For the strongest fresh experiment, exclude all potentially overlapping BioMotionLab collections from the new synthetic training roster unless a reliable crosswalk establishes separation. CMU, KIT and ACCAD are candidate alternative sources, subject to their own identity and duplicate checks. Dataset names alone do not prove that people never recur. [AMASS project and constituent data](https://amass.is.tue.mpg.de/), [MoVi release in AMASS](https://amass.is.tue.mpg.de/news.html).

Create a single manifest with corpus, original person, canonical person, recording, timestamp range, source hash, derived-artifact hash, split and exposure history. Split by person before rendering or window extraction. Put alternate cameras, repeated actions, augmentations and duplicate/near-duplicate recordings in the same group. Check checkpoint training provenance for each frozen pose estimator and learned motion prior; an estimator-family name does not establish that its weights never encountered the test data. Record unknown exposure as unknown.

Every final learned restorer and fitted calibration control must use the same fresh training roster. Models fitted on BioMotionLab_NTroje remain development diagnostics unless overlap with MoVi is resolved; comparing a newly trained candidate against controls retaining potentially overlapping training data would undermine the design. Preparing and auditing the replacement non-BML motion roster, rendering it and extracting tracks are on the critical path.

An illustrative MoVi allocation is 20–30 eligible people for integration/development and up to 60 untouched people for the final evaluation. This is a proposed split, not an official protocol or a guaranteed available sample. Separate the technical reference audit from method inspection: a reviewer can check test timestamps and annotations under fixed rules without seeing model results. If overlap cannot be resolved, describe a dataset/recording holdout and retain that limitation rather than claiming proven unseen people.

**A data-quality process that can detect the relevant failures**

High quality here means that the images, identities, joint definitions and times support the intended claim. A famous dataset does not supply that assurance automatically.

| Check before large-scale fitting | What to inspect or verify | Handling a failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and duplication | Person/recording aliases, original splits, exact motion hashes and flagged near-duplicates. | Group linked records together or exclude ambiguity under a documented rule before outcome inspection. |
| Temporal alignment | Whole-clip RGB/reference playback, time units, dropped frames and rapid changes in direction. MoVi has 30 Hz video and 120 Hz mocap; its action-boundary flags use both rates. | Correct an evidenced indexing/calibration error and version the adapter. Do not optimize a separate time shift for each test model or clip. |
| Camera and image coordinates | Reference skeleton on full RGB frames and actual estimator crops; camera transform, lens distortion, image orientation, units and crop inversion. | Stop the large import until round-trip checks and blinded overlays pass. |
| Joint meaning | Explicit shared-joint map and left/right labels. Compare Visual3D joint centers with MoSh++ body-model joints as different conventions. | Freeze the convention on development data. Do not refit a mapping on test outcomes to favor a method. |
| Reference support | Distinct flags for valid reference, RGB visibility, source interpolation and finite model output. MoVi's original processing can fill short mocap gaps. | Preserve provenance and give all methods the same reference-based masks. Report missing predictions and unsupported outcomes. |
| Difficult examples | Long occlusions, blur, turns, varying speeds, low reference amplitude and detector failures selected from metadata or reference-only flags. | Keep legitimate difficulty; remove only invalid evidence under preset criteria. |
| Human inspection | For each person/view, at least two metadata-selected clips plus every automatic failure flag. Use a second reviewer for questionable cases and a fixed random audit of accepted clips. | Record reviewer, time, source hash, decision and reason. Fix systematic adapter errors rather than selectively deleting unfavorable examples. |

The review sample is a practical initial proposal, not a guarantee that all errors will be caught. Choose the random-audit fraction and escalation rule before scoring. Distinguish “no issue seen in this clip” from “anatomical ground truth certified.” No outcome-specific data cleaning is allowed.

For the real-video adapter, explicitly choose whether to resample to 25 Hz or operate at native 30 Hz. Preserve physical timestamps and record the interpolation/anti-alias policy. At 30 Hz, 0.20 seconds spans six frames, not five. A 64-frame native-rate input covers a shorter duration than the trained 25 Hz window. This change requires a declared protocol and development check; it is not a harmless array-shape conversion.

Measure projections in the original image coordinates and one fixed evaluation scale per window, alongside raw pixels where comparable. Model normalization must remain input-only. Reference-only scaling is for scoring. Retain native benchmark scores as additional outputs so the study does not create an opaque metric solely for its own method. A valid marker-derived hidden joint may be scored if its reference semantics support that use; hidden image landmarks without an independent reference must remain unscored.

**Design for a useful effect and honest uncertainty**

The statistical audit reconstructed all 60 existing paired person-level contrasts and retained its [script and verification](stats-audit.md). It illustrates why the desired finding cannot simply be “p below 0.05”: paired versus shuffled JEPA changes displacement error by only 0.023–0.055%. Those effects can have moderate standardized values because their variance is also tiny. A small p-value would not make them useful.

Choose one primary confirmation contrast before opening test predictions: the final selected candidate versus the strongest matched direct coordinate-and-motion model on person-balanced 0.20-second displacement error for a prespecified extractor excluded from restoration fitting. ViTPose results have already influenced development interpretation, so its family is not untouched during method selection. Fresh-person evaluation with that fixed family can provide confirmation; a claim of generalization to a previously unexamined family requires another family reserved before development. Pair every method on identical people, recordings, inputs and valid references. Give each person equal influence after the declared within-person averaging. The other extractors, corruption breakdowns and motion lags are supporting outcomes, with a prespecified testing hierarchy or adjustment where confirmatory claims are intended.

Before generating test predictions, record the entire primary population: dataset and round, exact person split, activity, camera, natural versus added corruption, reference convention, joint mapping, temporal resampling and within-person weights. A concrete starting proposal is **MoVi F-round walking, PG2 camera, natural video without added corruption, projected Visual3D reference joints on the shared audited joint set, and one fixed reference scale per window**. Give each person equal weight, average eligible windows within that person, and keep the tested estimator fixed. The first-day import must establish that this proposal is feasible; any revision must occur before test predictions. Lock the 25/30 Hz adapter choice at the same time. Additional actions, PG1, artificial obstruction and projected MoSh++ joints are separately named secondary analyses. Visual3D and MoSh++ define different targets; neither constitutes manual COCO anatomical truth.

A method claim should require useful displacement improvement and a position-accuracy guardrail. An illustrative engineering target is a 5% displacement reduction with no more than 2% position-error degradation, but these values require justification from reference repeatability, absolute error and the intended measurement use before becoming decision thresholds. Designate 5% as either a planning effect or a required demonstrated benefit. The latter requires the corresponding lower confidence bound to exceed 5%; power to reject zero when the true effect is 5% does not provide that guarantee. The position guardrail requires its prespecified upper confidence bound to fall below the tolerated degradation, rather than just an acceptable mean. A confidence interval including zero does not establish practical equivalence; equivalence needs justified bounds and its own test.

| Assumed paired effect, in person-difference standard deviations | Independent people for 80% power | Independent people for 90% power |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.25 | 128 | 171 |
| 0.35 | 67 | 88 |
| 0.50 | 34 | 44 |
| 0.75 | 16 | 21 |

These exact noncentral-t calculations use a two-sided 5% test with independent normal paired differences and condition on fixed trained models. They omit unknown training and real-transfer variance. At 60 people, the corresponding 80%-power sensitivity is about 0.37 paired standard deviations. In a separate illustrative calculation, doubling the pilot's held-ViTPose direct-versus-static displacement SD and assuming a true 5% improvement requires 61 people for 80% power against zero; a 2% effect requires 367. Neither calculation estimates the actual effect of a new JEPA candidate on MoVi. [Planning assumptions and complete calculations](stats-audit.md), [NIST sample-size guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm).

Aim conditionally for 40–60 fresh eligible people and five prespecified training seeds for decisive methods, with a separate integration set and a fixed resource bound. If fewer people are available, state the resulting sensitivity. Seeds estimate training variation on the chosen training population; they do not create additional people or estimate all possible training datasets. Resample person and seed factors independently while preserving the same fitted seed across all its evaluated people. With only three to five seeds, assess coverage sensitivity and also show each seed's effect; a bootstrap cannot create missing training replication. [Crossed-factor resampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.2125).

Keep the old eight-person set as development. Freeze candidate selection, data eligibility, mappings, metrics, primary comparator, margins and sample size before test outcomes. Use a deterministic metadata-based recruitment order and a fixed stopping rule; do not add people, switch datasets or select seeds until a favored significance threshold appears. Changing the test after reading its results creates a new exploratory analysis requiring a new confirmation set.

If an empirical result is an intended fallback, specify its contrast, endpoints and decision rule before opening the fresh test results, with the associated multiplicity plan. Explanations or contrasts first suggested by those results remain exploratory until independently evaluated. The project can change direction on development data without retroactively calling a test-selected story confirmatory.

Report coordinate and displacement effects with intervals, reference coverage, prediction failures, and every seed. For ankle peaks, give precision, recall, extra/missed events and conditional timing error. Nearly zero reference amplitude can make ratios unstable, so set a reference-only eligibility rule in advance. Peaks remain two-dimensional ankle-separation maxima unless independently validated as gait events. Clinical validity and abnormal-gait preservation are outside the current normal-walking evidence.

**A compact experimental matrix**

| Stage | What to run | Evidence needed to proceed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data and recipe check | Existing seed/budget results; online/teacher and initialized/coordinate probes; a few verified real development clips. | Correct reference projection and timing; access to a sufficient prospective test population; measured preparation throughput. |
| Bounded development | Direct position-only; direct position+motion; current paired JEPA; one motion-target candidate; matched shuffled candidate. Reuse the same data and selection budget. | A defined explanation worth testing. Select one candidate and one strong direct comparator. Preserve failures. |
| Frozen main comparison | Unchanged, affine calibration, development-selected filter, verified SmoothNet, one feasible recent refiner, direct comparator, paired candidate and decisive initialized/shuffled controls. | Same input and supervision scope; declared seeds complete; no test-driven tuning. |
| Prespecified stress tests | Held estimator, new people/actions, longer occlusion and detector crops; a secondary reference convention. | Benefits or failures interpretable without changing the primary population or silently dropping missing predictions. |

A faithful baseline adaptation matters more than the number of method names. For the core representation question, prioritize the strongest matched direct model, original paired method, selected candidate if any, initialized and shuffled controls, and frozen calibration/filter baselines. Add a verified learned refiner for practical context. The present SmoothNet-style network is a local adaptation, not a verification of the author's full recipe. A new-method performance claim also needs a credible recent competitor, such as SynSP or PS-Mamba, after its implementation, joint schema and tuning budget are checked. If that adaptation cannot be validated, narrow the claim rather than count an unreliable port as a baseline. Label all adaptations and checkpoint exposure; do not claim state of the art from an incomplete or incompatible roster.

Cache rendering and frozen pose extraction once; reuse those exact tracks across neural seeds, probes and compatible losses. Measure the real bottleneck before projecting GPU hours. Separate preparation, extraction, fitting, diagnostics and failed attempts in the cost report. This research plan does not submit jobs or commit an unmeasured allocation.

**A realistic one-week decision schedule**

The conservative external-transfer design requires new non-BML training data as well as the real-reference adapter; the cached current tracks cannot alone supply it. If access, a viable replacement roster or reference alignment remains unresolved after the first-day feasibility check, reduce the deadline commitment before launching a full matrix.

| Time from now | Work and deliverable | Decision if it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First day | Check MoVi access, import a few development videos, inspect reference overlays, retrieve original run artifacts, finish recipe probes, measure throughput. | If access or alignment is unresolved, stop promising a large real-data result for this deadline. Use only an already available verified dataset or a clearly synthetic scope. |
| Day 2, only if data and adapter checks pass | Freeze the audited data splits and metric contract; run the bounded candidate screen; record the primary contrast and sample-size rationale. | If no representation benefit or interpretable mechanism emerges, close the new-method branch and assess an explicitly empirical paper. |
| Days 3–4 | Run the cached main comparisons and declared seeds on the fixed populations; write methods and related work alongside them. | Preserve technical failures; rerun only documented failures under the fixed budget. Do not choose a favorable incomplete subset. |
| Day 5 | Open the full confirmation results, estimate paired uncertainty and inspect prespecified failures. | If evidence is broad or inconsistent, report that limit and decide whether to extend the project beyond this deadline. |
| Final days | Finish figures, reproducibility records and the manuscript; replace promised results in the abstract with completed evidence. | The paper's claims must fit completed results, even if the preferred JEPA hypothesis fails. |

This is conditional on data access and measured runtime. A method paper would be supported by a meaningful gain over equally supervised direct learning, an informative pairing ablation, and a credible independent-domain result. An empirical paper would need replicated, well-explained conditions under which feature learning adds little or changes motion fidelity, with strong baselines and reference checks. Neither outcome follows automatically from more computation or a larger dataset.

**Figures and artifacts that should carry the argument**

The paper should show the actual data and reference conventions before presenting aggregate gains: an annotated input/crop/reference example with timeline, followed by a person-level plot of paired coordinate and displacement effects with every seed visible. A matched trajectory example should show input, reference, strongest direct baseline and the candidate on shared axes, together with missing/extra peaks. A representation-probe and ablation figure should test the proposed explanation, while a separate external-domain table gives eligible-person counts, coverage and uncertainty. Select illustrative examples by declared metadata or fixed quantiles and label outcome-selected failure examples as such.

Release the permissible manifests, source/config/checkpoint hashes, data adapters, metric definitions, fixed split identifiers, reproducible analysis and visual-review schema. Keep restricted videos and body assets out of redistribution unless their terms permit it. The [independent review record](review.md) records the challenges to this plan and the remaining uncertainties.
