**One-week research plan following the completed synthetic restoration pilot**

19 September 2026. This is a proposed research schedule, not a compute authorization or a promise of a favorable result. No additional HAIC jobs or external submissions are made by this document.

The defensible opportunity is a controlled empirical study of whether latent prediction preserves useful motion beyond spatial calibration and matched coordinate learning. The present two-person, one-seed pilot motivates that question but cannot establish its general answer. A reproducible null or failure analysis can be scientifically substantive if it survives strong controls, adequate training checks, broader independent people and meaningful motion endpoints. Neither a statistically significant result nor ICLR acceptance can be promised within a week.

Generic temporal pose refinement is established by [SmoothNet](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136650615.pdf), while recovery of motion from noisy partial observations is a central pretraining task in [MotionBERT](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Zhu_MotionBERT_A_Unified_Perspective_on_Learning_Human_Motion_Representations_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf). Consequently, adding a synthetic denoiser or a JEPA component is not an established novelty claim. The intended contribution is the controlled separation of spatial bias correction, motion preservation and latent-pretraining utility, provided the experiments demonstrate a generalizable and useful distinction.

**What the evidence already supports.** The [downloaded-results analysis](../results/postrun-analysis-20260919/README.md) reconstructs 840 aggregate cells and 24 timing summaries. Spatial offset/affine controls recover large coordinate gains. Paired JEPA has no consistent coordinate advantage, increases aggregate displacement error, and loses to coordinate pretraining on the held nuisance combination for every extractor. Only a quarter of track records satisfy the timing-reference conditions. Every ineligible record has an ankle visibility gap, and time-varying scale affects peak counts. Training completed with finite losses and nonconstant feature diagnostics. These are exploratory findings from the inspected recipe and panel, not evidence that all JEPA approaches fail.

**The first day should resolve measurement ambiguity without new large training.** Copy the original `source-01/effective-config.json`, source evaluation tables, `data/bundle`, prediction files, fit reports and relevant preparation provenance from HAIC. The downloaded diagnostics omit several original baseline outcome tables and the raw neural predictions/targets. Preserve their identities and receipts. Recover the measured preparation and fitting times before projecting a larger compute budget.

Retain the old endpoint values as the original pilot. On separate output paths, compare raw pixel motion, normalization by a constant independent reference scale per window, and the original framewise scale. Use the same camera projection and scale policy for all methods. The independent evaluation scale is used only in scoring, never as an extra inference input. Check that adding a constant offset preserves the chosen displacement/timing measurements, that controlled amplitude attenuation and temporal shifts change the corresponding metrics as expected, and that a side swap changes the signed side-sensitive measurement. These are software/measurement checks, not new empirical model results.

Define two distinct evaluation populations before looking at the new comparison's outcomes: (1) fully supported visible-reference temporal windows, with exclusions and coverage reported; and (2) valid synthetic projected joints, including explicitly occluded proxies. The second can evaluate synthetic recovery where image visibility is absent, but does not establish real hidden-joint or anatomical accuracy. Do not fill unsupported metrics with zeros or replace the registered population by an outcome-selected subset. Establish event criteria using reference-only evidence, retain missed/extra counts, and give displacement or another supported physical-time observable priority over a fragile peak metric. An eligible panel must still contain the intended range of difficulties.

**The next sample should broaden independent motion information.** The existing protocol's provisional 24-training/8-development-person target is a reasonable scheduling target if valid assets, exposure records and measured throughput support it; it is not a power calculation. Preserve person/motion partitions and aliases, include multiple motions per person where available, and hold nuisance combinations and the extractor family out of fitting. Additional rendering views are desirable only after their projection/visibility path is verified; do not assume the current fixed-front adapter implements a new viewpoint. Unknown-exposure data remain development evidence. Existing sealed tests and prior STOP decisions remain intact.

**Freeze a compact but meaningful comparison.** Reuse implemented unchanged/filtering baselines, joint-offset and affine calibrations, initialized encoder plus fitted readout, matched coordinate pretraining, direct/static restoration, the temporal MLP and paired JEPA. Ordinary and shuffled-pair JEPA remain valuable supervision/pairing controls; the original outcome table must first be inspected. Calibration uses training labels and excludes the held extractor. All objective comparisons use the same pairs, partitions, readout capacity, masks, tuning allowance and declared resource contrast. Matching updates alone does not establish matching total compute.

The short pilot's teacher retains 33% direct initialization weight. Before repeating many seeds, inspect one prespecified longer training point for the relevant learned controls using an affordable, measured learning-curve allowance. Fix the EMA and learning-rate schedule before the decisive run. Changes need a new development recipe; extra updates are not guaranteed to improve generalization. Do not select favorable seeds or keep modifying settings until the primary contrast becomes positive.

The central test is whether temporal/latent learning supplies reproducible improvement beyond calibration while preserving independently specified motion measurements. If a calibration-first input variant is added to investigate residual motion learning, fit calibration on training data, train every matched arm on the same calibrated inputs, and retain the raw-input controls. Removing a bias estimated on development labels would leak outcome information and is not an admissible mechanism test.

**Define success independently of which method wins.** A positive method result requires a practically meaningful improvement over coordinate and practical controls, adequate preservation coverage, no unacceptable clean-input harm, and consistency across people/seeds or a clearly explained prespecified setting. A substantive evaluation result requires replication that calibration accounts for coordinate gains or that coordinate/motion rankings diverge, with controlled evidence separating those explanations from a broken metric, insufficient training or one particular synthetic convention. Report uncertainty by resampling independent people with motions nested within them, and report seed variation separately. Do not treat views, frames, windows or repeated seed runs as new people; do not infer equivalence from a nonsignificant difference. Final margins require reference repeatability and appropriate development evidence, not favorable candidate outcomes.

An independent reference check would strengthen both interpretations. A small real annotated panel can provide limited transfer evidence if annotations are available in time; it is not a prerequisite for a clearly scoped synthetic study and should not be rushed into unsupported clinical claims. If it is infeasible, keep the conclusions explicitly about synthetic proxies and demonstrate that the central finding is not confined to the two original motions or one measurement convention.

**Decision on Sep 23.** Continue toward a full paper only if the endpoint contract is working, the core comparison has enough completed independent evidence to support its stated scope, and the explanation is more informative than a single underperforming run. Either JEPA improvement or a well-supported calibration/preservation result may justify writing. Broad inconsistency, unresolved reference problems, or a remaining two-person study would be reasons to continue the research beyond this deadline. The paper should not depend on obtaining a desired p-value during its final two days.

All compute projections must include rendering, pose extraction, fitting/readouts and retries. The existing four-hour managed scope and the older 48-H100-hour proposal do not automatically authorize this expanded study. Use measured costs to produce a concrete bounded run plan before launching it; this document does not initiate that expansion.
