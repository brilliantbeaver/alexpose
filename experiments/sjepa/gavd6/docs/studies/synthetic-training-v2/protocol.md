# Paired synthetic pose restoration — development protocol 1

Frozen question, 18 September 2026: does paired synthetic supervision improve imperfect **2D body-12** tracks across unseen motions and pose extractors while preserving timing and anatomical sides, and does latent pretraining add value beyond matched coordinate denoising? This document defines the first development experiment. It does not freeze a confirmation claim or assert sufficient statistical power.

The task is offline restoration over 64 samples at 25 Hz (2.52 seconds first to last). Every method receives the same observed window and latency. The target is the same-camera, same-time projection of approximate SMPL-H joint centers onto the named COCO body-12 schema. It is a synthetic proxy, not an independent anatomical annotation. No clinical, 3D, hidden-real-joint, identity-recognition, or forecasting claim follows.

## Information and grouping

Inputs are estimated xy coordinates, actual estimator scores, observed flags, physical timestamps, and a constant per-window normalization computed only from observed inputs. Target coordinates, validity, exact visibility, intervention labels, renderer camera parameters and reference boxes are separate records. A missing input can have a valid target. Missing inputs are query positions, not structurally padded joints. Target-mask changes must not alter inference.

Initial top-down boxes are supplied foreground boxes for synthetic clips. They are privileged and recorded as such. The same boxes apply to all temporal arms. Input normalization uses no boxes; independent evaluation box diagonals normalize reported errors. Development box perturbations and detector/tracker boxes are required before automatic-pipeline claims. Confidence calibration may use only source training/development and must never infer confidence from true errors.

Use the existing AMASS identity registry, aliases, reservations and exposure history. A locomotion audit must name reviewer, evidence and review date; asset eligibility alone is insufficient. Keep all windows of an original motion, all overlapping windows, aliases and render variants together. Previously examined models/conditions and exposed recordings are development data. Unknown exposure prevents confirmation. Planned initial maximum: 24 training + 8 development people, at most 4 nonoverlapping windows/person, 6 conditions including clean; counts depend on eligibility. This is a feasibility proposal, not a power calculation.

Fix motion, shape, timestamps, camera, background, lighting, appearance and random seed within each declared nuisance pair except the named intervention. Begin with fixed-camera blur and obstruction. Pixel downsampling may model resolution loss without changing camera; camera-distance variants must use their own projected target and are separately named. Hold out nuisance combinations and a genuinely excluded extractor family. Do not infer that existing ViTPose assets are untouched.

## Comparisons and tuning allowance

All learned arms see identical training partitions and inference channels. Baselines are unchanged tracks, interpolation/filtering (strengths 0, 1, 2 frames), a source-trained SmoothNet-style 2D temporal MLP, a same-backbone direct denoiser, and a per-frame coordinate control retaining the same support/confidence/time information. Representation arms are initialized frozen, coordinate reconstruction, ordinary noisy-target masked JEPA, paired clean-target masked JEPA, and shuffled-pair pretraining. Each representation fits its own readout of the same architecture on the same allowed clean pairs. Shuffled donors stay within training people and nuisance strata, exclude the same source window, and never change natural evaluation pairs.

The main objective contrast is paired JEPA versus coordinate reconstruction with matched pairs, masks, steps, seeds, encoder/readout capacity and label access. Ordinary JEPA is a supervision-source control. The direct denoiser and SmoothNet-style model are practical comparators. Extra teacher/predictor parameters and cost are reported. A separate equal-total-compute comparison uses a measured common time budget including pretraining and readout, permitting unequal steps/exposure; it cannot be inferred from equal steps.

Starting model: width 96, 4 encoder layers, 2 predictor layers, 4 heads, patch4. AdamW 3e-4, weight decay 0.01, 5% warmup, gradient cap 1.0, batch 64 if measured memory permits. Mask 50% of eligible input tokens in joint/time blocks. The centered/sharpened objective uses student/teacher temperatures 0.1/0.06 and shared EMA 0.99→0.999. Losses/diagnostics are float32. This is privileged synthetic supervision, assembled from established components. No label-free or official S-JEPA-v2 claim.

One seed for feasibility, 200 updates smoke and at most2,000 screening updates, then three prespecified seeds 17/29/43 for decisive finalists. The same allowed learning-rate candidates (1e-4,3e-4), selected only on development, apply to coordinate and latent arms. Further changes require a new development protocol and measured learning curves within the cap. CPU fixtures use explicitly smaller capacity and 2 updates and cannot select a scientific method. Log realized masks, skipped support, teacher entropy/variance/rank, input dependence, initialized/online/teacher states, effective teacher lag and initialization contribution.

## Endpoints and decision rules

Primary coordinate endpoint: visible body-12 Euclidean landmark error divided by independently supplied reference-box diagonal, with missing predictions penalized by1 diagonal. First average repeated variants/windows within motions, then motions within people (or verified recording groups), then people. Report each extractor and training seed separately. Report lower limbs,95th-percentile error, initially accurate/clean tracks and nuisance strata. Synthetic all/occluded-joint errors are separate endpoints.

Companion motion endpoints: signed ankle separation and fixed 0.20 s displacement error in normalized image coordinates; amplitude error/attenuation and supported event timing in seconds. No time warping during scoring. Unsupported trajectories return explicit counts and missing metrics. Cadence is pending unless at least two cycles are observed. Save predictions, targets, masks, times and group identifiers; reconstruct all tables from them. Draw paired cluster uncertainty with people outermost and motions nested; render repetitions are not independent samples. Separate seed variability from sample uncertainty.

Provisional meaningful-effect proposals are 2% relative primary improvement, 5% displacement-error reduction and <1% relative clean degradation. Final margins and confirmation sizes require fresh development variance and annotation repeatability. Gate B needs matched-coordinate and practical-control comparisons, adequate support, motion preservation and multiple seeds. A failed JEPA gate stops its expansion, while a useful direct denoiser may proceed independently. Gate A separately requires synthetic image adaptation to exceed augmented-COCO replay or establish motion-coverage value without retention harm. A failure of either branch does not cancel the other.

Personalization remains closed on the old panel: retrospective extra student-specific oracle headroom is about 0.0406%, not an achieved policy. New development headroom is required before reopening. Video features require an independent incremental-benefit gate and are outside the first implementation milestone. Gates use exactly `pass`, `fail`, or `insufficient_evidence`; fixture success is never a scientific pass.

## Pre-fit implementation clarifications following independent review

These clarifications precede any source fitting. The [review snapshot](protocol-review.md) remains unchanged. Confirmation margins remain uncalibrated; no automatic real confirmation scorer is enabled in this milestone.

**Normalization and metric formulas.** For each noisy observed window, origin is the median observed xy point and scale is the Euclidean norm of the axis-wise 95th minus 5th percentile spans. This single constant transform applies to noisy inputs, clean teacher coordinates and coordinate targets. Predictions are inverted to image pixels before scoring. Evaluation uses its separately supplied per-frame reference-box diagonal, never a scale fitted to candidate output. The scale's independence from predictions does not make the synthetic landmark convention an independent anatomical reference. Missing confidence is numerically filled with zero only at the model adapter; the observed mask remains false and the raw artifact keeps its missing score.

Visible error averages supported joints within frames, then frames within windows; variants within windows, windows within motions, motions within canonical people are balanced. Displacement error is the norm of predicted minus reference displacement at exactly 0.20 seconds, divided by the starting frame's evaluation diagonal. Both endpoints require visible references; missing output receives one diagonal penalty. Signed ankle separation is `(left_ankle_x - right_ankle_x) / reference_diagonal`; its error is an absolute difference, so opposing signed errors cannot cancel. Amplitude is the RMS of this separation after subtracting its window mean. Amplitude error is the absolute difference of predicted and reference RMS. The ratio is undefined for reference amplitude at or below 1e-8.

Amplitude requires complete bilateral visible-reference and finite-prediction support over the window. Event timing uses strictly positive local maxima of demeaned signed separation, at least two reference maxima, equal predicted/reference peak counts, and ordered one-to-one pairing. It reports mean absolute timestamp error in seconds without warping. These are 2D ankle-separation maxima, not heel strikes or clinical events. Unmatched or unsupported trajectories remain missing evidence, never successful zeros. Cadence is not estimated. Gaps prevent a preservation pass rather than being silently discarded. Clean relative degradation is undefined when the unchanged clean-condition error is at or below 1e-8.

**Mask and objective boundary.** Select half the observed time-patch/joint tokens (up to rounding) using only observed input support. Separate restoration queries include any patch with at least one naturally missing input frame. Loss query support is the union of those two sets intersected with valid clean target tokens for both paired JEPA and coordinate pretraining. Coordinate error averages valid frames within each patch before identical per-window reduction. Ordinary JEPA uses the same queries with observed-track target support, which can be smaller. Its supervision-source difference is intentional. Fixed output queries are created independently of target masks. The EMA teacher sees clean synthetic tracks transformed by noisy-input geometry; its embeddings are detached. Target validity enters only teacher training support and loss reduction. Shared stability machinery is centered CE plus the existing VICReg helper at weight 0.05 on two translated prepared-input views (translation magnitude 0.02); this is an explicit local recipe, not a published-model reproduction.

**Practical MLP.** The SmoothNet-style adaptation applies one shared temporal MLP independently to each anatomical joint. Its input has 64 × 5 entries (xy, confidence, observed, seconds), two hidden layers of twice the configured encoder width with GELU, and 128 coordinate outputs. It predicts residuals on observed inputs and absolute coordinates otherwise. The practical loss is L1 coordinate error plus reference-acceleration L1 on valid consecutive triples with actual elapsed times. Predeclared acceleration weights are 0 and 0.1, with 0.1 as the initial setting. It does not penalize acceleration toward zero. These adaptations change the published architecture/loss; no official reproduction claim is made. The same learning-rate allowance applies to practical and coordinate arms; source model selection must count all fits. The static control uses per-frame coordinates after the common whole-window normalization, plus the same full auxiliary channels. It tests incremental coordinate history conditional on that transform, not zero information from other frames.

**Executable source adjudication.** Without a pre-fit decision specification bound into run identity, Gate B returns insufficient evidence. The specification must cite a calibration artifact and supply minimum independent groups (at least 2), minimum training seeds (at least 3), coordinate/displacement margins, clean degradation ceiling, maximum amplitude error and maximum event-time error. Two groups is a software admissibility minimum, not a power recommendation. The initial planning targets remain 2%, 5% and 1%; final absolute amplitude/timing bounds require independent reference repeatability and fresh development evidence. They cannot be chosen from a favorable candidate result.

For each prespecified extractor and seed, compare paired JEPA against each of coordinate pretraining, direct denoising, SmoothNet-style refinement and initialized readout. Require the relative coordinate improvement to meet its margin and its paired 95% interval to exclude zero; require displacement reduction, supported amplitude and event timing within calibrated bounds, and clean retention against unchanged tracks. Missing support yields insufficient evidence. A failed practical/preservation condition yields fail. The overall gate is the conjunction of these predeclared comparisons; report each component and interval. These conservative rules do not license real transfer. An initially useful denoiser can progress through a separate independently referenced real-development decision even if JEPA fails.

**Source preparation scope.** The first executable adapter is deliberately smaller than the six-variant proposal: fixed front camera, clean, blur, lower-body obstruction, and blur+obstruction held out on development people. It changes no motion or camera to create a pair. Camera-distance and additional viewpoints remain later declared experiments. Achieved counts, exact extractor families and exclusions, authoritative identity/reservation/exposure manifests, contact sheets and asset/code hashes must exist before source fitting. Unusual-motion and independently annotated anatomical strata remain pending rather than receiving gait-preservation claims. No executed fixture selects their eventual assignments.

The decision specification must name the actual calibration artifact and its SHA256. Before fitting or adjudication, the loader verifies matching body-12 schema, 64/25-Hz time contract, source/real-development evidence status, reviewer and reference-manifest provenance, explicit exclusion of candidate outputs, and exact agreement between its retained decision values and the requested margins. A nonempty hash string alone cannot authorize a gate. The software cannot authenticate a human's declaration; independent review must inspect the underlying references.
