# Research-direction review dispositions

28 September 2026. Applies to the appended `writeup/RESEARCH_DIRECTION.md`; the three-page overview source remains unchanged.

| Independent objection | Revision |
|---|---|
| Broad novelty already occupied. | Cited Stenum, DeepGaitLab, Cotton–Sinz, Pace, SynthGait-19K and OpenCap Monocular near the claim. Restricted originality to the proposed measurement test and a demonstrated benefit from targeted conflicting-trajectory search. Removed any basis for a first-ever claim. |
| Landmark agreement does not establish equivalence of all video evidence. | Search fits visible 2D detections only; hidden predictions remain unobserved. The text states that silhouettes could rule out an alternative. |
| Local search cannot provide outer uncertainty bounds. | Search is explicitly incomplete. Successful alternatives can show ambiguity under assumptions; failed searches do not certify identifiability. CLOSURE and SLUE are cited as methodological precedents. |
| “Unchanged” can be confused with an inconclusive interval. | Defined change beyond a pilot-derived resolution band, sub-resolution change within it, and inconclusive comparisons otherwise. Both legs' intervals are calibrated jointly. |
| Frame rejection can erase knee peaks. | Retain or decline complete trial pairs; preserve fixed support and exact percentile measurements. |
| Primary success and selection could be mixed. | Test change-error noninferiority, then trajectory superiority, on all reference-eligible pairs with prespecified failure handling. Evaluate selective reporting separately at matched retention. Person-level resampling retains all pairs; calibration accounts for repeated pairs. |
| A public pilot may contain too little real knee change and may already have tuned a comparator. | Added a reference-resolvable-change gate, prespecified null/change composition, the ten-person healthy-adult limitation, and independent-data requirement for final comparative claims. |
| v08's 2D encoder cannot be assumed to initialize an unspecified 3D model. | Defined a compatible 2D encoder and 3D readout receiving a fixed 3D initialization. JEPA pretraining varies before identical supervised adaptation, with its extra data and compute reported. |
| Stronger algorithms must earn their complexity. | Kept zero-response and interpolation controls; compare reporting flags against visibility, calibrated intervals and ensemble disagreement. Report failures, group retention, and computation sensitivity. |
| HCI or clinical benefit could be assumed. | Resident/assessor evaluation remains future work. No claim of demonstrated fall prediction, balance inference, or clinical benefit. |
