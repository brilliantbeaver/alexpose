## Figure 1 in the overview: three primary comparisons

[Vector SVG](09-overview-results.svg) · [Vector PDF](09-overview-results.pdf) · [300 dpi PNG](09-overview-results.png)

The graph preserves v08's three primary gains and their intervals. Response gains and waveform gains remain separate outcomes. The source estimates and hash are retained in [09-overview-provenance.json](09-overview-provenance.json).

Alt text: Three recorded primary gains have 95 percent intervals spanning zero: core JEPA versus direct training with the change objective, delta versus endpoint feature training, and dense versus low-scalar readout supervision.

## Figure 2 in the continuation: crossed movement and visibility

[Vector SVG](10-research-design.svg) · [Vector PDF](10-research-design.pdf) · [300 dpi PNG](10-research-design.png)

This conceptual diagram shows clear and digitally occluded versions of two separately recorded walking trials. Within-row comparisons should preserve the same movement measurement. Between-row comparisons should preserve the independently measured change. The caption specifies every clear/occluded pairing, including mixed-visibility cases not individually connected with arrows. No experimental outcome is plotted. [10-research-provenance.json](10-research-provenance.json) records its conceptual status and generating-script hash.

Alt text: Proposed crossed experiment: each of two reference-measured trials is observed clearly and with digital occlusion. Within-trial comparisons should preserve the same measurement; between-trial comparisons should preserve the reference-defined change.

Both figures use vector text with embedded PDF fonts. The scripts check text bounds before export; both were visually inspected in the final combined PDF.
