# World-model-first candidates

**Terms.** A world model predicts system change. Latent prediction forecasts compressed internal features. Inverse dynamics infers the action or cause behind a transition. SMPL is a parametric 3D human mesh. A JEPA predicts masked latent targets. SVHO means source-video-held-out GAVD evaluation.

1. **Horizon atlas** `[physics-vision,S-JEPA-gen]`. Q/bet: does impairment alter joint-by-horizon error topology? Topology, not mean surprise, carries signal. Base `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`; adapt horizon heads. Bridge: camera/occlusion/noise-project clean AMASS 3D to noisy GAVD 2D. Primary: SVHO map repeatability. Null: detector noise wins. Shortcut/control: confidence/speed; stratify/time-warp. 72h: three horizons, 50 epochs, `1x3x50/100=1.5 H100-h`. Prior difference: MDM uses scalar surprise.

2. **Forward-inverse cycle** `[S-JEPA-gen,grounded-motion]`. Q/bet: does round-trip inconsistency expose implausible transitions missed forward-only? Base `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_paired_shared_no_cross_best.pt`; adapt inverse-delta head. Bridge: clean AMASS 3D to corrupted 2D, then noisy GAVD 2D joints. Primary: SVHO cycle residual. Null: cycle equals smoothness. Shortcut/control: autocorrelation; reverse/shuffle clips. 72h: 50 epochs, `1x3x50/100=1.5 H100-h`. Prior difference: SC3-Eval targets robot actions.

3. **Minimum healthy edit** `[grounded-motion,sim-loss]`. Q/bet: is the smallest dynamics-preserving repair stable across lifts? Bases `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt` and `Refinement.pt`; adapt a SMPL-to-Rajagopal retargeter. Bridge: corrupt projected clean AMASS 3D, multi-lift noisy GAVD 2D. Primary: SVHO edit-direction stability. Null: monocular repair is unidentified. Shortcut/control: speed/scale; preserve both. 72h: 25 epochs, `1x3x25/100=0.75 H100-h`. Prior difference: GAITGen transfers severity latents.

4. **Physics-typicality cross** `[sim-loss,grounded-motion]`. Q/bet: are mechanical feasibility and healthy typicality separable axes? Bases `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt` and `pp-motion_pretrained/checkpoint_latest.pth`; adapt a two-score calibrator. Bridge: corrupt clean AMASS 3D to 2D; multi-lift noisy GAVD 2D. Primary: SVHO disagreement stability. Null: both measure lift quality. Shortcut/control: pose confidence; corruption-matched negatives. 72h: 25 epochs, `1x3x25/100=0.75 H100-h`. Prior difference: PP-Motion scores generated motion singly.

5. **Depth gauge** `[depth,physics-vision]`. Q/bet: can relative depth remove view-dependent JEPA error without metric depth? Bases `Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf` and `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_reflection_equivariant_best.pt`; adapt a depth-conditioned residual head. Bridge: render clean AMASS 3D to noisy 2D plus relative depth; sample noisy GAVD 2D/depth joints. Primary: SVHO cross-view error agreement. Null: relative depth adds no gauge. Shortcut/control: background/view; depth-shuffle within view. 72h: 25 epochs, `1x3x25/100=0.75 H100-h`. Prior difference: VideoMDM commits to one lift.

6. **Video-skeleton agreement** `[physics-vision,S-JEPA-gen]`. Q/bet: does future disagreement isolate motion that RGB and pose priors explain differently? Bases `vitl.pt` and `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`; adapt projection heads. Bridge: pair clean AMASS 3D stick renders with corrupted 2D, then noisy GAVD RGB/2D-pose. Primary: SVHO temporal disagreement. Null: video latents encode appearance. Shortcut/control: background; person crops/background swaps. 72h: 50 epochs, `1x3x50/100=1.5 H100-h`. Prior difference: CARE-PD uses class probes.

7. **Body-device dynamics** `[body+object,physics-vision]`. Q/bet: do assistive objects change predictable body dynamics beyond visibility? Bases `sam2.1_hiera_large.pt` and `vitl.pt`; adapt joint body-object predictor. Bridge: attach devices to clean AMASS 3D, render/noise to 2D, prompt-track noisy GAVD 2D devices. Primary: SVHO coupling residual. Null: tracks add nothing. Shortcut/control: device presence; mask/swap devices. 72h: 50 epochs, `1x3x50/100=1.5 H100-h`. Prior difference: ProGait recognizes, not predicts, coupling.

8. **Tool-selecting judge** `[Qwen-orchestrator,depth]`, reject-first. Q/bet: can learned tool choice improve calibrated abstention over a fixed pipeline? Base `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct`; adapt a small reinforcement-learning policy head over depth, mask, and JEPA calls. Bridge: train tool choices on corrupted clean AMASS 3D-to-2D tasks; run noisy GAVD 2D. Primary: SVHO risk-coverage. Null: fixed routing suffices. Shortcut/control: source/view; hide metadata/swap backgrounds. 72h: 25 epochs, `1x3x25/100=0.75 H100-h`. Prior difference: HumanScore fixes its tools.

9. **Masked human action transfer** `[body+object,physics-vision]`, reject-first. Q/bet: do robot-trained mask controls encode human response dynamics zero-shot? Bases `masked_world_lora_high.safetensors` and `masked_world_lora_low.safetensors` on `PAI/Wan2.2-Fun-A14B-Control`; adapt only a score head. Bridge: render clean AMASS 3D limb masks and SAM-track noisy GAVD 2D masks. Primary: SVHO control-response agreement. Null: robot semantics do not transfer. Shortcut/control: static copying; reverse controls/background swaps. 72h: 10 epochs, `1x3x10/100=0.3 H100-h`; reproduction is `8x96=768 H200-h`, unconverted. Prior difference: MVA studies robots.
