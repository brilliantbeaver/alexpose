# AMASS audit

Audit date: 2026-09-03.

## Scope and method

AMASS is a harmonized archive of motion capture stored as SMPL+H, a parametric 3D body model with hands. I attempted a read-only SSH audit of `tedmui@haic.stanford.edu`. The first attempt resolved the host, but port 22 timed out. A second attempt failed during host-name resolution. `/hai` is not mounted locally. Counts below therefore come from manifests copied from HAIC, not a live directory walk. On 2026-09-03, the user confirmed that the registered SMPL+H assets distributed for AMASS are available on HAIC.

`amass_raw_inventory.csv` was generated by recursively opening every `*_poses.npz`, recording its shape and frame rate, and hashing its bytes. All 10,941 rows are readable. Paths and SHA-256 values are unique. Recorded HAIC paths are:

- source: `/hai/scratch/tedmui/alexpose/experiments/sjepa/gavd6/data/amass/extracted`
- original Core11 tensors: `/hai/scratch/tedmui/alexpose/experiments/sjepa/gavd6/data/amass/outputs/core11`
- gauge-neutral tensors: `/hai/scratch/tedmui/alexpose/experiments/sjepa/gavd6/outputs/latent-laterality/amass-neutral/core11`
- body models: `/hai/scratch/tedmui/body_models`

The source and body-model paths come from the HAIC environment contract. Run artifacts embed the original Core11 root. The gauge-neutral root follows the executed Slurm output contract. The body assets are operator-confirmed; the current source and output directory contents remain unverified from this environment.

## Locomotion estimates

No action ground truth is attached to this snapshot. Two reproducible proxies disagree:

- The completed gauge-neutral conversion accepts pelvis displacement at least 0.10 m and path straightness at least 0.20. It accepts 3,134 sequences, 35.4%, totaling 575,290 canonical frames or 5.327 hours. This is travelling motion, not gait. It includes crawling and transitions and rejects treadmill gait.
- A case-insensitive word-boundary regex over `motion_id` for `walk|run|jog|treadmill|march|stroll|pace|stride|locomotion|gait`, including inflections, finds 2,079 eligible sequences, 23.5%, and 5.431 native hours, 18.6%. The intersection with the travel proxy is 682 sequences and 1.079 hours.

These are candidate-pool sizes, not lower or upper bounds on gait seconds. BABEL frame labels are needed for a defensible duration. The user confirms that the BABEL annotations have been downloaded but not extracted.

## Existing AMASS checkpoints and results

A joint embedding predictive architecture (JEPA) predicts masked latent features. `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/` contains four seed-7 best checkpoints. Its only intact aggregate row is `standard_sjepa`: 100 epochs, selected epoch 95, 7,326 seconds, validation KL 0.2555, and no test read. Treat all four as diagnostic feature extractors.

`outputs/latent-laterality/decisive/amass-v2-chart-paired/` contains correction-first, structured-gauge JEPA (SG-JEPA), and uniform-posterior seed-7 checkpoints. Validation used 15 identities and 1,146 windows. SG-JEPA and the uniform control have nearly identical odd-orbit NMAE, 0.8671 versus 0.8668, so current evidence does not support probability-aware structure. `outputs/swap-probe-seed7/` and `outputs/latent-laterality/amass-gauge-v2-seed7-validation/` hold the corresponding readouts.
