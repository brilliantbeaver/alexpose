# Recent close gait priors

All sources were fetched and read in full on 2026-09-03. “Collision” statements are repository-specific inferences; other claims below are reported full-text facts or direct artifact observations.

## CARE-PD

**Method and result.** CARE-PD harmonizes RGB video through WHAM and MoCap through SparseFusion into 30 Hz anonymized SMPL: 362 participants, 8,477 walks, 18.66 hours, nine cohorts, eight centers, six countries (Table 1). It benchmarks frozen motion encoders plus linear/kNN probes under within-site, cross-dataset, leave-one-dataset-out, and adaptation protocols, then adapts MotionAGFormer and MoMask on clinical pretext tasks. CARE-PD adaptation moved MotionAGFormer from 60.7 to 7.5 mm MPJPE and 48.1% to 65.1% severity macro-F1 (Table 2). **Collision (inference):** multi-site SMPL curation, frozen-encoder PD probes, and generic cross-site severity benchmarking are taken; use CARE-PD as an external transfer benchmark. **Limits:** only four cohorts have severity labels; score 3 is rare; protocol/rater, mesh, demographic, and lab-domain biases remain (Sec. F). **Access:** data and MIT code are public; project terms restrict data to noncommercial use and altered redistribution. No CARE-tuned checkpoint was found; the repository contains an upstream MoMask weight. Sources: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.04312>, <https://neurips2025.care-pd.ca/>, <https://github.com/TaatiTeam/CARE-PD>, <https://huggingface.co/datasets/vida-adl/CARE-PD>.

## DiffuseGaitNet

**Method and result.** A classifier-free guided DDPM learns 30-frame 3D-pose windows conditioned on severity and 15 clinical gait features; DDIM generates augmentation using random labels and the unlabeled test subject's features, then an encoder-only transformer classifies real plus synthetic data (Algorithm 1). The plot reports about 93% subject-level PD macro-F1 under LOSO, versus about 50% without diffusion augmentation (Fig. 2d; approximate plot reading). **Collision (inference):** “diffusion-generated PD gait improves severity classification” is taken, including test-feature-conditioned synthesis. **Limits:** the PD experiment is binary scores 0/1 on 29 early-stage participants, uses transductive test features and short windows, and costs 10 to 12 GPU-hours per held-out subject (Sec. IV). **Access:** MIT code and one fold-0 TensorFlow diffusion checkpoint are public; the clinical dataset is explicitly private. Sources: <https://doi.org/10.1109/TNSRE.2025.3589074>, <https://www.yousefnezhad.com/publications/TNSRE25.pdf>, <https://github.com/arshakRz/DiffuseGaitNet>.

## GAITGen

**Method and result.** GAITGen trains a conditional residual VQ-VAE with separate motion/pathology latents and codebooks, then masked and residual transformers; “Mix and Match” recombines motion from one walk with pathology from another. Its participant-held-out PD-GaM classifier rises from 0.66 to 0.74 F1 with full synthetic augmentation (Table 2), while generation AVE is 0.194 versus 0.898 for retrained MoMask (Table 1). **Collision (inference):** severity-conditioned impaired-gait generation, motion/pathology disentanglement, and latent pathology transfer are directly taken; a counterfactual claim needs a distinct normal-only predictive objective and validation. **Limits:** PD-GaM has 30 people and 1,701 WHAM-derived sequences; evaluation remains mostly in-dataset, with six raters on 65 clips. **Access:** MIT training code is public, but the README still marks processed data and pretrained checkpoints unreleased; the repository contains evaluator statistics, not GAITGen weights. This conflicts with the paper's “publicly available” data claim. Sources: <https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Adeli_GAITGen_Disentangled_Motion-Pathology_Impaired_Gait_Generative_Model_--_Bringing_Motion_WACV_2026_paper.pdf>, <https://vadeli.github.io/GAITGen/>, <https://github.com/TaatiTeam/GAITGen>.

## Aggregate, Don't Adapt

**Method and result.** A frozen Human3.6M-pretrained MotionAGFormer-S feeds one 4x512 linear head, test-mean centering, subject-mean posterior aggregation, subject-kNN pooling, and label-free test-marginal adjustment. It ranked first of 58 at 0.69447 hidden-test macro-F1; subject aggregation alone added 0.143 (Tables 2 and 3). **Collision (inference):** frozen-probe cross-site severity, subject-level posterior aggregation, and transductive calibration are taken baselines, not novel endpoints. **Limits:** all selection used paid reads on one fixed hidden test after local CV was anticorrelated; five seeds span 0.558 to 0.694, and one-label-per-subject aggregation discards within-subject variation (Secs. 4, 7). **Access:** MIT code and the exact 14 KB trained head are public; CARE-PD data and three third-party binaries are referenced, not redistributed. Sources: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.20587>, <https://github.com/jlshen025/codabench/tree/main/mocha>.
