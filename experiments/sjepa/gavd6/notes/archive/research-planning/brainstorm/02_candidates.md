# Consolidated candidate set

## Terms and common contract

A world model predicts how a system changes. A latent prediction forecasts an internal feature vector rather than a pixel or coordinate. Inverse dynamics infers the preceding action or motion change that could explain a transition; it does not imply measured force here. SMPL is a parametric 3D body model. A joint-embedding predictive architecture, or JEPA, predicts hidden latent targets from visible motion.

GaitDynamics is a diffusion motion prior over Rajagopal OpenSim coordinates, which describe a musculoskeletal body without arms. PP-Motion is a learned physical-perceptual evaluator for SMPL motion. WHAM and GVHMR recover world-grounded SMPL or SMPL-X motion from monocular video. SMPL-X extends SMPL with hands and a face. V-JEPA 2 predicts video features. The Human Motion Diffusion Model, or MDM, generates 3D motion. SAM 2 is a promptable video mask tracker. A heteroscedastic head predicts uncertainty that can change with each input. A zero-initialized branch starts with no effect on its frozen base. A rank-8 adapter represents an update with two thin matrices whose inner dimension is eight. Split-conformal calibration sets an error threshold on a held-out calibration split. A risk-coverage curve plots error against the fraction of cases the model accepts.

Every GAVD result uses source-video-held-out folds because the release has no participant identifier. The primary label analysis is macro one-versus-rest average precision, the mean area under the precision-recall curve after treating each `gait_pat` label against all others. Only labels with enough source videos for each fold enter that mean. `style` and `pregnant` remain descriptive because they have only three and two source videos. No output is called a diagnosis. The common shortcut set, called S0 below, matches or stratifies camera view, duration, cadence or speed, centroid drift, foreground area, pose confidence, and missing-joint rate. It also includes raw-coordinate, random-encoder, and confidence-only controls when applicable.

Every bridge starts with clean AMASS 3D motion, projects it through sampled cameras, scale, occlusion, and detector-like noise, and applies the same observation route to noisy GAVD video. Multi-lift candidates compare several plausible 3D reconstructions instead of treating one monocular lift as truth. All 348 source videos are operator-confirmed as decodable on HAIC and cover all 1,874 sequences, but a complete pose manifest does not exist. A 72-hour pilot can extract a locked source subset directly; the validated local cache of 121 sequences from 18 videos remains the fallback smoke test.

Every virtual perturbation, repair, or rollout is a query to the model. It is not evidence of how the person would respond to a physical intervention.

Compute uses the conservative local anchor of one H100 for 3 hours per 100 JEPA epochs. Independent runs reduce wall time on eight H100s but not GPU-hours. Frozen-model inference is measured separately. Published A100, H200, and other hardware costs are never converted to H100-hours.

Every local checkpoint written as `seed-7_*.pt` below resolves under `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/`. The phrase local S-JEPA means `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` unless another full path is named.

Lens tags are: `L1` vision physics, `L2` physical grounding, `L3` discriminative or generative S-JEPA, `L4` simulator-corrected loss, `L5` depth on a motion embedding, `L6` Qwen reinforcement-learning tool use, and `L7` body-object keypoints.

## Candidates

**C01. Horizon-resolved latent error atlas `[L1,L3]`.** Question and bet: does the joint-by-time shape of prediction failure distinguish observed gait presentations after camera effects are removed? The bet is that topology across 0.25, 0.5, and 1.0 second horizons carries information that pooled surprise loses. Freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and train small heteroscedastic horizon heads on paired clean and corrupted AMASS Core11 motion, then score 2D and WHAM-witnessed GAVD. Primary evidence is source-video-held-out label association and anatomical map repeatability. Trained, random, and raw parity would teach that detector noise dominates. S0 plus time warping controls the main shortcut. `Atlas-72` trains three 25-epoch seeds: `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`. Unlike MDM surprise, the object is an error field across horizons.

**C02. Forward-inverse motion cycle `[L1,L2,L3]`.** Question and bet: does disagreement between a future prediction and the preceding motion change recovered from that future expose transitions missed by forward error alone? Freeze local `seed-7_paired_shared_no_cross_best.pt` and add matched zero-initialized forward and inverse latent-delta heads. Train on clean AMASS and its corrupted 2D twin, then run GAVD through paired 2D and lifted paths. The primary measurement is a joint-time cycle residual. No gain over finite differences would show that the cycle is only a smoothness test. S0, reversed clips, shuffled transitions, and a forward-only arm control autocorrelation. `Cycle-72` uses two heads, three seeds, and 25 epochs: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 2 x 3 = 4.50 H100-hours`. SC3-Eval applies this query to robot actions, not human gait.

**C03. Multi-lift minimum normalizing edit `[L1,L2]`.** Question and bet: is the smallest normal-prior motion edit anatomically stable across plausible monocular lifts? Freeze public `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt` and `GaitDynamicsRefinement.pt`, retarget AMASS SMPL into Rajagopal coordinates, learn a lift-error envelope from corrupted projections, and use constrained test-time inpainting on GAVD lift ensembles. The primary output is a joint-angle correction with cross-lift agreement and an abstention flag. Unstable edits would establish that a monocular normalizing counterfactual is unidentified. S0, cadence preservation, camera perturbation, phase shuffle, and equal-size random edits control the main shortcuts. `Repair-72` fits three 10-epoch stability calibrators: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus measured frozen inference. This changes the scientific object from scalar surprise to an explicit repair.

**C04. Physical feasibility versus healthy typicality `[L1,L2,L4]`.** Question and bet: are mechanically infeasible motion and clinically atypical motion separable axes? Freeze `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt` for healthy-motion typicality and `pp-motion_pretrained/checkpoint_latest.pth` for simulator-derived feasibility. Map AMASS SMPL into both coordinate systems, estimate the disagreement induced by projection and relifting, then apply the same route to GAVD. Evidence is stable two-dimensional geometry and label profiles at fixed feasibility. Collinearity after corruption matching would show that both scores only measure lift quality. S0, randomized PP-Motion outputs, and confidence-matched AMASS corruptions are the key controls. `TwoAxes-72` trains three 25-epoch calibration heads: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`; PP-Motion training hardware is unreported and not converted. Prior scoring work reports one scalar at a time rather than their disagreement.

**C05. Motion-conditioned depth residual `[L1,L3,L5]`.** Question and bet: can temporal motion predict relative joint depth well enough to remove view-dependent JEPA error? Freeze `Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf` and local `seed-7_reflection_equivariant_best.pt`, then add a zero-initialized joint-depth distribution head. Exact AMASS depths supervise corrupted 2D projections; Depth Anything and WHAM act as independent witnesses on GAVD. Primary evidence is interval calibration and cross-view stability of joint-depth residual maps. Dependence on camera label or witness would reject depth as a stable gait measurement. S0, per-frame affine depth normalization, depth shuffle within view, and a single-frame depth baseline control scene leakage. `Depth-72` trains true and shuffled heads for three 25-epoch seeds: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 2 x 3 = 4.50 H100-hours`, plus frozen extraction.

**C06. Video-skeleton predictive agreement `[L1,L3]`.** Question and bet: does disagreement between RGB and skeleton futures isolate motions that the two priors explain differently? Freeze V-JEPA 2 `vitl.pt` and local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`; train rank-8 cross-modal prediction tokens. Pair AMASS 3D with randomized stick-figure and silhouette composites, then align those paths to person-cropped GAVD RGB and 2D pose. Evidence is source-held temporal disagreement that remains stable after background replacement. If background-only or raw 2D features win, the video prior adds appearance rather than motion. S0, person-mask swaps, background swaps, and frozen random projections are required. `CrossModal-72` uses three 50-epoch seeds: `1 x 3 x 50/100 x 3 = 4.50 H100-hours`, plus a 4 H100-hour extraction budget. CARE-PD probes representations; this candidate measures disagreement between predictions.

**C07. Body-device relational dynamics `[L1,L3,L7]`.** Question and bet: does predicting relative body and assistive-device motion reveal more than merely seeing the device? Freeze SAM 2.1 `sam2.1_hiera_large.pt` and local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`; add rank-8 object tokens and a joint body-object predictor. Attach simple cane, walker, and prosthesis endpoint constraints to AMASS motion for the clean 3D pole, render and corrupt both track sets, then obtain GAVD device endpoints from prompted masks. ProGait supplies a public device stress test. Evidence is the body forecast improvement and contact-timing residual beyond a presence-only model. Equality with shuffled device tracks would show no relational content. S0, source-matched crops, device masking, and trajectory swaps control context leakage. `Device-72` runs three arms, three seeds, 25 epochs: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 x 3 = 6.75 H100-hours`, plus 4 extraction hours.

**C08. Lift-consensus abstention `[L2,L5]`.** Question and bet: is disagreement between independent lifts a usable uncertainty signal rather than a gait signal? Freeze `wham_vit_w_3dpw.pth.tar` and `gvhmr_siga24_release.ckpt`, and fit a two-layer consensus calibrator. Project and relift AMASS to learn error against known 3D, then ensemble both routes on GAVD. The primary result is a selective risk-coverage curve and the stability of C03 repair vectors within the accepted set. Universal disagreement would forbid the planned 3D claims and would still be informative. S0, known AMASS corruptions, and a pose-confidence-only abstainer are the controls. `LiftGate-72` trains three 25-epoch calibrators: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`, plus a 4 H100-hour extraction budget. Registered AMASS body assets are available; exact WHAM and GVHMR variant compatibility remains a setup check.

**C09. Cross-prior disagreement abstention `[L1,L2,L3]`.** Question and bet: does disagreement among independently trained motion priors identify cases where a characterization should be withheld? Freeze local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`, `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt`, and MDM `humanml-encoder-512-50steps`; calibrate their disagreement on corrupted AMASS and lock a split-conformal threshold before held-source GAVD testing. Primary evidence is risk-coverage area and macro average precision at fixed coverage. Failure to rank errors would show that the priors share a blind spot. S0 and a pose-confidence-only conformal rule control the obvious explanation. `PriorGate-72` fits three 10-epoch calibration seeds: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus frozen inference. This is not subject aggregation, which GAVD cannot support.

**C10. Missing-modality predictive set `[L3,L5,L7]`.** Question and bet: can one normal-motion representation retain calibrated evidence when pose, lift, depth, silhouette, or object tracks disappear? Freeze local S-JEPA, WHAM, Depth Anything V2, and SAM 2.1; train a set-pooling adapter with explicit availability masks and leave-one-modality-out alignment. AMASS supplies synchronized clean 3D, projected 2D, depth, silhouette, and synthetic device views; GAVD supplies noisy subsets. Evidence is selective risk under every modality subset, not only complete input. A mask-only model matching the full adapter would expose class-correlated missingness. S0, label-balanced dropout, modality shuffle, and a validity-only arm are required. `DropSet-72` trains dropout and complete-only arms for three 25-epoch seeds: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 2 x 3 = 4.50 H100-hours`, plus frozen tool extraction. SleepFM motivates the missing-input pattern, not the gait claim.

**C11. Simulator-teacher residual forecasting `[L2,L3,L4]`.** Question and bet: can a simulator-derived correction teacher remove impossible futures without erasing atypical but feasible gait? Freeze local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and PP-Motion `checkpoint_latest.pth`; train a zero-initialized future head with both the JEPA objective and a PP-Motion ranking loss. Retarget AMASS SMPL to the teacher's 60-frame input, apply GAVD-like projection noise during training, and score GAVD lift ensembles through the same branch. Evidence is a base-to-corrected residual map and typicality error conditioned on feasibility. Equality with a jerk penalty would show that the teacher adds no physical content. S0, smoothness matching, a shuffled teacher, and a frozen-base arm are required. `SimTeacher-72` runs three arms, three seeds, 25 epochs: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 x 3 = 6.75 H100-hours`.

**C12. Generative JEPA posterior `[L2,L3]`.** Question and bet: do local S-JEPA latents support multiple anatomically valid futures rather than one mean prediction? Freeze local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and train a low-rank mixture-density decoder on clean and projected AMASS. Condition it on noisy GAVD 2D and compare samples across lift hypotheses. Primary evidence is future coverage, anatomical calibration, and whether posterior shape carries source-held label information beyond mean error. Random-encoder or raw autoregression parity would show that the latent has no generative content. S0, speed matching, random encoders, and a unimodal decoder control easy gains. `Posterior-72` runs three arms, three seeds, 25 epochs: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 x 3 = 6.75 H100-hours`. S-JEPA itself does not decode future coordinates.

**C13. Intervention response fingerprint `[L1,L2]`.** Question and bet: does a normal-motion model's response to the same controlled perturbation reveal gait organization that passive surprise misses? Freeze `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt` and apply matched phase-specific edits to knee flexion, hip adduction, ankle dorsiflexion, and pelvis motion in AMASS and multi-lift GAVD. Measure how each edit propagates and relaxes during constrained rollout, then calibrate a compact response vector. Stable response differences would support a dynamic instrument; dependence only on edit size or lift would reject it. S0, equal-norm random edits, speed-preserving edits, opposite-direction edits, and lift consensus control the main shortcuts. `Response-72` evaluates four interventions and fits three 10-epoch calibrators: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus frozen inference. Unlike pathology-conditioned generation, no disease label enters the rollout.

**C14. Conditional coordination graph `[L1,L3]`.** Question and bet: does the pattern of which body regions predict one another measure gait coordination more directly than a pooled latent? Freeze local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and train one shared masked-region head that alternately hides pelvis, left and right arms, thighs, shanks, and feet. Learn on clean and corrupted AMASS, then compute directed conditional-prediction edges on GAVD. Evidence is graph repeatability and source-held label association, with an anatomical graph as output. Random masks producing the same graph would show that visibility, not coordination, is measured. S0, balanced masks, left-right swaps, confidence-matched joints, and a covariance graph baseline are required. `CoordGraph-72` trains three 25-epoch seeds: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`. This extends JEPA from feature extraction to a conditional dependency instrument.

**C15. Normal-manifold tangent decomposition `[L1,L2]`.** Question and bet: can motion variation that stays on a healthy manifold be separated from variation that leaves it? Freeze GaitEncoder `saved_models_final/vae_final.pth` and estimate local latent directions induced by speed, cadence, and stride changes in retargeted AMASS. Decompose each multi-lift GAVD residual into tangent and orthogonal components. Evidence is whether observational styles move mainly along the manifold while pathological categories retain stable orthogonal structure. Equality with Euclidean distance would show that the geometry adds nothing. S0, body-scale matching, synthetic tangent and off-manifold perturbations, phase shuffle, and raw Rajagopal distance are required. `Tangent-72` fits three 10-epoch local metric heads: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`. GaitEncoder reports latent distances, not this directional decomposition.

**C16. View-counterfactual score consistency `[L1]`.** Question and bet: can alternate-view rendering expose whether a gait score follows motion or camera? Freeze the public `Ryan241005/PoseAnything` 6B BF16 checkpoint and V-JEPA 2 `vitl.pt`. Reproject AMASS and GAVD lift hypotheses into canonical skeleton views, hold the first-frame identity fixed, render each view, and measure the variance of a shared future-difference head. A lower, label-preserving variance would support a view-robust measurement; larger AMASS control variance would show generator artifacts dominate. S0, identity swaps, fixed backgrounds, time-shuffled skeletons, and equal-magnitude camera changes are required. `ViewCF-72` renders a fixed 60-sequence panel and trains three 10-epoch heads: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus measured PoseAnything inference. The checkpoint remains frozen.

**C17. Repair-set degeneracy `[L1,L2]`.** Question and bet: does the number and diversity of equally plausible normalizing repairs provide better uncertainty than one minimum edit? Freeze `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt`, sample constrained repair sets for retargeted AMASS corruptions and GAVD lift ensembles, and summarize their anatomical dispersion. Evidence is whether dispersion predicts cross-lift instability and source-held characterization errors while C03 edit magnitude is held fixed. Diversity that changes only with sampler seed would reject the measurement. S0, fixed noise banks, known AMASS generating edits, equal-cost random search, and speed-preserving constraints are required. `RepairSet-72` samples 16 repairs per clip and fits three 10-epoch calibrators: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus frozen inference. This asks whether a counterfactual is identified, not merely whether one can be generated.

**C18. Phase-resolved transient localizer `[L1,L3]`.** Question and bet: do prediction-error peaks recur at consistent gait phases within a clip? Freeze local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`; fit a periodic phase head on AMASS, then transfer it through projected noise to GAVD. Evidence is within-video recurrence, left-right consistency, and source-held label association of phase-binned error. Failure would teach that the nearly empty GAVD gait-event field prevents credible event localization. S0, cadence matching, cyclic shifts, shuffled phase, and pose-confidence peaks are required. `Phase-72` trains three 25-epoch heads: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`. The output is descriptive timing, not a claimed clinical gait event.

**C19. Predictive-fingerprint discovery `[L1,L3]`.** Question and bet: do unsupervised clusters of error maps reveal recurrent motion phenotypes that cut across the coarse GAVD taxonomy? Freeze local S-JEPA and GaitDynamics checkpoints, form corruption-calibrated horizon, joint, and intervention summaries, and cluster without GAVD labels. AMASS provides the clean origin and projection-noise envelope. Evidence is cluster stability across source-held folds and enrichment that survives view and source controls. Clusters tracking source, background, or detector confidence would show that discovery is unsafe. S0, source-balanced resampling, label permutation, background swaps, and raw-feature clustering are required. `Discover-72` fits three 25-epoch projection heads: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`, plus frozen inference. Clinical names are not assigned to clusters.

**C20. Few-shot taxonomy from predictive summaries `[L3]`, likely cut.** Question and bet: do prediction-derived summaries reduce the number of observational GAVD labels needed compared with raw pose? Freeze local `seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and train capacity-matched heads at several source-video label fractions after AMASS projection-noise adaptation. Evidence is the source-held learning curve. Raw pose or random encoders matching the curve would reject transfer. S0, identical labeled sources, repeated group splits, and model-capacity matching are required. `FewShot-72` trains true and random heads for three 25-epoch seeds: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 2 x 3 = 4.50 H100-hours`. GaitForeMer, CARE-PD, and Aggregate, Don't Adapt already occupy the broad few-shot representation claim, so even a positive result would be incremental.

**C21. Qwen corruption-aware tool policy `[L5,L6]`, high risk.** Question and bet: can learned selective sensing beat an equal-cost fixed pipeline? Freeze `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct`, SAM 2.1, Depth Anything V2, and WHAM; train a small contextual-bandit policy over tool calls using clean AMASS 3D recovery error and tool cost as reward. Transfer the locked route to GAVD with full call traces and abstention reasons. Evidence is a selective-risk versus tool-cost curve against every fixed route and an all-tools ensemble. Equality with a pose-confidence decision tree would show that agency adds ceremony. S0, person-only crops, background swaps, random equal-budget routing, and hidden metadata are required. `Route-72` trains two policies for three 25-epoch seeds: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 2 x 3 = 4.50 H100-hours`; Qwen and tool throughput is measured separately.

**C22. Robot-trained masked-action transfer `[L1,L7]`, expected cut.** Question and bet: do robot-trained masked controls encode human response dynamics without gait adaptation? Freeze `masked_world_lora_high.safetensors` and `masked_world_lora_low.safetensors` on `PAI/Wan2.2-Fun-A14B-Control`; train only a small response score head. Render AMASS limb and synthetic-device masks, then use SAM-tracked masks on GAVD. Evidence is source-held control-response agreement. Failure would show embodiment-specific mask semantics. S0, static-copy tests, reversed controls, device swaps, and background swaps are required. `MVA-72` fits three 10-epoch score heads: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus measured frozen inference; reproducing MVA was `8 x 96 = 768 H200-hours`, unconverted. The conceptual collision and domain mismatch make this reject-first.

**C23. Robot action-latent transplant `[L2]`, expected cut.** Question and bet: do V-JEPA 2-AC action latents survive a mapper from Core11 motion changes? Freeze `vjepa2-ac-vitg.pt`; train only a Core11 action mapper and inverse head on perturbed, rendered AMASS, then apply the locked model to GAVD lifts. Evidence is forward-inverse residual versus raw finite differences. Failure would quantify embodiment specificity. S0, time-shuffled actions, random mappings, and held-out AMASS perturbations are required. `RobotLatent-72` uses three 50-epoch seeds: `1 x 3 x 50/100 x 3 = 4.50 H100-hours`. The candidate is cut on day 2 unless it beats raw deltas on held-out AMASS before seeing GAVD labels.

**C24. Pose-conditioned visual counterfactual `[L1]`, likely cut.** Question and bet: can paired rendering make a normalizing skeleton edit inspectable without changing appearance? Freeze `Ryan241005/PoseAnything`, V-JEPA 2 `vitl.pt`, and `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt`; train only a paired visual-difference head. Render observed and C03-repaired skeleton tracks from the same first GAVD frame after learning an artifact envelope on AMASS. Evidence is temporal consistency and source-held association of the paired difference. If equal-size AMASS edits or identity swaps create larger differences, the visualization is not identified. S0, fixed first frames, time shuffle, and equal-size control edits are required. `VisualCF-72` renders 60 sequences and fits three 10-epoch heads: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus frozen inference. Generator artifacts make this a visualization test, not a primary instrument.

**C25. Plain MDM surprise transfer `[L1]`, expected cut.** Question and bet: does published MDM diffusion surprise survive GAVD observation shift? Freeze `humanml-encoder-512-50steps`, map clean and corrupted AMASS to HumanML3D, and score WHAM-lifted GAVD with a small calibration head. Evidence is anatomical surprise after corruption calibration. Failure would be a useful replication boundary, but success would not create a new measurement. S0, empty-prompt normalization, shuffled checkpoint outputs, and raw motion are required. `Surprise-72` fits three 10-epoch calibrators: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus inference. Zero-shot Gait Classification with Diffusion Models already occupies the central claim.

**C26. Static GaitEncoder taxonomy probe, expected cut.** Question and bet: do laboratory gait latents retain any source-held GAVD association after an explicit camera bridge? Freeze `saved_models_final/vae_final.pth`, retarget and stride-normalize AMASS into its 32-channel Rajagopal input, estimate projection and automatic-phase error, then fit a linear GAVD head. Evidence is source-held label association and nearest exemplars. Raw kinematics parity would show that the latent is not an in-the-wild instrument. S0, cadence matching, phase scrambling, and a phase-quality-only model are required. `Static-72` trains three 25-epoch heads: `1 x 3 x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`. This lacks a predictive measurement and collides with GaitEncoder and CARE-PD.

**C27. PP-Motion scalar-only gait judge `[L2,L4]`, expected cut.** Question and bet: can a simulator-trained physical score alone characterize observed GAVD gait? Freeze `pp-motion_pretrained/checkpoint_latest.pth`, retarget AMASS and multi-lift GAVD into its 60-frame SMPL input, and fit a small calibration head. Evidence is score stability across lifts and source-held labels. Collinearity with jerk, pose confidence, or lift residual would show that the score is not a gait instrument. S0, matched smoothness, shuffled weights, and raw kinematic quality metrics are required. `ScalarPhysics-72` fits three 10-epoch calibrators: `1 x 3 x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`, plus inference. HumanScore, PhyMotion, and PP-Motion already establish scalar physical scoring, so disagreement or correction is needed for a stronger contribution.

## Open questions

- All 348 GAVD videos are confirmed decodable and cover all 1,874 sequences. A complete full-video pose manifest must still be created.
- Registered AMASS SMPL+H assets are available. Confirm the exact SMPL, SMPL-X, and SMPLify variants required by each lifting tool.
- PP-Motion code and its public checkpoint are accessible through the official repository, but no repository or weight license is stated.
- BABEL annotations are downloaded but not extracted. AMASS gait membership remains a filename-and-travel proxy until those frame labels are reconciled.
- GAVD does not support participant-held-out, severity, laterality, or diagnosis claims.
