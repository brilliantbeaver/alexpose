# Clinical-gait-first candidates

## Definitions and evaluation contract

A **world model** predicts how a system state changes through time or under an intervention. **Latent prediction** forecasts internal feature vectors rather than pixels or joint coordinates. **Inverse dynamics** infers the preceding control or motion change that could have produced an observed transition; here it never implies measured force. **SMPL** is a parametric 3D human body model whose pose and shape generate a mesh and joints. A **joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA)** predicts masked or future latent features from visible observations.

All primary tests use source-video-held-out GAVD splits because GAVD has no participant IDs. They report macro one-versus-rest average precision over the observed `gait_pat` labels with support in both sides of a fold, plus separate descriptive results for very-low-source labels such as `style` and `pregnant`. A train-fold-only, capacity-matched linear probe maps each proposed measurement vector to these labels; the scientific object remains its error, edit, disagreement, or abstention signal rather than the pooled embedding. No output is called a diagnosis. All 348 source videos are operator-confirmed as decodable on HAIC and cover all 1,874 sequences, but the full pose manifest still needs to be created. The 72-hour pilots use the audited 682-sequence AMASS lexical-and-travel intersection and a locked GAVD source subset extracted directly from video. Every bridge projects clean AMASS 3D through cameras, occlusion, scaling, and detector-like noise, then compares several lifts of noisy GAVD 2D rather than trusting one lift.

Compute arithmetic treats each adapted run as a single-H100 job at the conservative JEPA anchor. Independent arms and seeds may run in parallel, but GPU-hours are never divided by eight. Reported costs on other accelerator types remain unconverted.

GaitEncoder already occupies compact clinical latents, CARE-PD occupies multi-site SMPL transfer and frozen probes, and Aggregate, Don't Adapt occupies subject aggregation and test-time calibration. DiffuseGaitNet occupies disease-conditioned diffusion augmentation, GAITGen occupies pathology-conditioned generation and latent transfer, and Zero-shot Gait Classification with Diffusion Models occupies scalar diffusion surprise. The candidates below must instead expose predictive structure, a controlled edit, disagreement, or an abstention rule.

Lens tags abbreviate the requested lenses: `vision-physics`, `physical-grounding`, `S-JEPA`, `sim-corrected-loss`, `depth-on-motion`, `Qwen-RL-tools`, and `body-object`.

## C1. Horizon-resolved JEPA error atlas [advance]

**Question and bet:** Does the anatomy-by-horizon shape of failed prediction distinguish observed gait presentations after camera and detector effects are removed? I bet stable temporal error geometry carries information that a pooled embedding loses. **Start and adaptation:** freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and train a small heteroscedastic future-latent head; this is not another generic frozen probe. **Bridge:** project and corrupt AMASS SMPL/Core11 into noisy 2D, train the head on paired clean/noisy views, then ensemble WHAM and 2D-only GAVD paths. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision from error-map summaries, with joint-time map stability as the inspectable output. **Null and clinical meaning:** if trained, random-encoder, and raw-coordinate maps agree, prediction error is not a clinical instrument; a positive result localizes when and where motion departs from the normal prior and abstains when lifts disagree. **Shortcut/control:** pose confidence or camera view could draw the map, so match confidence, view, speed, duration, centroid drift, and box area and include confidence-only and random-JEPA controls. **72-hour experiment:** `Atlas-72` fits three 25-epoch seeds and tests horizon ordering on the local cache. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 3 seeds = 2.25 H100-hours`; seeds are independent single-GPU runs. **Lens tags:** `S-JEPA`, `vision-physics`.

## C2. Multi-lift minimum normalizing edit [advance]

**Question and bet:** Does the smallest normal-prior edit remain anatomically consistent across plausible monocular lifts, or does it merely repair the lifter? I bet edit consensus is more interpretable than the scalar surprise already shown with MDM. **Start and adaptation:** use downloadable `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt` and `GaitDynamicsRefinement.pt`, keep both frozen, perform constrained test-time inpainting, and fit a tiny edit-stability calibrator. **Bridge:** retarget clean AMASS SMPL to Rajagopal coordinates, project/corrupt it, learn the lift-to-Rajagopal error envelope, then apply the same WHAM-to-Rajagopal route to noisy GAVD 2D. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision of edit signatures and cross-lift edit agreement, with an overlaid joint-angle correction and abstention flag for review. **Null and clinical meaning:** unstable edits would show that normalizing counterfactuals are unidentifiable from one video; stable edits would describe model-proposed motion changes, not treatment advice. **Shortcut/control:** slow motion may require larger edits, so constrain path and cadence and compare speed-matched AMASS, phase-shuffled gait, and raw-2D residual controls. **72-hour experiment:** `Repair-72` runs three lifts on 40 sequences per supported label and calibrates stability. **Compute:** three 10-epoch calibrators cost `1 H100 x 3 h x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`; frozen GaitDynamics inference was reported on RTX 3050 and remains unconverted. **Prior boundary:** unlike GAITGen and DiffuseGaitNet, no impaired gait is generated for augmentation. **Lens tags:** `physical-grounding`, `vision-physics`.

## C3. Physical feasibility versus healthy typicality [advance]

**Question and bet:** Are physically implausible motion and clinically atypical motion separable axes in in-the-wild gait? I bet some GAVD presentations are feasible but atypical, while bad lifts are atypical and infeasible. **Start and adaptation:** freeze `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt` for typicality and `pp-motion_pretrained/checkpoint_latest.pth` for feasibility, then train a two-axis calibration head without changing either prior. **Bridge:** convert clean AMASS SMPL to both 60-frame PP-Motion input and GaitDynamics coordinates, project/corrupt/lift it to estimate method-induced disagreement, and process GAVD through the identical noisy-2D ensemble. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision from the two-dimensional score and conditional label profiles at fixed feasibility, with a clinician-facing scatterplot, motion overlay, and abstention region. **Null and clinical meaning:** complete score collinearity after corruption matching would show no separable measurement; separation would distinguish a likely perception failure from a motion that is feasible yet atypical. **Shortcut/control:** lift quality can fake both axes, so compare confidence-matched AMASS corruptions, randomized PP-Motion weights, and a pose-confidence-only two-axis control. **72-hour experiment:** `TwoAxes-72` estimates the AMASS corruption manifold and tests whether GAVD points leave it. **Compute:** three 25-epoch calibration heads cost `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 3 = 2.25 H100-hours`; PP-Motion hardware is unreported and is not converted. **Prior boundary:** this is disagreement, not GaitEncoder distance or zero-shot diffusion surprise. **Lens tags:** `physical-grounding`.

## C4. Forward-inverse motion consistency [advance]

**Question and bet:** Does disagreement between a future prediction and the motion change recovered backward from that future reveal presentation-specific instability? I bet cycle error detects locally inconsistent transitions even when forward error alone accepts them. **Start and adaptation:** freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`; attach matched zero-initialized forward and inverse residual heads that predict latent joint changes, never forces. **Bridge:** train both heads on clean AMASS windows and their camera-projected, occluded, jittered 2D twins, then run paired 2D and WHAM-lifted GAVD paths. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision of joint-time cycle maps and the fraction of windows that pass a predeclared consistency gate; outputs are recovered motion arrows or abstention. **Null and clinical meaning:** if backward recovery adds nothing beyond forward residual or finite differences, cycle consistency is not a useful gait measurement; if localized and stable, it marks transitions the normal dynamics cannot explain consistently. **Shortcut/control:** walking speed and dropped joints can inflate both directions, so use speed/visibility-matched windows, time-reversed controls, a forward-only arm, and a random-head arm. **72-hour experiment:** `Cycle-72` trains both directions for 25 epochs over three seeds and tests the consistency gate. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 2 heads x 3 seeds = 4.50 H100-hours`; all are single-GPU jobs. **Lens tags:** `S-JEPA`, `vision-physics`.

## C5. Missing-modality set predictor [advance]

**Question and bet:** Can a fixed normal-motion representation retain calibrated evidence when 2D joints, lifted 3D, depth, or silhouettes disappear? I bet leave-one-modality-out alignment turns detector failure into an explicit availability state rather than false zero motion. **Start and adaptation:** freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`, `wham_vit_w_3dpw.pth.tar`, `Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf`, and `sam2.1_hiera_large.pt`; train only a set-pooling adapter with availability masks and a small task head. **Bridge:** render clean AMASS SMPL into paired 2D, relative depth, silhouette, and 3D streams with controlled dropout, then feed noisy GAVD streams through the same encoders and retain cross-lift uncertainty. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision and selective risk under every modality subset, with per-modality evidence and abstention shown to the reviewer. **Null and clinical meaning:** if worst-subset risk matches complete-case training or a validity-only baseline, alignment is unnecessary; otherwise the model states which surviving measurements support its characterization. **Shortcut/control:** class-correlated missingness can become the answer, so apply identical missingness masks across labels and compare mask-only, modality-shuffled, and complete-only controls. **72-hour experiment:** `DropSet-72` trains a 25-epoch adapter and a capacity-matched no-dropout arm for three seeds. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 2 arms x 3 seeds = 4.50 H100-hours`; frozen tool inference is benchmarked separately. **Prior boundary:** it adapts SleepFM's missing-input pattern, not CARE-PD's generic frozen-probe result. **Lens tags:** `depth-on-motion`, `S-JEPA`.

## C6. Cross-prior disagreement as abstention [advance]

**Question and bet:** Does disagreement among independently trained normal-motion priors identify cases where a gait characterization should be withheld? I bet disagreement ranks source-shift errors better than detector confidence alone. **Start and adaptation:** freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`, `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt`, and MDM `humanml-encoder-512-50steps`; fit only a split-conformal disagreement calibrator. **Bridge:** map AMASS clean 3D into each prior, project/corrupt/lift it to calibrate coordinate-system disagreement, and apply the same three routes to GAVD noisy 2D. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out multiclass risk-coverage area for `gait_pat`, with macro average precision reported at fixed coverage and a three-prior evidence panel for every accepted case. **Null and clinical meaning:** if disagreement fails to rank errors, these priors share the same blind spot; if it succeeds, the output is a defensible abstention mechanism rather than an unsupported label. **Shortcut/control:** low pose confidence may drive all disagreement, so compare a confidence-only conformal rule and evaluate within matched confidence, view, and occlusion strata. **72-hour experiment:** `Abstain-72` calibrates on held-out AMASS corruptions and one GAVD source fold, then locks thresholds before the remaining folds. **Compute:** three 10-epoch calibrator seeds cost `1 H100 x 3 h x 10/100 x 3 = 0.90 H100-hours`; foundation inference is frozen. **Prior boundary:** this is neither Aggregate, Don't Adapt subject aggregation, which GAVD cannot support, nor scalar MDM surprise. **Lens tags:** `vision-physics`, `physical-grounding`.

## C7. Depth residual on a motion embedding [advance]

**Question and bet:** Can a motion latent predict view-relative joint depth well enough that its residual separates dynamics from monocular camera ambiguity? I bet temporal context resolves some depth flips, but the model should abstain when WHAM and image depth disagree. **Start and adaptation:** freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt`, add a zero-initialized joint-depth distribution head, and use frozen `Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf` plus `wham_vit_w_3dpw.pth.tar` only as target-domain witnesses. **Bridge:** project clean AMASS SMPL under sampled cameras to exact relative depths, corrupt its 2D joints during head training, then compare predicted depth with both witnesses on noisy GAVD 2D. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision of depth-residual maps and calibrated interval coverage, with per-joint depth bands and an abstention flag. **Null and clinical meaning:** a view-dependent or witness-dependent residual would show that depth adds no stable clinical evidence; stable residuals could identify where the observed motion contradicts the depth expected from its recent trajectory. **Shortcut/control:** camera view and body scale are dominant risks, so normalize per-frame affine depth, match camera strata, and include shuffled-depth, single-frame-depth, and raw-2D controls. **72-hour experiment:** `DepthResidual-72` trains the true and shuffled branches for 25 epochs over three seeds. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 2 arms x 3 seeds = 4.50 H100-hours`; frozen depth and WHAM runtime is measured directly. **Lens tags:** `depth-on-motion`, `S-JEPA`.

## C8. Body-device relational prediction [advance, high risk]

**Question and bet:** Does predicting the relative motion of body and assistive object reveal information beyond merely seeing the object? I bet contact timing and relative trajectories matter, especially where a body-only prior fails. **Start and adaptation:** freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and `sam2.1_hiera_large.pt`; add a zero-initialized object-token branch and joint-object predictor. **Bridge:** attach simple hand-ground device kinematics to clean AMASS SMPL, project and corrupt body plus device tracks together, then derive endpoints from prompted SAM tracks beside noisy GAVD 2D poses. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision and joint-object prediction maps, with special descriptive reporting for `prosthetic` but no binary claim; the output shows predicted versus observed contact and can abstain. **Null and clinical meaning:** if shuffled trajectories or device presence alone match the predictor, the relational model has no clinical content and should be cut; a positive result would characterize coordination with an observed aid, not diagnose device use. **Shortcut/control:** object presence, background, and clinic setting may reveal the label, so compare presence-only masks, trajectory-shuffled tracks, body-only prediction, and source-matched crops. **72-hour experiment:** `DeviceRelation-72` tests synthetic recovery and visually checks automatic tracks on the local cache before any label result. **Compute:** three 25-epoch arms across three seeds cost `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 3 arms x 3 seeds = 6.75 H100-hours`; SAM inference is frozen. **Lens tags:** `body-object`, `S-JEPA`.

## C9. Qwen corruption-aware tool policy [test, likely cut]

**Question and bet:** Can an agent learn when to call 2D pose, depth, segmentation, or lifting tools and when to abstain, rather than always trusting one pipeline? I bet known AMASS reconstruction error supplies enough reward for tool selection without clinical reward shaping. **Start and adaptation:** freeze `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct`, `Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf`, `sam2.1_hiera_large.pt`, and `wham_vit_w_3dpw.pth.tar`; train a compact contextual-bandit policy head with tool cost and reconstruction rewards. **Bridge:** render and corrupt AMASS clean 3D, let the agent select tools, score recovery against known 3D, and transfer the learned routing policy to noisy GAVD video with lift disagreement exposed. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` selective macro average precision and risk-coverage against fixed single-tool and fixed-ensemble routes, with the tool trace and abstention reason visible. **Null and clinical meaning:** equality with a fixed route would show that orchestration adds ceremony rather than measurement; improvement would offer auditable acquisition choices, not autonomous diagnosis. **Shortcut/control:** background and label-correlated tool failure may drive routing, so use person-only crops, background swaps, identical tool budgets, and a pose-confidence decision tree. **72-hour experiment:** `RouteRL-72` learns two 25-epoch policies over three seeds on synthetic corruptions and freezes them before GAVD. **Compute:** screening arithmetic is `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 2 policies x 3 seeds = 4.50 H100-hours`; Qwen throughput is unknown and measured separately, not inferred from JEPA. **Lens tags:** `Qwen-RL-tools`, `depth-on-motion`.

## C10. Pose-conditioned visual counterfactual [test, likely cut]

**Question and bet:** Does rendering the same person under observed versus normal-prior-repaired skeletons expose clinically relevant motion differences without changing appearance? I bet paired rendering can aid inspection, but generator artifacts may dominate. **Start and adaptation:** freeze the downloadable `Ryan241005/PoseAnything` 6B BF16 checkpoint, V-JEPA 2 `vitl.pt`, and `GaitDynamicsDiffusion.pt`; train only a paired visual-difference head. **Bridge:** project clean AMASS SMPL to 2D skeleton controls, add GAVD-like dropout, learn generator-artifact baselines, then condition on the first GAVD frame with observed and GaitDynamics-repaired noisy-2D tracks. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision of paired V-JEPA differences plus counterfactual temporal consistency; reviewers receive side-by-side clips and an abstention when identity or limb consistency fails. **Null and clinical meaning:** if AMASS control changes produce larger residuals than repaired-versus-observed GAVD, visual counterfactuals are not identifiable and the candidate is cut; otherwise they visualize the model's proposed motion edit. **Shortcut/control:** background copying and skeleton-render fidelity can fake the result, so hold the first frame fixed and compare time-shuffled, identity-swapped, and equal-magnitude AMASS edits. **72-hour experiment:** `VisualCF-72` generates a fixed 60-sequence panel and fits three 10-epoch heads. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 10/100 x 3 seeds = 0.90 H100-hours`; frozen PoseAnything inference is timed rather than estimated. **Prior boundary:** unlike GAITGen, this neither learns pathology codes nor synthesizes training labels. **Lens tags:** `vision-physics`, `S-JEPA`.

## C11. Plain MDM diffusion surprise transfer [expected cut]

**Question and bet:** Does the published MDM surprise signal survive in-the-wild camera and detector shift across the observed GAVD taxonomy? The weak bet is that anatomical channel patterns transfer even if the scalar does not. **Start and adaptation:** freeze MDM `humanml-encoder-512-50steps` and fit only a ten-epoch calibration head. **Bridge:** convert AMASS clean 3D to HumanML3D, project/corrupt/lift it to calibrate surprise caused by observation noise, then process noisy GAVD 2D through WHAM and the same converter. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision of channel-wise surprise maps, with anatomical maps and abstention under lift disagreement. **Null and clinical meaning:** failure after corruption calibration would show that the reported clinical correlation does not generalize to this setting; success would still be replication evidence, not a new measurement. **Shortcut/control:** speed, pose confidence, and view can inflate diffusion loss, so use matched strata, empty-prompt normalization, raw-motion controls, and shuffled checkpoint outputs. **72-hour experiment:** `SurpriseTransfer-72` runs frozen scoring and three 10-epoch calibrators. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 10/100 x 3 seeds = 0.90 H100-hours`; MDM inference is frozen. **Cut reason:** zero-shot diffusion surprise already occupies the claim, and its private preprocessing blocks exact replication. **Lens tags:** `vision-physics`.

## C12. Static GaitEncoder taxonomy probe [expected cut]

**Question and bet:** Do public laboratory gait latents retain any source-held-out association with the observational GAVD taxonomy after an explicit camera bridge? The conservative bet is that they will expose the bridge's limit more clearly than they characterize gait. **Start and adaptation:** freeze `saved_models_final/vae_final.pth` and train a linear head only. **Bridge:** retarget and stride-normalize clean AMASS SMPL to the 32-channel, 24-point Rajagopal input, project/corrupt/lift it to quantify phase error, and apply the same automatic phase route to noisy GAVD 2D because `gait_event` is almost empty. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision with nearest-latent exemplars and abstention when phase or lift uncertainty exceeds the AMASS envelope. **Null and clinical meaning:** parity with raw kinematics would show that this laboratory latent is not an in-the-wild instrument; any association would only support it as a baseline representation. **Shortcut/control:** cadence and auto-phase quality may dominate, so match cadence, scramble phase within cycles, and compare raw Rajagopal features plus a phase-quality-only model. **72-hour experiment:** `StaticProbe-72` fits three 25-epoch heads and audits every accepted exemplar. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 3 seeds = 2.25 H100-hours`; the encoder remains frozen. **Cut reason:** GaitEncoder, CARE-PD, and Aggregate, Don't Adapt already occupy frozen clinical probing and adaptation, while this candidate lacks a predictive measurement. **Lens tags:** control candidate only.

## C13. Simulator-teacher residual forecasting [advance]

**Question and bet:** Does a simulator-derived correction teacher improve a normal-motion forecast without erasing atypical but physically feasible gait? I bet a small residual route can remove impossible futures while leaving typicality error available as a separate signal. **Start and adaptation:** freeze local `outputs/repaired-jepa-seed7-v2/seed-7_standard_sjepa_best.pt` and `pp-motion_pretrained/checkpoint_latest.pth`; train a zero-initialized future head with the JEPA latent objective plus a PP-Motion ranking loss. **Bridge:** retarget clean AMASS SMPL to PP-Motion's 60-frame format, project/corrupt it to noisy 2D during training, then score ensemble lifts of GAVD through the same branch. **Primary measurement:** source-video-held-out `gait_pat` macro average precision of the base-to-corrected residual map and feasibility-conditioned error, with paired forecasts and abstention when the teacher and lift disagree. **Null and clinical meaning:** equality with a simple smoothness penalty would show that simulator teaching adds no physical content; a useful split would mark perception or feasibility failure separately from atypical motion. **Shortcut/control:** the teacher may only suppress velocity and jerk, so match output smoothness and compare equal-capacity jerk-loss, shuffled-teacher, and frozen-base arms. **72-hour experiment:** `SimTeacher-72` trains the three 25-epoch arms over three seeds and locks the PP-Motion weight on AMASS validation only. **Compute:** `1 H100 x 3 h x 25/100 x 3 arms x 3 seeds = 6.75 H100-hours`; PP-Motion's original hardware is unreported and remains unconverted. **Prior boundary:** this corrects a predictive loss rather than using DiffuseGaitNet or GAITGen to synthesize labeled impairment. **Lens tags:** `sim-corrected-loss`, `S-JEPA`.
