# Image ownership

Existing root and `long-term`/`short-term` images belong to the historical
fixed-reflection material until migrated. New shared diagrams belong in
`shared/`; new semantic-gauge diagrams belong in `semantic-gauge/`; new
biomechanics-validation diagrams belong in `biomechanics-validation/`.

Do not reuse a fixed-reflection diagram to illustrate a latent semantic gauge:
the latter must visibly distinguish sensor-frame convention, anatomical side,
and unresolved sign orbit.
