# Data scope for Study 02

AMASS supplies clean skeletons and exact synthetic gauge labels. It is the
controlled identifiability benchmark, not proof that real laterality errors
occur.

GAVD supplies monocular pose transfer and is the natural-ambiguity audit. Its
source video, not sequence or window, is the independent unit. It cannot
validate participant-level force, balance, diagnosis, or prognosis.

Biomechanical validation requires a public, readily downloadable cohort with
participant IDs and independently measured quantities. Before it is named as a
paper dataset, record its download terms, side-label provenance, force/contact
definition, stability computation, exclusions, and participant split. The
cohort validates the representation's usefulness; it does not create the
representation claim.
