# Biomechanics-validation protocol

Use participant-held-out evaluation. Keep all trials, cycles, visits,
conditions, views, and reflected copies from one participant in the same outer
split. Fit preprocessing, gauge anchors, calibration, and readouts inside the
outer-training participants only.

For an odd target, document its physical side labels and report signed error,
sign accuracy, and unanchored orbit coverage separately. For an even stability
or balance outcome, use the invariant state and report that it cannot validate
the signed-laterality mechanism by itself.

Force reliability, contact attribution, quality exclusions, label availability,
and participant-level aggregation are eligibility gates. Any clinical statement
remains out of scope unless a dataset and protocol directly support it.
