# Adversarial novelty audit

The study must not claim novelty for its ingredients. The prospective novelty
is their semantic-gauge/identifiability combination.

| Existing work class | What it already establishes | What Study 02 must add |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [S-JEPA](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/html/4755_ECCV_2024_paper.php) | masked latent prediction for skeletons | gauge-marginal target transport and semantic identifiability |

be reframed or stopped. A literature update is required before submission.
