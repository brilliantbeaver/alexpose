# Literature and public-access ledger

Checked 12 September 2026. We used primary papers, author pages and official repositories. Search aggregators were discovery aids, not evidence for scientific claims. An official released weight link, a successful file response and a successful local inference run are different states. No checkpoint was installed or executed for this portfolio.

## The requested inspiration papers

| Source | Reading performed | What changes our plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GoalForce](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05848v1), [code](https://github.com/brown-palm/goal-force) | Full paper and official release | A structured condition can create a new use for a frozen generator. The reported adaptation is under 48 hours on four A100s. Relative-force outputs do not validate gait forces. |
| [Masked Visual Actions](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19343v1), [code](https://github.com/HadiZayer/masked-visual-actions) | Full paper, training recipe and weight listing | Masking one entity's motion creates forward and inverse queries. Fifteen hours is the robot-data quantity; training took four days on eight H200s with rank-256 LoRA. Small data does not imply small compute. |
| [ControlNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05543), [code](https://github.com/lllyasviel/ControlNet) | Full paper and official code | A frozen base plus a zero-initialized conditioning route provides a useful adaptation pattern and an exact initialization control. Applying that pattern alone is not the contribution. |
| [S-JEPA](https://sjepa.github.io/), [ECCV paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/04755.pdf) | Full published paper and project page | Skeleton latent prediction is an economical student architecture. Masked prediction is not automatically causal forecasting. No verified official author checkpoint was found. |
| [SleepFM](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04133-4), [accessible full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12920147/) | Full final article through PMC | Reuse rich pretrained representations with small task heads. Its clinical scope depends on linked outcomes, which GAVD does not supply. |
| [GaitDynamics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-025-01565-8), [author preprint](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-6206222/v1_covered_bd1a4fd8-bd9f-402b-a1ea-d951837522b9.pdf?c=1767687457) | Final abstract, figures and availability; full public preprint for methods | A flexible gait prior is publicly available. Its released Rajagopal representation excludes arms and cannot accept AMASS joints by substitution. Final full text was subscription restricted. |
| [GAVD](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.04190v1), [data release](https://github.com/Rahmyyy/GAVD) | Full paper and release, plus local manifests | Diverse observed gait presentations make a useful real-video stress test. Sequence totals are not independent-person counts, and presentation labels are not measured clinical outcomes. |
| [GaitForeMer](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.00106), [code](https://github.com/markendo/GaitForeMer) | Full paper and official repository | Forecast-pretrained gait assessment already exists. Its clinical data are private and the old weight link remains unverified. It is a comparator in the literature, not a critical dependency. |

The design principle we retain is **large prior, small new interface, independently tested new capability**. We do not copy the training scale or assume that robot action semantics transfer to gait.

S-JEPA author weights, GaitForeMer weights, FoundationGait's forthcoming weights and UMO's forthcoming code/models are **not required dependencies**. A compatible local S-JEPA checkpoint was not verified in this review. The contribution cannot depend on an unreleased checkpoint appearing this week.

## Search and reading boundaries

The review ran separate streams for world-model adaptation, gait models/data, and local evidence with distillation novelty. Targeted searches then attacked the surviving ideas: reconstruction hallucination, ambiguity and motion recovery, teacher utility, distributional privileged learning, cross-activity personalization and constrained generation. The agents exchanged collisions and revised the shortlist before drafting.

The user's optical-flow addition triggered a second parallel search across motion representations, correspondence quality, pretrained estimators and track-based world models. It changed P1's concrete measurement branch and replaced the weakest proposal with the current P7. The [optical-flow synthesis](optical-flow.md) records this final decision.

Several official pages were protected, timed out or exposed only a file listing. We recorded those cases rather than treating a paper's promise as a verified download. A runtime load check remains necessary even for an openly released artifact. Dataset/model terms are recorded in the detailed memos where they affect redistribution; the proposed deliverable should release code, split identifiers and measurements rather than promise redistribution of restricted source motion.
