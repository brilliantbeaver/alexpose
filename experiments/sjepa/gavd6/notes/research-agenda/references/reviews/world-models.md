# World-model evidence and candidate audit

Research memo, 12 September 2026. This is a bounded literature and artifact review, not an experimental result. The review read the earlier `brainstorm/lit` memos, proposal portfolios 02 and 03, and current primary papers and official repositories. Public web pages establish release availability; no checkpoint was downloaded or run in this review.

## What the inspiration papers actually support

**Goal Force.** A frozen Wan2.2 image-to-video model receives a trainable control branch. Separate input channels distinguish an applied force from a desired later force, so the same model can infer an antecedent event or predict an outcome. The paper reports a branch copied from ten DiT blocks, 3,000 steps, effective batch four, and under 48 hours on four 80 GB A100s. The task uses relative force and mass scales, not calibrated physical measurements. Its evaluation filters visually degraded samples before calculating some planning results. The transferable insight is the cause versus goal interface; the model does not validate gait mechanics or hidden clinical forces. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05848v1), [official code](https://github.com/brown-palm/goal-force).

**Masked Visual Actions.** The input reveals the motion of one scene entity and asks the model to complete the others. Robot-motion conditioning gives forward prediction; object-motion conditioning can produce inverse behavior even though training reveals robot motion. The reported 15 hours refers to the data, not GPU training: the paper uses rank-256 LoRA, eight H200s, batch four, and approximately 10,000 steps over four days. It reports optimistic imagined task progress and explicitly distinguishes learned interaction correlations from causal relationships. The transferable insight is that a well-aligned interface can change the uses of a frozen backbone; the released robot adapter is not evidence of zero-shot gait competence. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19343v1), [official implementation](https://github.com/HadiZayer/masked-visual-actions).

**ControlNet.** Freeze the original generator, add a trainable conditioning branch, and initialize its output connections at zero. This provides an exact base-model control at initialization. The architecture is an adaptation mechanism, not a scientific contribution when merely reapplied to skeletons. Its image checkpoints cannot be inserted unchanged into a Wan video model. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.05543v3), [official code](https://github.com/lllyasviel/ControlNet).

**Wan2.2.** Official code exposes A14B text and image models, a smaller TI2V-5B model, and Animate-14B. A14B has two experts with approximately 14 billion active parameters per diffusion step. The official repository supplies inference and links to training support in DiffSynth-Studio. This establishes access and plausible inference fit, not a measured H100 training budget for this project. Animate takes an input video and reference character image, so it is an appearance-changing renderer rather than a source of independent motion truth. [Official repository](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2), [Animate model card](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-Animate-14B).

## New work that materially changes novelty claims

1. **CAER**, posted 31 August 2026, identifies action-sensitive tokens by contrasting a world model's conditioned and unconditioned predictions, then reweights the training loss. A proposal based only on highlighting predicted motion effects is already occupied. Any teacher-response proposal must show a different scientific object and compare against action-effect weighting. [Primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30897), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30897v1).
2. **Semigroup-JEPA**, posted 9 September 2026, jointly trains a latent encoder and recursively rolled-out predictor with gravity conditioning. Plain multi-step consistency or physical-parameter conditioning is therefore not a new research direction. Its tests concern supplied gravity in simulated systems, not preservation of unusual measured human movement. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10464v1), [official code and checkpoints](https://github.com/sg-jepa/sg-jepa).
3. **Principia**, posted 3 September 2026, uses calibration-independent relationships between paired objects to test video-model physics. A generic relational physics benchmark is no longer an unoccupied niche. A gait proposal would need a distinct, defensible observation model and a decision beyond another physical-consistency score. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04200).
4. **PhyMotion** recovers SMPL trajectories, retargets them into MuJoCo, and combines kinematic, contact/balance, and dynamics rewards for video-generator post-training. Replacing a VLM judge with a skeleton physics scorer is already close prior work. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14269v1).
5. **LikePhys** already evaluates video diffusion models through denoising-loss preferences over matched valid and invalid videos. A denoising-loss gait plausibility score is insufficient novelty. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11512), [author project](https://yuanjianhao508.github.io/LikePhys/).
6. **Articulated Kinematics Distillation** already uses a video diffusion model to optimize skeleton-based motion through score distillation. Using Wan as a motion prior is a starting point, not the contribution. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01204), [author project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dir/akd/).
7. **Knowledge Transfer with Jacobian Matching** establishes derivative matching as a form of knowledge transfer, with connections to input-noise distillation. Teacher derivatives are not a new loss family. [ICML 2018 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/srinivas18a.html).
8. **Decoupled Self-Forcing Distillation**, posted 9 September 2026, motivates low-dimensional motion generation because full video latents contain unrelated identity and appearance. It uses a frozen renderer and video teacher to supervise motion. Our global-feature-transfer critique therefore needs a concrete new estimator, identifiable target, and measured student benefit. [Primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10317).

## Candidate A: preserve evidence while removing noise

**Research question.** Can a small adaptation of a frozen motion prior remove tracking error without suppressing unusual movement that was actually present? Demonstrate a better preservation versus denoising frontier in 72 hours, then replicate it across motion sources and corruptions by day seven.

**First principles.** An unusual arm-leg relationship can be real. A noisy ankle coordinate can be false. A prior trained on common movements may treat both as unlikely and move both toward a common gait. A model can consequently improve average joint-position error while damaging the movement attribute a downstream scientist wanted to measure. This is a testable failure mode, not an assumption that every prior necessarily erases evidence.

**Why this is connected to the repo.** The retained analyses show that strong pretrained representations can lose useful movement readout performance even when source-sensitive latent prediction looks successful. They do not establish that a motion denoiser erases unusual coordination. Candidate A turns that concern into an experiment with known clean motion rather than another binary GAVD classifier.

**Experimental construction.** Use whole-body AMASS trajectories. Before any corruption, define unusual but observed coordination relative to training-only distributions, with speed, duration, body scale and camera held matched. Use at least two independent movement attributes, for example arm-leg phase relationships and trunk-pelvis relative rotation. Compare real recorded tails of these attributes, not only artificial pathological-looking edits. Generate corruptions independently of rarity, including low-frequency tracking drift whose spectrum overlaps the true motion. Keep subject and motion-source splits before generating variants. Use GAVD only as a supplementary audit of visible movement preservation and real tracking failures. No force, diagnosis or missing-motion ground truth is inferred from its class labels.

**Methods worth testing.** Start with an available motion prior, such as GaitDynamics if its input contract is satisfied, and RoHM when its assets load. Add a small reliability-conditioned residual head that chooses which correction directions to retain. Learn reliability from independent corruption replicas and observed support, while making the normality score an explicit competing signal. A simple data-consistency projection is a mandatory baseline, not a claimed invention. Frozen S-JEPA can provide an additional temporal consistency feature only if it helps over coordinates and uncertainty alone.

**Primary decision object.** At a fixed denoising improvement, measure signed attenuation of the true movement deviation, with clean-motion error and corrupted-input error both reported. Show the full Pareto frontier as the strength of prior correction varies. Score rare and common true motions under identical corruptions. Report error against the original clean 3D motion, so merely copying a noisy observation cannot win.

**Closest prior work and novelty condition.** RoHM already reconstructs noisy and occluded motion while matching image evidence. Denoising Diffusion Null-Space Models already preserve measurements in inverse problems. The new contribution must be a transferable distinction between measurement reliability and movement typicality, plus a correction method that improves their tradeoff across priors. A motion-specific confidence gate that merely reproduces a tuned projection is incremental. [RoHM paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Zhang_RoHM_Robust_Human_Motion_Reconstruction_via_Diffusion_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf), [DDNM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00490).

**Kill experiment.** On a training-independent AMASS pilot, compare raw input, smoothing, a learned small denoiser, frozen prior, prior with tuned observation projection, and the proposed adapter across the same corruption levels. Stop the method claim if projection matches the frontier or no reproducible erasure exists. Continue an audit paper only if the failure is substantial, occurs across at least two prior families, and changes a meaningful held-out measurement. Synthetic edit detection alone is not enough.

**Budget discipline.** First use a few hundred motions and cached prior outputs, with one GPU per independent baseline. No large backbone training. Expand only after measuring inference throughput and observing the failure. A reasonable planning cap is 128 H100-hours for the first 72-hour phase, not a claim that this workload has already been benchmarked.

**Assessment.** Highest-value candidate in this memo because its main question survives either a positive or negative answer, and clean AMASS makes falsification possible. ICLR quality still requires a general mechanism and a nontrivial corrective method or broad counterexample, not a small gait-specific audit.

## Candidate B: transfer how a teacher responds to motion changes

**Research question.** Can a skeleton student learn a useful response operator from a frozen video encoder even when absolute teacher features are dominated by appearance? Within 72 hours, test whether controlled motion differences carry stable information across renderers; train a student only if they do.

**Plain explanation.** Instead of asking a student to reproduce everything the video teacher knows about a clip, show the teacher two clips with the same person and scene but a controlled difference in movement. Transfer the change in its features. Repeating this across appearances could reveal which responses belong to movement rather than clothing or scenery.

**Concrete method.** Render recorded whole-body AMASS motions under controlled appearances. Use local motion substitutions or bounded edits that preserve body lengths and visible support. Compute teacher feature differences with fixed rendering nuisance, then compare those differences across new appearances. Train a small S-JEPA adapter on the response vectors. All paired variants of one original motion stay in the same split. Use a real future-motion task on unseen original trajectories as the primary endpoint; edit-parameter recovery is only an interface diagnostic.

**Novelty restriction.** This overlaps old dose-axis and future-innovation proposals, classical Jacobian matching, and recent CAER. The defensible new object would be a response operator stable across rendering nuisance and predictive of actual student transfer, not merely finite-difference feature matching. Compare full-feature KD, residual-feature KD, classical Jacobian or noisy-input KD, direct motion supervision, and action-effect weighting. If none of these distinctions produces a clear empirical separation, remove this proposal from the final seven.

**Kill experiment.** Before student training, ask whether teacher differences caused by motion remain more consistent across appearances than differences caused by camera or shading. Use nuisance-matched negative pairs and motion-matched positive pairs. If a simple optical-flow or joint-angle descriptor wins, stop. If teacher differences are unstable, a larger student cannot fix the teacher target.

**Assessment.** Feasible and connected to the latest run, but less novel than Candidate A. It should be a conditional reserve, not a leading bet presented as an untouched idea.

## Alternatives searched and rejected

- **Asynchronous RGB-pose registration.** Direct overlap with [Learning by Aligning 2D Skeleton Sequences and Multi-Modality Fusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19480) and [MoBind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19004). Periodicity and cross-correlation are strong cheap alternatives. Do not use this to fill the seven-proposal quota.
- **Plain physical plausibility scoring.** Overlaps LikePhys, PhyMotion, Principia and the first proposal generation's physics-versus-typicality idea.
- **A skeleton-conditioned Wan generator.** It copies the adaptation mechanism in MVA and ControlNet without supplying a new scientific question. Generation quality is a weak surrogate for faithful gait measurement.
- **Semigroup-consistent S-JEPA.** Recent SG-JEPA occupies the broad idea. Merely moving the same rollout loss to gait is not the conceptual leap requested.
- **Inverse whole-body completion from desired foot trajectories.** Human motion diffusion composition and joint constraints are established. A new prompt interface alone needs a downstream decision with independent truth to meet the requested quality bar. [Human Motion Diffusion as a Generative Prior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01418).

## Specific revision advice to the portfolio lead

Use Candidate A as a falsifiable scientific problem, not a claim that clinical abnormalities are noise. Make model-prior influence and sensor reliability separate axes. Do not call a pointwise projection a new theorem. Do not use generated videos as independent ground truth. Include a clean-motion endpoint and matched data-consistency baseline early enough that the team can stop by day three. Keep Candidate B only if the final portfolio needs a methodological reserve and clearly marks its substantial overlap with existing work.
