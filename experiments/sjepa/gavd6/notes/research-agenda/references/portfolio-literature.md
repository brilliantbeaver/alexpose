# Sources, usable models, and novelty boundaries

> **Scope: September 14 seven-proposal portfolio (P1–P7).** Additional prior work for the later direction is in the [synthetic-training proposal](../proposals/synthetic-training-selection.md) and [its independent review](../reviews/synthetic-teaching.md).

Reviewed 14 September 2026 using primary papers and official repositories. This is a targeted novelty review, not a guarantee that no related work exists. The research proposals are hypotheses. A link resolving is not a successful binary download or an inference test.

**The required inspiration papers suggest a strategy, not a promised result.**

| Work | Lesson used here | What does not transfer automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Goal Force](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05848v1), [official code](https://github.com/brown-palm/goal-force) | A carefully chosen conditioning interface can repurpose a large pretrained model with limited adaptation. | Its relative force interface and reported robot/object demonstrations are not measured human kinetics. It reports a smaller adaptation run, but our data and objective differ. |
| [Masked Visual Actions](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19343v1), [official code](https://github.com/HadiZayer/masked-visual-actions) | Choose an input/output interface that makes a useful capability measurable. | Fifteen hours is the dataset size. Reported adaptation used eight H200s for four days. A small dataset does not establish a tiny compute budget or human causal prediction. |
| [ControlNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05543), [code](https://github.com/lllyasviel/ControlNet) | Keep the large base fixed and train a small conditioning path with a controlled initial effect. | A conditioning branch is an established technique, not the proposed novelty. |
| [S-JEPA](https://sjepa.github.io/), [ECCV paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/04755.pdf) | Predict hidden skeleton representations without requiring action labels. | Action-recognition representations are not automatically sufficient predictive states. Official author weights were not verified. |
| [SleepFM](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04133-4), [official code](https://github.com/zou-group/sleepfm-clinical) | Frozen representations plus a small head can support meaningful outcomes when the data actually contain them. | Its clinical endpoints depend on linked longitudinal records. GAVD provides no equivalent labels. |
| [GaitDynamics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-025-01565-8), [official code](https://github.com/stanfordnmbl/GaitDynamics) | Pretrained motion distributions can be reused for measurement tasks. | Its OpenSim model differs from SMPL/Core11. Public weights do not establish compatible input or force truth here. |
| [GAVD](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.04190v1), [dataset code](https://github.com/Rahmyyy/GAVD) | Real, diverse gait videos provide a necessary external observational test. | Its label scheme and abnormal-only test subset do not support a claim that balanced external diagnosis is solved. Local IDs identify recordings, not verified people. |
| [GaitForeMer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00106), [official code](https://github.com/markendo/GaitForeMer) | Forecasting can support subsequent gait analysis. | Forecast-pretraining plus severity estimation is already known. The PD data are nonpublic; the checkpoint link was not verified downloadable in this review. |

**The core model choices are supported by actual availability.**

| Asset | Verified access and intended role | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [V-JEPA 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985), [V-JEPA 2.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14482), [official model table](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2#models) | Official public checkpoints and builders; the user has local weights. Existing repository work names `vjepa2_1_vitb_dist_vitG_384.pt`. Frozen 2.1 B features are the main shared dependency. | Do not assume a model is causal or that its predictor shares the encoder's feature space. The B/L distilled predictor targets G-size features. Robot AC weights are not a human-action simulator. |
| [SEA-RAFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14793), [official code](https://github.com/princeton-vl/SEA-RAFT), [checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/MemorySlices/Tartan-C-T-TSKH-spring540x960-M/blob/main/model.safetensors) | The motion-preservation pilot already ran real SEA-RAFT. Reuse local cached transport and weights where the observation boundary matches. | High finite/in-frame support does not establish surface visibility or anatomical-joint correspondence. |
| [MediaPipe Pose Landmarker](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker) | Official Lite/Full/Heavy downloads; the existing feature-study setup names local `pose_landmarker_lite.task`. Reuse that route for an actual-estimator condition. | Its 33 landmarks are not SMPL's 22 joints. Use a declared common subset and masks, and count inference on the permitted image resolution. Estimated world coordinates are not GAVD ground truth. |
| [MoMask](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00063), [official evaluation](https://github.com/EricGuo5513/momask-codes#-evaluation) | Real checkpoint inference ran in the pilot. Keep RVQ reconstruction as a documented comparison. | Current adapted reconstruction is not a demonstrated denoiser. Audit fidelity separately from repair usefulness. |
| [SmoothNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.13715), [official release](https://github.com/cure-lab/SmoothNet) | Author README links pretrained checkpoints for temporal pose refinement. A possible P3 comparator after a small load/clean-motion test. | Not executed in this review. Refinement quality must be measured on this error scale; confidence gating and schema compatibility still matter. |
| [DINOv2 official release](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2) | Public pretrained backbones. Optional non-JEPA feature family for P7 and matched RGB controls. | Public access verified, local load not performed. It is an image-feature comparator, not a native temporal predictor. |
| [AMASS](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03278), [local manifest](../../../manifests/amass/) | Existing HAIC motion, full-body parameters and eligible source inventory. | Body fitting, source imbalance and pretraining overlap limit physical and generalization claims. |

Human-JEPA, GOT-JEPA, GaitForeMer and other interesting papers whose usable binaries were not verified are related work, not one-week dependencies. Wan2.2, Goal Force, and MVA remain inspiration; the selected pilots do not need video generation. The existing local feature and geometry routes are enough to start.

**The closest work sets the contribution bar.**

| Proposal | Close primary sources | Result needed beyond that work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1: observation value | [EDDI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.11142), [GSM-AFA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02433), [ActiveMoCap](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08568), [Pose-DRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02024), [Video Active Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01662), [Sensing clocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01537), [REACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11370) | Observation-value predictions must remain useful after changing the forecaster and observation failure, at matched acquisition cost. New weighted combinations of known outputs alone are a modest compositional test. |
| P2: motion beyond joints | [MC-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12698), [JOPAT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23856), [H-MoRe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10676), [H-Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22629) | Show natural-motion prediction information missing from 22-joint histories, then preserve it more effectively than ordinary flow fusion or compression. |
| P3: repair judgment | [GOT-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14771), [SmoothNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.13715), [DNO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11994), [HTD-Refine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26879) | Transfer signed local repair judgment across useful correction generators, preserving real events at comparable repair beyond flow-only checks. |
| P4: motor memory | [Personalized Pose Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03528), [PersonaBooth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07390), [STyMo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04500), [MetaGait](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12886945/) | Walking support must improve actual futures in different activities after strong autoregressive, morphology and raw-support controls. Personal tokens and personalized prediction already exist. |
| P5: evidence-responsive futures | [HumanMAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03665), [SCDP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09574), [UWM-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25313), [uncertainty-aware human prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15221), [redundancy-adaptive learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.14496), [discounted fusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18024) | Establish and correct an evidence-response failure that ordinary fusion/calibration does not solve, with proper-score benefit after an observation shift. A mixture head alone is insufficient. |
| P6: ambiguity witnesses | [DiffPose](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16487), [D3DP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11579), [FMPose3D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05755) | Find independently checked, conclusion-changing alternatives substantially more efficiently than geometry and retrieval. Diverse plausible samples do not establish this. |
| P7: useful teaching | [LogME](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/you21b.html), [VAMP](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.04659), [predictive-state regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.05310), [teacher complementarity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13182), [generalized distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.03643) | Predict actual student improvement and reduce held-configuration choice regret beyond whitening, CCA, published criteria and short student pilots. |

**Ideas removed or demoted by the search.** [Human-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21160) already adapts human-centric forecasting. [PHASOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01851) covers phase-factorized representations. [WRBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20545) studies persistence under visibility changes. Recent [video-model evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15618), [layer probing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01551), and [The Intervention Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29998) make a generic latent-versus-physical or robustness audit a crowded headline. [JEDi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05203) and [WMReward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10553) already use video representations for quality/physical scoring. These can be relevant comparisons, but renaming them for gait does not create the proposed contribution.

The review used full accessible HTML or official implementations for the central design claims, and abstract/release checks for some secondary collisions. No inaccessible method was declared reproduced. Detailed source-reading and access limits are in [world models](../reviews/world-models.md), [gait and data](../reviews/gait-data.md), and [scientific options](../reviews/research-options.md). Candidate methods require local smoke inference before an expensive run; that is a bounded scientific feasibility check, not a new engineering project.
