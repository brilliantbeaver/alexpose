# Gait models, public data, and distinct candidate mechanisms

Research memo, checked 12 September 2026, Pacific time. This is a candidate-development memo, not a claim that any proposed method has worked. Source access, scientific findings, and our inferences are separated below.

GAVD's 1,874 sequences correspond locally to 348 source recordings; participants cannot be reliably enumerated from release IDs. Source separation is weaker than verified participant separation. ## Newly checked collisions that change the shortlist

**Generic clinical adaptation is already strong prior work.** [CARE-PD](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04312v1), NeurIPS 2025, compares frozen encoders, clinical adaptation and healthy-gait adaptation. It explicitly reports that generic pose features overpredict normal severity and that clinical adaptation helps more than healthy walking alone. Therefore, “a healthy motion model misses pathological gait” cannot be the main discovery. Its released protocols and converters are valuable infrastructure.

**Generic whole-body health embeddings are also occupied.** [A Gait Foundation Model Predicts Multi-System Health Phenotypes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25283), March 2026, studies 3,414 deeply phenotyped adults and anatomical contributions to broad health prediction. The full paper says code is request-based during review. The data and checkpoint are not a dependable one-week starting point. [FoundationGait](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00691), revised August 2026, spans identity, health and attributes; its [official repository](https://github.com/ShiqiYu/OpenGait) still says its checkpoints are coming soon. Both rule out “SleepFM for gait” as a distinctive proposal.

**A compact clinical gait prior already exists.** The July 2026 [GaitEncoder preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.07.26357479v1.full) was accessible through indexed excerpts, while direct full-text opening returned 403. Its official repository documents a 16-dimensional VAE, pathology transfer, recovery tracking and patient-specific surgical-change prediction. A generic scalar impairment score or small clinical-effect head would be incremental. This model is more useful as a pathology-inclusive comparison against a generic prior.

**Generative restoration faithfulness has a very close recent precedent.** [Hallucination-Aware Diffusion Sampling via Robust Prior Updates](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02331v1), June 2026, directly studies measurement-unsupported reconstruction. Its sampler changes the prior update and preserves the measurement-conditioning rule. It is a required baseline or conceptual comparison. “Respect observations while using a pretrained prior” is not a sufficient novelty claim.

**Diffusion features for gait are occupied.** [DenoisingGait, CVPR 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Jin_On_Denoising_Walking_Videos_for_Gait_Recognition_CVPR_2025_paper.html) already extracts gait features from diffusion models. [GAITGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22397) already separates motion and pathology for conditional generation. [PGcGAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14409) already conditions pose generation on pathological class. Neither diffusion use nor impaired-gait synthesis is a novelty argument on its own.

**Full-body missing-part prediction is crowded.** [FUSION](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03959) and [AtomicMotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22631) cover whole-body priors and reconstruction from sparse body parts. Their abstract-level claims were inspected; public usable checkpoints were not established. Whole-body expansion is an important controlled variable, not the conceptual contribution.

## Candidate A: Restore measurement failures without deleting reproducible movement

**Specific question.** Within one week, can a small gate around a frozen motion prior reduce tracking corruption while retaining unusual, independently supported temporal events, on an event family and recording process excluded from training?

**Why this could matter.** A denoiser can lower mean joint error while deleting a small movement that determines a scientific conclusion. Average reconstruction quality rewards a smooth answer; reliable analysis requires preserving evidence that is rare but real. The proposed object is the tradeoff between corruption removed and true events attenuated. The contribution must establish and improve that tradeoff across model families, not merely report a normal-prior bias.

**Mechanism.** Generate a candidate repair with frozen MoMask or another verified motion prior. Keep the input alongside the repair. A small temporal gate decides where to retain the original residual, using confidence plus disagreement among independently measured views or pose passes. Train against clean full-body AMASS trajectories and independently corrupted observations. A residual S-JEPA student can learn the same retain-or-repair behavior from paired targets, but its benefit must be compared with an equally sized coordinate model. Do not make S-JEPA mandatory if it contributes nothing.

**Two controlled event families.** First, change the relative timing of an arm and the opposite leg over several cycles while preserving their individual amplitude and frequency. Second, insert a brief foot-clearance excursion using a smooth joint-angle edit with a bone-length-preserving forward model. These are controlled motion edits, not simulated diagnoses. Apply the corresponding perturbation only to estimated keypoints in a second condition while leaving its RGB unchanged. Match support, duration, RMS displacement, maximum velocity, frequency-band power and confidence. The true-motion version changes rendered RGB; the observation-error version does not.

The model must not be rewarded for a synthetic seam. Use smooth envelopes, apply the same start/end windows to both conditions, and reject test pairs if a classifier using the listed simple summaries separates origin above a predeclared chance tolerance. Evaluate additional natural AMASS motions selected before observing model errors, rather than claiming the synthetic edits represent patient disease.

**Identifiability is a first-class control.** If the available skeleton observations are exactly equal, no skeleton-only model can distinguish true movement from tracker error. Such pairs are an abstention test. Any correct origin decision must be supported by independent evidence, such as the RGB or a second observation. Two detectors are not statistically independent simply because their names differ. Report systematic detector-bias cases separately.

**Decisive comparison.** Plot event amplitude retained against joint error removed. Match reconstruction error before comparing retention. Include raw pass-through, tuned smoothing, robust Kalman filtering, confidence gating, dual-tracker disagreement, optical-flow consistency, ordinary prior reconstruction, and Robust Prior Updates when applicable. Require a useful retention gain over the best baseline, not merely over an aggressive default denoiser. Event-origin classification by itself is too easily solved by a second accurate tracker.

**Day 1 gate.** On 128 held-out motion instances and at least two camera conditions, establish that a verified frozen prior deletes supported events at a meaningful rate and that a simple confidence gate does not already solve it. Stop if the effect is absent or a cheap baseline reaches the useful frontier. The sample count is a proposed pilot budget, not an audited count of available unusual clips.

**Day 2 to 3 gate.** Fit one small gate, with one event family held out. Continue only if it improves event retention at matched corruption removal under a held-out detector or corruption mechanism. Use source/motion-level bootstrap uncertainty and at least three actual optimization seeds for any trained method.

**Day 4 to 7 expansion.** Test actual unusual mocap movements, then existing CARE-PD meshes and GAVD videos. GAVD is a robustness and observable-evidence test, not a ground-truth 3D benchmark. A small source-grouped multiclass presentation probe can test whether the method helps preserve useful information, but it should be secondary.

**Risk and novelty assessment.** Novelty is moderate to high only if temporal evidence preservation and transfer across unseen event families go beyond standard measurement consistency. Feasibility is high for the audit and moderate for a significant method result. If optical flow or dual tracking wins, this is an important engineering result but a weak ICLR method paper. Prefer a compact adaptation budget with a hard 48-hour gate over a week of adapter tuning.

## Candidate B: Report which motion conclusions the observation actually identifies

**Specific question.** Can a model abstain on a motion conclusion exactly when multiple 3D motions consistent with the available views disagree, while still answering when extra observation resolves the ambiguity?

**Concrete test.** Use full-body AMASS meshes to construct paired trajectories with a shared monocular projection but different depth motion, then reveal a second view or a short additional visible block. A test conclusion could be the sign of a selected foot's depth displacement or relative arm-leg phase. Evaluate both deliberately ambiguous pairs and naturally sampled unambiguous cases. The conclusion must not reduce to clip length, centroid drift or foreground area.

**Method.** A frozen motion prior supplies candidate completions. A small S-JEPA-conditioned head predicts a set of supported answers, with a raw geometry baseline and calibrated sample-based alternatives. Evaluate coverage, width and selective error at a fixed answer rate. The attractive output is a pair of motions that explain the same recording but imply different conclusions, followed by the exact added evidence that resolves them.

**Hard limitation.** A finite sample of a biased prior is not a certificate that no alternative exists. The controlled test needs constructed counterexamples and an independent geometric optimizer. Do not call learned uncertainty a proof of identifiability. Generic calibrated uncertainty, conformal prediction and 3D ambiguity are established; the new part would need to be reliable conclusion-level transfer under structured missing observations. This overlaps the earlier sparse-anchor proposal, so it should be retained only if the conclusion-level problem is clearly distinct.

**48-hour gate.** Check whether prior sample spread misses constructed ambiguous alternatives and whether the new head recovers them without abstaining almost everywhere. Stop if an analytic camera-support rule performs equally well. This is a lower-compute, lower-wow fallback to Candidate A, not a second headline for the same paper.

## Candidate C: Predict a person's movement response to a recorded medication state change

CARE-PD's BMCLab and E-LC metadata provide an unusually relevant possible response study: predict the ON-minus-OFF change in multiple motion features from OFF motion, with participant-held-out and cohort-transfer evaluation. Baselines would predict no change, cohort-average change and changes from raw kinematic features. The target must be paired within person and must exceed repeat-trial variability. Whole-body measurements matter because upper and lower limbs can respond differently.

This is a **reserve candidate, not a recommended one-week flagship**. The release schema establishes medication metadata, not how many complete paired people survive consistent protocol and quality requirements. Dose, time since medication and acquisition changes may be absent. CARE-PD already tests group medication sensitivity, GaitEncoder already models individual clinical change, and prior clinical work already quantifies medication-responsive kinematic domains. Simply replacing surgery with medication is not a conceptual leap. A defensible contribution would require robust prediction of heterogeneity beyond a conditional average, which is statistically uncertain with the available independent people.

If pursued, first count paired participants and repeated trials and verify baseline reliability. Kill the idea if the target change is mostly measurement noise or average change explains the available signal. Do not use a class label to imply treatment causality or recommend treatment from the resulting predictor.

## Decision

Candidate A offers the most useful combination of inexpensive first evidence, strong connection to the repository's repeated loss of motion information, and a possible general lesson for pretrained world models. Candidate B is only competitive if it demonstrates failures that standard uncertainty misses and survives the analytic geometry baseline. Candidate C has clinical appeal but insufficiently verified paired data and a close intervention-model precedent.

The existing latest result supports asking which information is preserved, not assuming that more pretraining or a more powerful teacher will supply useful gait information. None of these candidates justifies a numerical probability of ICLR acceptance.
