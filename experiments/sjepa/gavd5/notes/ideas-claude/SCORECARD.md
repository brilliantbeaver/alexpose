# S-JEPA gait portfolio scorecard

This scorecard grades all 12 portfolio items on ONE consistent scale so the ranking is comparable across audits and world-model retrains. Each item is scored 1 to 5 on five axes: Novelty (Nov), Mechanism-grounding (Mech), Generalizability-of-claim (Gen), Rigor and evaluation-validity (Rig), and Feasibility-given-ambition (Feas). The composite is a weighted sum on the 1.0 to 5.0 scale: composite = 0.20 Nov + 0.20 Mech + 0.25 Gen + 0.25 Rig + 0.10 Feas. Bands: composite at or above 4.3 is a Strong main-track candidate; 3.6 to 4.2 is Competitive with revision; 2.8 to 3.5 is Workshop or borderline; below 2.8 is Not yet conference-level. Effort is reported separately as a (core weeks / plus reach weeks) tuple with a retrain scale and a data or compute need, and it is deliberately kept OUT of the five likelihood axes so that ambition is not double-penalized.

The unifying thesis behind every item is the discriminative symmetry axis: gait conditions separate by HOW they break walking, and each way ties to a validated, skeleton-recoverable biomarker. LATERALIZED conditions (stroke via corticospinal decussation, hemiplegic cerebral palsy via unilateral periventricular injury, early Parkinson's via contralateral nigrostriatal onset) raise a signed left-minus-right Symmetry Ratio (Patterson 2010, PMID 19932621). Parkinson's adds a RHYTHM signature, elevated stride-time CV (8.8 percent in fallers versus 4.2 percent in non-fallers, Schaafsma 2003 PMID 12809998; Hausdorff 1998 PMID 9613733). Myopathy is SYMMETRIC, a proximal muscle disease with low left-right asymmetry (Xiong 2023, PMID 37525241), preserved cadence, and raised anterior pelvic tilt (16.4 versus 11.6 degrees, Vandekerckhove 2022 PMID 35721358). The neuroscience DEFINES each target and each falsifiable prediction; it never upgrades the n=18-source, single-normal-source, transductive cohort into a clinical-accuracy claim. Generalizability can reach 5 only where a real external cohort backs the claim: PD variability (PhysioNet Gait-in-PD, gaitpdb, DOI 10.13026/C24H3N, force and IMU, label level) and cross-view invariance (CASIA-B, OU-MVLP-Pose, GREW, Gait3D, non-clinical multi-view pose). It stays capped near 3 for cerebral-palsy-specific and myopathy-specific claims, because no participant-disjoint public skeleton cohort exists for either, an honest limitation stated on every relevant item.

## Per-item summaries

**05 signed-laterality-decodability (composite 4.60, Strong main-track candidate).** A positive result confirms that the frozen d0acc262 encoder already carries a signed left-minus-right laterality axis that beats a raw-coordinate null by at least 0.05 R-squared, sits within a mirror-slope band near y = -x, and holds sign on at least 75 percent of held-out sources; an informative null overturns the belief that the curriculum encoder learned a directional asymmetry axis at all and localizes the failure to representation rather than probe. It scores highest because the mechanism grounding is the tightest in the portfolio (signed laterality is THE discriminative axis), the four-lane instrument with pre-registered margins is maximally rigorous, and the axis generalizes as a reusable diagnostic. Effort is minimal: a frozen-encoder read on cached tensors, three core weeks with Day-5 and Day-14 gates and no retrain, which is why Feasibility is 5.

**09 reflection-equivariant-symmetry-axis (composite 4.55, Strong main-track candidate).** A positive result confirms a transferable design claim: wiring the signed axis to be antisymmetric by construction beats letting a standard encoder learn it, exceeding d0acc262 by at least 0.05 held-out-source R-squared on item 05's own instrument; an informative null overturns the intuition that adding the equivariance is a free win at this scale, showing the standard encoder already learned the symmetry or that the signed axis is not the bottleneck on n=18 sources. Novelty and Mechanism are both 5 (an inductive-bias claim aligned exactly to the neuroanatomy), and Rigor is 5 because it reuses item 05's frozen bounds and adds a by-construction manipulation check. Generalizability is 4 rather than 5 because the clinical side stays transductive with no external CP or myopathy skeleton cohort. Effort is a full five-stage curriculum retrain (600 epochs, 11,400 updates) matching the d0acc262 lineage, roughly 3 to 4 core weeks plus a 2 to 3 week non-clinical multi-view reach, hence Feasibility 3.

**12 cross-view-invariance (composite 4.35, Strong main-track candidate).** A positive result confirms that treating viewpoint as an action (view_delta) with a strict no-flip rule yields more view-stable features than flip augmentation by at least a 10 percent relative drift reduction, while the mirror probe shows flip augmentation inverts the lateralized biomarker; an informative null overturns the claim that view-as-action adds anything over flip augmentation for robustness, separating the invariance benefit from the asymmetry-protection benefit. Generalizability is 5 because the core arm runs on real external multi-view cohorts (CASIA-B, OU-MVLP-Pose, GREW, Gait3D) with identity-disjoint leave-one-view-out, the strongest external footing in the portfolio, and Novelty is 5 for the view-as-action framing. Rigor is 4 (two endpoints, two non-neural ceilings, an explicit identity-control fallback). Effort is a predictor-head retrain plus mandatory external downloads and pose-normalization, three core weeks, with any clinical claim gated on nonexistent multi-view clinical data, hence Feasibility 3.

**04 resize-timing-tax (composite 4.25, Competitive with revision).** A positive result confirms an algebraic non-identifiability: absolute cadence and speed are provably not recoverable under the 64-frame resize, so the cadence and speed tax (1 minus R-squared) stays at or above 0.70 and double-support at or above 0.50; an informative null overturns the proof's empirical reach if some cadence signal survives the resize, which would itself be a surprising and publishable measurement artifact. Novelty is 4 for the crisp proof, and Rigor is 5 because the claim is partly analytic and the empirical arm is tightly bounded. Generalizability is 4 (a resize-pipeline lesson that transfers to any fixed-frame skeleton model). Effort is low but not zero: no encoder training, yet it needs an NPZ plus fps regeneration to recover the true time base, 1 to 2 core weeks, so Feasibility is 4.

**11 target-isolation-substrate (composite 4.05, Competitive with revision).** A positive result names a winning prediction target for gait: one of four matched families (raw coordinates, one-frame motion, centered-sharpened latent, normalized latent regression) recovers a validated biomarker above the raw-input ceiling by at least 0.05 R-squared with everything else held identical; an informative null overturns the JEPA enthusiasm that target choice is the bottleneck at small skeleton scale, locating the limit in data breadth, and it would confirm rather than contradict the known stride-time-CV and asymmetry decodability limits. Rigor is 5 (the matched-substrate design is the cleanest attribution mechanism in the set), and the target-design principle generalizes (Gen 4). Effort is the heaviest: four full matched curriculum retrains, 3 to 4 core weeks of compute, hence Feasibility 2.

**01 provenance-pathway-attribution (composite 3.95, Competitive with revision).** A positive result confirms that a Provenance-Decodability Index can quantify how much of the embedding's normal-versus-abnormal separability (about 0.96 AUC) is explained by the canonical-versus-augmented extraction path rather than gait, with a pre-registered kill gate; an informative null (index at or below the gate) overturns the worry that the headline separability is an acquisition artifact and clears the other items to trust the embedding. Rigor and Feasibility are both 5: a near-zero-retrain read on the frozen d0acc262 checkpoint over cached tensors, three core weeks and no external cohort. Novelty is 3 (a confound audit, not a new object) and Mechanism is 3 (it is about acquisition, not neuroanatomy), so despite top Rigor and Feasibility it lands mid-band.

**07 group-loss-supervision-isolation (composite 3.95, Competitive with revision).** A positive result confirms that the L_group term makes Stages 1 to 4 supervised fine-tuning by isolating the transductive-minus-inductive gap and per-stage V-information, with inductive macro-F1 improving by at least 0.05 when the term is ON versus OFF-matched versus OFF-random; an informative null overturns the belief that the group loss adds inductive structure beyond transductive memorization. Novelty is 4 (the transductive-versus-inductive isolation is a genuinely new lens) and Rigor is 5 (three matched arms, per-stage V-information). Effort is a fold-local finetune at gavd5 scale (159 sequences, 35 sources) with no fresh pretraining, three core weeks plus a 2 to 3 week reach, so Feasibility is 3.

**02 surprise-tomography (composite 3.90, Competitive with revision).** A positive result confirms that the spatial pattern of masked-prediction surprise (a 12-by-16 image) beats a pooled scalar for separating conditions and that an injected deficit lands its surprise mass in the matched anatomical rows above a 0.60 threshold, gaining at least 0.05 AUC; an informative null overturns the claim that surprise has usable spatial structure beyond a single number. Mechanism is 4 (surprise localized to biomarker regions) and the method plus the gaitpdb reach give Gen 4. It is a test-time read on the existing checkpoint, no retrain, 2 to 3 core weeks, so Feasibility is 5. Novelty is 3 because it reads the existing checkpoint rather than building a new object.

**03 inference-time-motion-energy (composite 3.90, Competitive with revision).** A positive result confirms that position versus motion energy read from the frozen encoder separates PD from myopathy (the rhythm-versus-symmetric contrast) under a majority rule across at least 7 of 12 sources and both PD folds, within-window relative regularity only; an informative null overturns the belief that motion energy adds a separable channel over static posture at this window length. Mechanism is 4 (the PD-versus-myopathy contrast is mechanism-defined) and Gen 4 (method plus gaitpdb reach). Zero-retrain on cached tensors, three core weeks, so Feasibility is 5. Novelty is 3 as another frozen-encoder readout.

**08 concept-bottleneck-disentangled (composite 3.75, Competitive with revision).** A positive result confirms steerable disentanglement: after a full curriculum retrain with three biomarker-supervised subspaces (z_asym, z_rhythm, z_posture), intervening on one subspace moves only its biomarker (steerability ratio at least 3, leak at most 0.2) and beats a raw-coordinate ceiling; an informative null overturns the belief that biomarker heads plus VICReg decorrelation plus biased masking suffice to disentangle these axes in a small skeleton JEPA, honestly flagging z_rhythm as the highest-risk subspace since stride-time CV is not linearly decodable from roughly 2-second windows. Novelty is the joint-highest 5 (named causally steerable subspaces is the most ambitious new object). Generalizability is capped at 3 because z_posture cannot be externally confirmed (no CP or myopathy skeleton cohort). Effort is a full curriculum retrain plus three aux heads and a decorrelation term, 6 to 8 weeks, so Feasibility is 2.

**10 prediction-error-severity (composite 3.65, Competitive with revision).** A positive result confirms that a normal-only world model yields a mechanism-decomposable prediction-error field whose channels track the direction and magnitude of a synthetic injection (one-sided knee flexion loads the asymmetry channel, symmetric proximal deficit loads posture) above an 80-percent-of-raw-coordinate-ceiling bar; an informative null overturns the claim that the normal-only model learned a spatially structured expectation rather than merely echoing the perturbed coordinate. Mechanism and Novelty are 4 (label-free severity that side-steps label-versus-source collinearity). Generalizability is 3 because normal comes from a single video (3KnFt8bH3tE), so the model is transductive to one source, and the injection test has an acknowledged near-tautology risk. Effort is a from-scratch normal-only Stage-0-budget train, three core weeks, so Feasibility is 3.

**06 mask-geometry-as-object (composite 3.65, Competitive with revision).** The augmented item makes a transferable claim, the mask-family-at-equal-coverage principle: holding encoder, compute, steps, and per-sample masked-token count fixed, the GEOMETRY of a gait-shaped Stage-0 mask decides which clinical axis becomes linearly recoverable from the frozen features. It pre-registers three mechanism-defined families, each its own Arm-U-versus-Arm-S-versus-Arm-S-scram contrast under the same difficulty control and provenance kill gate. Contralateral-pair masking hides one side's lower limb so the predictor must infill the hidden side from its partner, forcing an asymmetry axis that maps to lateralized conditions (corticospinal decussation in stroke PMID 30571044, contralateral nigrostriatal onset in early Parkinson's PMID 22367437, unilateral periventricular injury in hemiplegic cerebral palsy PMID 19081519; biomarker the Symmetry Ratio, Patterson 2010 PMID 19932621), and it targets the weakest-decoded scalar (asymmetry R-squared about 0.154). Half-cycle or future-phase masking hides a contiguous half of the gait cycle so the predictor must infill from rhythm, forcing a rhythm axis that maps to Parkinson's basal-ganglia loss of automaticity (Redgrave 2010 PMID 20944662, Wu 2015 PMID 26102020; biomarker stride-time CV, 8.8 percent in fallers versus 4.2 percent in non-fallers, Schaafsma 2003 PMID 12809998, Hausdorff 1998 PMID 9613733). Proximal-segment masking hides the hips and proximal thigh chain on BOTH sides so the predictor must infill a symmetric proximal pattern, mapping to myopathy (diffuse symmetric proximal weakness, Barohn 2014 PMID 25037080; biomarker anterior pelvic tilt 16.4 versus 11.6 degrees with preserved cadence and low left-right asymmetry, Vandekerckhove 2022 PMID 35721358, Xiong 2023 PMID 37525241). A positive result confirms that at matched reconstruction difficulty each family raises recoverability of exactly its predicted axis above both the uniform mask and the marginal-matched scramble, without raising provenance decodability; an informative null overturns the belief that mask structure, rather than data scale, token geometry, or label-aware fine-tuning, is the lever at this scale (96 sequences, 18 sources, embed 64, depth 2), redirecting effort away from mask engineering. Mechanism-grounding rises to 4 because each family now carries a verified neurological-source-to-biomarker chain, but Generalizability stays capped at 3: only the PD-rhythm family has even a cross-modal label-level external anchor (PhysioNet gaitpdb), while the asymmetry and symmetric-proximal families have no participant-disjoint public skeleton cohort. Novelty is 4 (mask geometry as the manipulated variable, tied to three mechanism axes, is fresh) and Rigor is 4 (equal-coverage difficulty control, in-space scramble, hard provenance kill gate at 0.02, readout-time permutation sanity check, non-neural coordinate floor), held below 5 because the three-family expansion multiplies fold-local retrains and the held-out folds rest on tiny per-condition source counts (Parkinson's 2, cerebral palsy 2). Effort is medium: build and verify the fold-local Stage-0 retrain harness once, then run each family through the same control and gate, roughly 4 to 6 core weeks with no external cohort, so Feasibility is 3. The higher composite over the prior draft reflects the tightened mechanism-to-biomarker link, not weaker execution.
