# Title options for the RISEx one-page candidate

The paper uses neurological and clinical gait literature as motivation, but encoder training has no neurological measurement, diagnosis, affected-side label, or clinical target. “Neurologically informed” could reasonably suggest that such information enters the model or evaluation. I therefore prefer “neurologically motivated.” Scores are editorial judgments on a five-point scale, not acceptance probabilities.

The chosen title names the clinical rationale without claiming that the model is neurologically informed. Its weakness is that it makes the RISEx robotics connection less visible. Adding “for human-aware robotics” would make an untested application appear central, so it should be avoided. The first sentence of the abstract supplies the narrower AI/robotics motivation instead.
