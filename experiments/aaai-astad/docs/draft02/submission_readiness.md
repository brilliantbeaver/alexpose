# Submission readiness

The package supports a reproducible exploratory measurement paper with a bounded negative detection result. It does not support a state-of-the-art detector, a neurological mechanism, or clinical deployment. The strongest positive finding is useful held-out completion in the independent laboratory study; [draft03](../draft03/README.md) develops that interpretation separately without changing the outcomes.

## What the evidence supports

The controlled predictor recovers known dependencies in software fixtures. In the laboratory data, the fitted bilateral family improves held-out healthy completion in every fold and beats direct interpolation. The GAVD focused distal task also exposes completion headroom. These are useful measurement findings under the declared predictor families, not estimates of neural coupling or a complete effective-capacity match.

The fixed equal-weight anomaly score loses AUROC relative to local movement in both datasets. The GAVD primary difference is −0.118, conditional interval [−0.217, −0.025]. The independent laboratory difference is −0.066, [−0.106, −0.031]. Demographic matching retains the direction. The relational score can still contain abnormality information; these results reject only the claimed advantage of this particular score construction.

The timing diagnostic has sufficient recording coverage but fails prediction adequacy on sparse paired inputs. Its positive minus-four-frame effect cannot support a reliable coordination interpretation. A failed scientific gate is a result, not a completed demonstration of the intended mechanism.

## Scientific strength and unresolved risks

These ratings are editorial judgments on a five-point scale, not acceptance probabilities.

| Dimension | Judgment | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Reproducibility and traceability | 4/5 | Actual fits, predictions, source roles, independent notebook executions and reconstruction checks are retained. |
| Methodological novelty | 3/5 | The value is the controlled measurement design and support-sensitive gate, not a new mask, model architecture or information theorem. |
| Evidence for useful completion | 3.5/5 | Independent participant folds and interpolation controls are encouraging, with unequal effective rank and limited trial sampling still unresolved. |
| Evidence for added anomaly detection | 1/5 | The frozen primary score is worse than the strong local baseline. |
| Clinical generalization | 1.5/5 | GAVD identities and labels are limited; the independent laboratory study covers only healthy and stroke walking with a different measurement system. |

The main reviewer risk is that a weakly calibrated fusion's failure can seem predictable. The paper must therefore lead with the observable estimator, exact input-access controls, the distinction between support and prediction adequacy, and the independent completion result. It should not market the negative fusion result alone as a major algorithmic breakthrough.

Five GAVD reference normals, previously explored normal recordings, unknown repeated people, camera/view changes, confidence failures, unequal residual ranks and conditional-only intervals remain material. A 128-update imputed STG-NF adapter is not a faithful benchmark, and the small JEPA probes do not settle representation-learning quality. No threshold, calibrated clinical risk or 95% specificity is well resolved here.

## Best next improvement

The highest-value next experiment is a separately frozen participant-held-out study with a stronger own-leg basis and normal-only score calibration, followed by evaluation on untouched trials or participants. It should test whether the completion benefit survives closer flexibility matching and whether a reliability-aware relational channel adds value without overwhelming posture and amplitude. Repeat-trial analysis would also test stability. These are future experiments, not repairs that can be quietly substituted into the present result.

Formatting and execution are ready for human review. Scientific readiness is conditional on accepting the bounded measurement contribution and its negative detection finding. The manuscript is not an established strong positive detection paper, and acceptance or impact cannot be guaranteed.
