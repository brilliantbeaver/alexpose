# Review of the rendered draft02 manuscript

Reviewed on 15 November 2026 against `manuscript.md`, its numerical fields, saved GAVD and external results, interpolation-audit derivatives, figure SVG text, and figure input hashes. Only this review was written. I did not change the manuscript, scientific code, models or artifacts.

The main result is supported. I independently recomputed 204 saved GAVD weighted within-fold AUROCs from source scores. The maximum discrepancy was 1.11e-16. The external result, matched sensitivity and post-outcome interpolation comparison also agree with their saved evidence. The revised manuscript now properly reports unequal residual ranks and preserves the distinction between prediction improvement and detection improvement.

I found several correctable reporting errors and a few clarity issues. None requires fitting a new estimator or replacing the frozen results.

## Confirmed reporting errors

### 1. The bootstrap replicate count is wrong in the general evaluation paragraph

**Priority: high for reporting accuracy. Location: manuscript line 108.**

The text says “Paired 5,000-replicate bootstraps” when describing the detection comparisons. Every recorded detection contrast in GAVD `paired_intervals.json` has `repeats: 1000`, including the primary S-minus-M and A-minus-U contrasts. External `paired_effects.json` also records 1,000 replicates. The matched external sensitivity and timing summaries use 1,000 draws. The 5,000-draw procedure belongs to the separate held-out-normal benefit summaries in `normal_test_benefit`, not the headline AUROC intervals.

**Correction:** State that detection and timing intervals use 1,000 replicates, and separately identify the 5,000-draw normal-benefit analysis if it is discussed. Keep the current reported interval endpoints. They match the saved 1,000-draw calculations; no rerun is needed to correct the prose.

For example, the primary GAVD interval is generated by the saved effect -0.1175115207 and endpoints [-0.2165898618, -0.0252880184], which correctly round to the manuscript's values.

### 2. The “Observation quality” row actually reports availability plus viewpoint

**Priority: medium. Locations: manuscript lines 106 and 144; renderer's detection-row mapping.**

The row labeled “Observation quality,” with focal/broad AUROCs 0.389/0.520, is rendered from `nuisance_distance`. In `source_controls.fit_source_controls`, this model combines 16 availability features with annotation-view fractions. It is not the availability-only `quality16` score. The latter has saved source scores whose weighted AUROCs are approximately 0.367512 and 0.452899.

This is a labeling error, not evidence that the listed numeric values are miscomputed.

**Correction:** Rename the existing row and comparator description to “Availability and viewpoint distance,” or another equally explicit description. There is no need to add another table row unless space permits. If the availability-only comparator is shown separately, identify it as `quality16` and use its own saved values. Do not silently substitute one score for the other.

Evidence: `iteration_runner.py:194-208`, `source_controls.py:163-180`, and `render_manuscript.py` mapping `nuisance_distance` to “Observation quality.”

### 3. The boundary audit has one failed frame, not an undecodable sequence

**Priority: medium. Location: manuscript line 197.**

The text says “one sequence fails decoding.” The source audit records one failed requested frame for source `EjPUpKUbZSg`. Its only sequence, `cljwbcjvq00043n6lllxhr535`, has 307 requested annotated frames and 306 decoded frames. The sequence was not wholly undecodable.

**Correction:** Write “one requested frame could not be decoded.” The same wording should be corrected in the generated documentation sentence that currently says a sequence could not be decoded. The retained source counts and outside-image fractions do not need to change.

Evidence: `video_review/boundary_source_audit.csv`, `boundary_audit.csv`, and `audit_gavd_boundaries.py:29`, which increments the counter per failed frame read.

### 4. The method figure blurs the two distinct normal roles

**Priority: medium. Location: `figures/method.svg`, the M box.**

The M box says “Distance from normal references.” In the study's defined terminology, reserved references are the five GAVD normals used to estimate R. M is fitted on the 16 fitting-normal sources. The same separation holds externally with 76 fitting participants versus 20 reserved references.

**Correction:** Label M as “Distance from fitting normals.” If space permits, label R as a departure from the reserved normal references. This is a figure-role clarification. The underlying implementations keep the roles separate correctly.

## Necessary clarifications that do not change results

### 5. Identify the plotted predictor in the timing caption

The timing SVG shows two offsets for the U-trained predictor, with the U effect and intervals reported in the paragraph. Neither the SVG text nor the paper caption explicitly identifies U. A standalone reader could mistake the figure for an average over U, J and A.

Add “U-trained predictor on 27 outer-test normal recordings” to the caption. Retain the statement that every policy's paired-support gate fails, but distinguish that broader conclusion from the specific plotted series. The existing numbers and intervals agree with the saved U timing summary.

The timing method paragraph would also benefit from one compact reproducibility sentence stating that the same 16 uniform 96-of-192 parent masks are applied before donor/original joint-and-hip visibility is intersected. That clarifies why a nominal half-mask can leave only about 1.75 editable distal tokens. The full protocol already records seed 217; this is a missing explanatory detail, not an implementation error.

### 6. Clarify what is paired during mask training

The sentence “Loss targets, budgets, named RNG streams and source order are paired” is too broad. U, J and A do not necessarily choose the same hidden training targets or retain the same valid loss terms. They share the target definition, source/window schedule, nominal budget and evaluation bank. The following sentence correctly acknowledges different usable supervision.

Suggested wording: “The coordinate target definition, nominal budget, unrelated RNG streams and source/window schedule are shared; selected training targets and usable loss terms can differ across masking policies.” Also say that the start is chosen uniformly from the listed aligned starts for A, because that assumption is needed for the displayed marginal-probability calculation.

### 7. Preserve the current narrow interpretation in the opening sentences

The abstract's phrase “without providing useful abnormality evidence” can sound broader than the actual finding, even though its final sentence is appropriately qualified. External R has AUROC 0.694, and normal prediction improves. “Without improving detection beyond a strong movement baseline” states the tested result more precisely.

Similarly, the introduction's “timing sensitivity is uninterpretable” is stronger than needed. The response is interpretable as predictor sensitivity under sparse edited context. What fails is its interpretation as a reliable measurement of useful gait timing or coordination. The results and conclusion already mostly make this distinction.

These are precision edits, not requests for a new estimator or additional favorable analyses.

## Limitations versus bugs

The following remain legitimate limitations, already substantially disclosed, rather than reasons to refit the current study: unequal effective ranks; different internal and final aggregation; five-reference uncertainty; omitted bilateral covariance; different M/R tails; processed PiG references; unmatched walking speed; one selected laboratory trial; GAVD identities and viewpoint uncertainty; and conditional intervals that omit training uncertainty.

The added interpolation audit resolves the earlier missing-baseline criticism for the external comparison. My earlier external review should be read with that update. The current manuscript does not need a post hoc new calibration or estimator to report its bounded negative result. Any such change would be a new research iteration, not a correction to these frozen outcomes.

## Novelty and claim boundary

The manuscript makes a defensible workshop-level claim about an executed controlled evaluation: distinguish learned completion, usable observation support and incremental detection value. It does not claim a new tube mask, a new neural world model, state-of-the-art accuracy or a recovered neurological cause. Its external replication is explicitly a refitted method in another representation, rather than encoder transfer.

The main editorial risk is making the failure sound universal. Keep R-alone discrimination, external normal prediction gains and the fixed-fusion qualification visible. The present conclusion largely does this correctly. Correct the reporting issues above, preserve the saved outcomes, and avoid implying that a broader class of relational detectors has been disproved.
