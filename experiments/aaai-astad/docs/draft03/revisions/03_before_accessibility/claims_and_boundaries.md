# Claims and evidence

The paper contributes a controlled procedure for measuring cross-leg prediction and assessing its use in gait anomaly detection. Completion, observation adequacy and anomaly ranking are separate empirical outcomes.

| Claim | Evidence and scope |
| --- | --- |
| The fitted bilateral family improves normal laboratory completion. | Mean error falls by 5.20%, with conditional paired interval [3.55%, 6.70%], across 128 healthy participants. All four fold means improve. |
| Bilateral prediction helps on the GAVD distal-leg diagnostic. | The uniform-trained reference lowers error by 19.5% relative to its own-leg augmentation. The absolute-benefit interval is [0.010721, 0.021300]. Targets are estimated pose coordinates. |
| Anatomical training helps the focused missing-observation task. | A versus U lowers E_leg error by 7.71%, with interval [6.36%, 9.00%], on identical targets. The unchanged uniform-task improvement is uncertain. |
| Observation support matters when interpreting a predictor. | All outer paired-support timing gates fail despite adequate recording counts. A nonzero response to an edit does not establish an adequate timing measurement. |
| Relational departure has an observed laboratory AUROC of 0.694. | R reaches AUROC 0.694. M reaches 0.958 and the fixed combination reaches 0.892, so incremental detection value is not established. |
| The method is auditable across measurement systems. | Saved predictions retain target keys, masks, validity, source roles and reference parameters. The laboratory study refits the algorithmic comparison on participant-separated angle signals. |

Equal residual widths do not equalize feature rank, inductive bias or effective capacity. The evidence supports the declared fitted-family comparison, not a capacity-free information estimate. The task is masked completion, not future forecasting. Its targets, references and shared body normalization do not identify physiological coordination or a disease cause.

The successful core comparison uses ridge coordinate and angle predictors. It is not evidence that JEPA pretraining causes the completion gain. The anatomical mask family and residual construction have established precedents; the contribution is their controlled use to evaluate relational anomaly evidence.

Completion percentages are ratios of group mean losses, not averages of individual percentage changes. The paired completion audit is post-outcome descriptive. Its conditional intervals keep fitted models, roles, masks and the selected analysis fixed. The 28 normal GAVD recordings informed development; upload IDs do not establish independent participants. The external study covers healthy and stroke walking, not all four condition families.
