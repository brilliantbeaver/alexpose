# Claims and their limits

The positive interpretation changes the emphasis of the paper, not its scientific protocol. Completion and masking comparisons refer to held-out normal observations. Detection results retain the original labels, sources and primary endpoint.

| Proposed claim | Status | Evidence and permissible wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The fitted bilateral family helps complete normal laboratory movement. | Supported under the declared model families. | Mean error falls by 5.20%, with conditional paired interval [3.55%, 6.70%], over 128 healthy participants. All four fold means improve. |
| Opposite-leg context helps on the declared GAVD distal challenge. | Supported for estimated coordinates and retained normal recordings. | The U-trained reference reduces error by 19.5% relative to its own-leg augmentation. The absolute benefit interval excludes zero. |
| Anatomical training improves the focused missing-leg task. | Supported as a post-outcome paired description of a predeclared bank. | A versus U reduces error by 7.71%, with interval [6.36%, 9.00%], on identical E_leg targets. |
| Anatomical masking is generally better. | Unsupported. | The unchanged U-task improvement is uncertain and one fold worsens. |
| The gain is independent of every capacity difference. | Unsupported. | Input widths match, but numerical feature ranks and inductive biases differ. |
| The fitted model measures physiological coordination. | Unsupported. | Timing adequacy fails; source signals include geometry, projection and shared preprocessing. |
| The fixed relational fusion improves abnormal-gait detection. | Contradicted by the completed comparison. | S is worse than M in GAVD and the independent laboratory study. |
| All relational anomaly methods are ineffective. | Unsupported. | Only a particular estimator, reference transform and fixed fusion were tested. R alone has laboratory AUROC 0.694. |
| A learned JEPA encoder is responsible for the positive completion finding. | Unsupported. | The positive core uses coordinate/angle ridge predictors. Small learned/random JEPA probes are a separate supervised experiment. |
| The experiment has clinical validity across neurological conditions. | Unsupported. | GAVD upload IDs and folder labels are limited; the participant study covers healthy and stroke walking in a different measurement system. |

The completion percentages are ratios of group mean losses, not averages of individual percentage changes. Conditional intervals keep fitted models, roles, masks and the chosen analysis fixed. They omit fitting uncertainty and the exploratory choice to emphasize these results. The GAVD units are recordings, and unknown repeated participants remain possible.
