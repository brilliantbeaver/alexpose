# Title alternatives for the WACV 2027 Vision4Neuro submission

The strongest fit is with the workshop's explicit interest in vision-based behavioural phenotyping and markerless pose analysis. The title should identify the representation-learning study without implying a validated diagnostic application. [Vision4Neuro call for papers](https://cvpath-vision4neuro.github.io/wacv2027/vision4neuro.html), checked 16 October 2026.

The scores below are editorial assessments of workshop relevance and fidelity to the experiment, on a ten-point scale; they should not be read as acceptance probabilities. These are alternatives to “Learning Skeleton Representations from Neurological Gait Video.”

| Alternative | Relevance | Why use it | Reason to choose another |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Predictive Skeleton Learning from a Small Collection of Neurological Gait Videos** | **9.5/10** | Makes the limited-data setting explicit while distinguishing the predictive learning approach. It communicates the study's contribution without claiming that pretraining has an isolated causal advantage. This is the strongest overall submission title. | Longer than the proposed title. Define “predictive” promptly in the abstract so readers understand that the training task predicts hidden features. |
| **Self-Supervised Skeleton Representations for Neurological Gait Analysis** | **9.0/10** | A concise, conventional research title that makes the encoder's training approach clear. It works well for a broad computer-vision audience and keeps the neurological application visible. | The small collection is not apparent from the title. The abstract must distinguish self-supervised feature learning from the supervised classifier used for label prediction. |
| **S-JEPA for Neurological Gait Analysis: Learning from a Small Video Collection** | **8.5/10** | Names the tested architecture and frames the work as a focused application study. It is useful when discoverability by readers interested in JEPA methods matters. | S-JEPA is less familiar to the general neuroscience audience. Naming an existing architecture also makes it especially important for the abstract to explain the empirical contribution of this study. |

**Original editorial recommendation:** the first alternative. It adds the method's predictive character and the small collection setting to the proposed title, making the paper's specific contribution clearer. The second is the strongest shorter option. The third is most suitable when the submission is deliberately positioned around the S-JEPA framework.

**Selected title:** alternative 2, **Self-Supervised Skeleton Representations for Neurological Gait Analysis**, as requested by the author. The main paper and supplement now use this title; both PDFs and the Overleaf package have been refreshed.
