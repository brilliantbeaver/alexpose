# Independent review of revised draft v4

Reviewed 2026-10-08. I read `wacv2007-v4.tex`, the frozen `references-v4.bib`, the prior v3 review and relevant evidence reports, then inspected all seven rendered PDF pages at readable size. I also checked the original and revised references against the completed 57-reference audit. This review focuses on scientific framing, literature accuracy, clinical limits, readability for educated researchers outside AI/ML, and the actual figure layout. It does not represent a new training experiment.

The paper now gives a defensible, bounded account of the notebook-associated experiment. The main numerical comparisons are consistent with the supplied results audit, the RF correction is visibly separated from the primary comparison, and the manuscript no longer attributes clinical meaning to its unverified labels. The new pipeline figure is a substantial improvement: straight rectangular boxes, balanced spacing and short internal lines are legible without collisions. The remaining work is mostly about making the argument easier to understand and closing a few concrete reproducibility and presentation gaps.

## Response to earlier critiques

The student masking operation now explains attention exclusion and replacement with a learned mask token. The center's token weighting is distinguished from equal-example loss averaging. The seven-region sampling rule, 36-draw guard, arm-region boost, limited context guarantee and notebook-derived 512-mask diagnostic are present. The RF feature vector and seed are explicit. Pixel coordinates, cleaning and body normalization are correctly distinguished. The reflection paragraph states how a teacher target can be exposed in transformed student context without confusing this with test-source leakage. The corrected-RF comparison now explicitly uses S-JEPA minus RF, so its positive difference and interval have an unambiguous sign.

## Required changes for v5

1. **Use the same fold indices in the figure and text.** Figure 2a labels its horizontal axis 1–5, whereas Table 2 uses 0–4 and the training-selection text refers to folds zero through four. This can make the striking third plotted point seem to contradict the reported fold-2 result. Change the plot labels to 0–4. The plot values themselves agree with the audited ordering. Preserve the simple plotting style; there is no need for decorative elements or curved connectors.

2. **Correct the interpolation-distribution claim and connect sampling to elapsed time.** Section 3 says “Padding and interpolation can change apparent dynamics; their distribution across labels is therefore reported explicitly.” Table 1 reports padding, but there is no per-label interpolation frequency or gap-length distribution. Write that padding counts are reported, while interpolation burden is unavailable from the retained cache. The notebook audit also documents that cached `fps=15` stores the requested rate, not each clip's realized sampling rate. Add the plain consequence: windows have the same number of sampled frames but can cover different lengths of time. Do not invent a precise effective-rate range or interpolation fraction from those caches.

3. **Introduce the research question and score in ordinary scientific language before the technical names.** The abstract is about 204 words, so length is acceptable; its obstacle is terminology. Readers encounter joint-embedding architecture, outer folds, inner holdout, frozen representation, pooled source-weighted macro-F1 and conditional percentile intervals before those ideas are explained. Lead with automatically tracked body landmarks and whether learning from their sequences improves on averaging body position. Define the learned representation once as a numerical summary of a clip. Describe the primary score as giving every source recording equal total weight and then averaging performance across the three labels. Introduce F1 in the evaluation section as combining precision and recall, with higher values indicating better classification; retain the exact formula for technical readers. Avoid implying that 0.457 is a percentage of correctly diagnosed people.

   An example of the intended register is: “We tested whether learning from sequences of automatically tracked body landmarks improves prediction of the labels attached to gait videos. The comparison used 88 clips from 41 source recordings, with recordings kept separate during training and testing. A simple summary of average body position performed similarly to the learned model.” The following sentences can carry the model name, exact scores and the uncertainty bound. This is a proposed explanation, not replacement evidence or an added experiment.

4. **Define or replace specialized terms at their first use.** “Frozen” means that encoder weights are kept unchanged when the label classifier is fitted; “linear probe” is the label classifier; a “token” is a group of four frames for one landmark; the target encoder supplies the learned reference descriptions used during training. Use “classifier” in Figure 1's training box instead of the unexplained “probes.” The figure otherwise reads well. A compact settings table can retain widths, layers, learning rates and temperatures while the surrounding prose explains what is learned and how it is evaluated. Keep mathematical and implementation precision in that table or the companion methods record rather than deleting it.

5. **Remove development-history language from the scientific narrative.** A new reviewer does not know what “the original PDF” or a “capstone notebook” means. The sentence declaring the original PDF not to be evidence belongs in the audit record, not the Results section. Name the experiment as the notebook-defined primary run, then refer once to the accompanying protocol and prediction records. Similarly, the sentence excluding normal-only pretraining and class-aware VICReg addresses obsolete possibilities not introduced by this paper. State the positive fact that pretraining uses all training sources without their labels, and retain version-history distinctions in the audit. This keeps the paper understandable to researchers who have not seen the editing process.

6. **Make the companion record real and reproducible.** At review time `evidence/reproducibility.md`, explicitly named in Section 6, did not yet exist. Create it before presenting this as supplied material. It should identify the historical and corrected execution directories, registry and prediction hashes, software versions and exact analysis command. The source splitter also sorts source IDs before applying seeded stratified splitting (`sjepa/splits.py`); include this ordering in the protocol record, because the same seed applied to a different row order does not reproduce the partitions. The manuscript may use a shorter reference to the accompanying reproducibility record after that file is present.

7. **Expose the preprint identifier in the rendered reference list and sharpen the adaptation statement.** The bibliography renders BlazePose as a title and year without its arXiv identifier because the supplied style does not print the current `eprint` field. Add a supported `note` or equivalent so `arXiv:2006.10204` appears. The retained authors, titles and years are otherwise correct. In Related Work, one sentence can distinguish the evaluated adaptation from the original S-JEPA: the present system uses video-derived 2D coordinates plus visibility and regional sampling, while the cited S-JEPA learns from skeletal action data and uses motion-aware target sampling. State this as a design difference, without implying that the adaptation improves upon the original method.

## Claims and limits to preserve

The paper should continue to treat “Normal,” MS and PD as collection labels, with no inference that the sources represent clinically verified independent patients. The similar pooled scores do not prove equivalence or absence of useful movement information. Visibility performance does not quantify how much recording information remains in the coordinate representations. The historical RF defect, corrected comparison and reflection problem should remain explicit, with no unsupported claim about why a defect changed classification. The bootstrap remains a retrospective description of source resampling around fixed predictions, excluding retraining and development variability.

The seven-page PDF has content through page 6, with references continuing on page 7, within the eight-page content allowance. All text, equations, tables and figures were readable in the inspected render. Figure 1 has no observed overflow or overlap. Figure 2 has readable matrix annotations with a common color scale; its remaining defect is the fold numbering. The anonymous review header still uses the deliberate placeholder `DRAFT`; a real submission identifier would be needed only when an actual submission is created. No submission is authorized or performed by this review.

No manuscript, notebook, prediction or figure was changed for this review.
