# Illustrated JEPA and gait FAQ

Read [Learning gait with JEPA](faq.md), or download the [Word document](faq.docx).

The guide contains 45 questions and 15 original vector illustrations explaining the latest `gavd3` experiments. It follows the reasoning from motivation and working hypotheses through data preparation, S-JEPA training, classification, source-video controls, gait probes, mask ablation, and future prediction. Positive results and failed comparisons appear alongside their methodological limits.

[FAQ assets and build instructions](faq/README.md) describe how to regenerate the Word file and illustrations. [The evidence ledger](faq/reviews/current-evidence.md) records checked results, and [the revision record](faq/reviews/revision-record.md) records review findings and responses. The document check covers matching text across formats, evidence hashes, relative links, and SVG embedding; model training was not rerun.

Relative notebook links resolve within a repository checkout. The FAQ's supporting evidence and illustrations are preserved under `faq/`; the Word document embeds its illustrations and retains clickable literature references.
