Negating the persistence error is an outcome-informed diagnostic, not a newly prespecified endpoint. Its AUROC equals one minus the original AUROC; the reflected interval is conditional on the original bootstrap distribution. This finding motivates freezing motion and static-posture controls in the next study. It does not license reversing other scores to report success.
