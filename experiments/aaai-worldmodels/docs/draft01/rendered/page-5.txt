Reproducibility and scope
All 80 saved model checkpoints and the input arrays are
fingerprinted. The complete run contains source member-
ship for every outer and inner fold, model selection grids,
predictions, per-window and per-source errors, and source-
level anomaly scores. Bilateral shrinkage beta counts across
fold/leg fits are 0.5: 5/10, 1: 5/10. The prepared cohort re-
quired 46,783 recorded model calls and 990.0 seconds for
this invocation; fitting and scoring required 21.2 seconds.
These timings are descriptive of the local environment.
The method predicts a measured articulation sequence un-
der observational source shift. It is not a disease simulator, a
patient-specific digital twin, a causal intervention model, or a
validated clinical diagnostic system. Participant verification,
external clinical evaluation, and explicit action or treatment
data remain necessary extensions.
