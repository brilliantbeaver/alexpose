Errors average observed coordinate discrepancies within
leg, give legs equal window weight, average windows within
sequence, and average sequences within source. Training
samples source, sequence, and window hierarchically so long
videos do not dominate. Mask policies share the model, ob-
jective, windows, targets, three seeds, and normal-selected
duration. Neural predictions are averaged across seeds be-
fore computing error. Seeds measure optimization sensitivity
rather than additional subjects.
The primary anomaly endpoint is the paired within-fold
AUROC difference between the conditional two-sided can-
didate and low motion. AUROC is the probability that an ab-
normal group ranks above a normal group, with half credit for
ties. Fold AUROCs are weighted by their normal-abnormal
pair counts. We also report equal-fold and pooled rankings.
Average precision summarizes precision across recall levels
and depends on abnormal prevalence. Neither metric alone
is clinical diagnostic accuracy. Secondary controls include
normal-only posture distance, motion/quality distance, prefix
movement, missingness, marginal calibration, and matched
posture calibration.
Primary 95% intervals use 5,000 paired bootstrap resam-
ples of groups within fold and binary label. Forecast intervals
resample the 29 normal components. These intervals condi-
tion on saved overlapping fits and omit unknown participant
links and complete pipeline retraining variation. Two ad-
ditional grouped partitions repeat posture and posture-plus-
velocity fits as correlated sensitivities. Mask comparisons are
exploratory; we make no confirmatory significance claims
across the family.
External transfer freezes all weights, transforms, nuisance
regressions, directions, and normal references. For each of
fifteen calibration components, we average windows within
recording and recordings within coded participant. Prefix co-
variates follow the same hierarchy. We then apply the compo-
nent’s normal-reference transform and average its participant
tail scores. Aggregation must precede this nonlinear calibra-
tion. The change from source videos to repeated participant
recordings may alter score variance. No external participant
fits or calibrates the model. Paired external intervals use
5,000 participant bootstrap draws.

Current posture supplies useful short-horizon
prediction
The current-posture transition has normal-source MSE
0.07174, compared with 0.12126 for persistence (Table 2).
Its paired absolute improvement is 0.04952, with condi-
tional 95% interval [0.03447, 0.06628], a relative reduction
of 40.8%. This establishes useful prediction relative to the
tested reference. It does not establish that the model has re-
covered a physiological state or needs the detailed order of
past movement.

A model might improve on persistence merely by predict-
ing an average normal pose at each future query. To check that
simpler explanation, a separately declared post hoc diagnos-
tic adds two normal-training intercept priors, without tuning:
a mean pose for each future query and an anchor plus mean
displacement. They have MSE 0.10898 and 0.12235, respec-
tively. Posture improves on the mean-pose prior by 0.03724
[0.02539, 0.05150]. This supports the value of current po-
sitions beyond a constant prior within an outcome-exposed
exploratory comparison.
Structured masks do not improve the fixed task
Uniform masking gives MSE 0.07761, compared with
0.07962 for anatomical tubes and 0.07916 for motion bal-
ancing (the masking comparison). With improvement de-
fined as uniform error minus candidate error, the differences
are -0.00201 [-0.00426, -0.00002] and -0.00155 [-0.00311,
-0.00013]. These exploratory comparisons do not support a
benefit. The tube advantage over geometry-matched random
groups is 0.00042 [-0.00086, 0.00173], which also does not
establish an anatomical advantage. Every seed and policy
remains in the supplementary registry.
Shared prefix dropout, small coordinate noise, and
opposite-leg time shifts do not reverse the tube or motion-
balanced comparison with uniform masking. These pertur-
bations test observation dependence rather than neurologi-
cal interventions. Phase, learned-mask, latent-objective, and
stochastic-future extensions remain deferred under the de-
clared evidence gates. A broader search after these outcomes
would be a new study.
