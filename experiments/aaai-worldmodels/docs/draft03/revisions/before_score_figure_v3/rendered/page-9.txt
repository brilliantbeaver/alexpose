Calibration details and all rankings
Within each outer normal development set, three rotations
choose calibration recordings C and forecast-development
recordings R. A forecaster fitted on R predicts C and outer
test recordings. Three out-of-fit partitions within R produce
errors for fitting the nuisance regression. Each such forecaster
selects its regularization only within its own normal training
pool. C never enters tuning or nuisance fitting. Exact mem-
berships and hashes are stored for all fifteen components and
the matched posture family.
The nuisance regression standardizes log movement and
missingness within its fitting set. Leave-one-recording-out
selection chooses ridge strength from the four-value grid.
For n calibration residuals, cumulative probability is (0.5
plus the number of smaller residuals plus half the number
of ties) divided by n plus one. Endpoints remain finite, and
extreme residuals can tie. Marginal calibration ranks log fore-
cast MSE without nuisance conditioning. A one-sided arm
ranks conditional residuals directly. Neither replaces the pri-
mary after outcomes.
With n separate exchangeable normal calibration record-
ings, usual split-conformal resolution is one divided by n
plus one. Fewer than nineteen cannot supply the usual fi-
nite 5% upper-tail cutoff. Our seven- or eight-recording
subsets are smaller, and the cross-fitted mixture has no
automatic coverage guarantee. We therefore report no
95% clinical normality threshold. The floor is fixed at
0.00000001; notebooks inspect numerical floors and empir-
ical tail resolution. A separately reviewed post hoc sensitiv-
ity, reference-v2-floor-posthoc-v2, refits only
the nuisance reference at floors 0.0000000001, 0.00000001,
and 0.000001. All 91 final recording scores, pairwise rank-
ing directions, and primary intervals remain unchanged. Its
first replay attempt stopped when it omitted the producer’s
intermediate CSV write/read step. That omitted step changes
coarse empirical scores at machine precision and can change
exact ranking ties, so the failed attempt and corrected pro-
ducer path are both preserved. The original primary scores
are unchanged.
