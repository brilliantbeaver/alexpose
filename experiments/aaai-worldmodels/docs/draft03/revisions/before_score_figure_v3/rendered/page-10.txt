Physiology, robustness, and observation
quality
The timing control moves coordinate-validity pairs together
and preserves the current anchor. A fixed global feature per-
mutation would be inadequate because linear regression can
absorb it. A separately fitted window-specific shift and an in-
ference shift are therefore distinct controls. Any effect reflects
dependence within this representation; it is not an estimate of
basal-ganglia function, muscle modules, or selective motor
control.
Dropout removes 40% of eligible non-protected prefix to-
kens. Coordinate noise has standard deviation 0.02 prefix-
torso units. Patterns are fixed by window ID and shared
across models; future targets and support stay unchanged.
Opposite-leg stress changes one leg’s past when predicting
the other, preserving its latest observed anchor. The robust-
ness table retains all four conditions and seven models, in-
cluding observed-prefix replays.
The recording-identification diagnostic uses thirteen nor-
mal recordings in outer fold zero’s training pool with at least
two annotated sequences. A fixed regularized classifier learns
identity from the first sequence and evaluates the others. The
forecaster already used these recordings, and sequences may
overlap in physical frames. This is a recording-information
diagnostic rather than biometric validation or new-person
generalization. Random encoder features are retained; the
learned features show no recording-invariance advantage.
