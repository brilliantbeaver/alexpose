Reproduction
Targets are BlazePose joints 25, 27, 29, and 31 on the left,
and 26, 28, 30, and 32 on the right. Each leg requires at least
four observed joint-time points through four future samples
and eight through all eight samples. Each target joint also re-
quires at least one prefix observation. This common support
gate removes three of the 2,818 prepared windows for every
model. The completed run contains 80 saved fold, leg, and
variant checkpoints, some sharing fitted coefficients. There
are 29 normal source groups, not 80 independent replica-
tions. Evidence tables, hashes, notebook records, and source
generators accompany this manuscript. Figures and diagnos-
tics are regenerated from frozen artifacts; the manuscript
builder never refits a model.
