# Responses to independent research criticism

The review combines a method-focused GPT subagent, a primary-source citation check and an actual Claude Opus 4.8 call. The Claude call used the full instruction text with tools disabled; its [provenance](reviews/claude_provenance.json), [readable critique](reviews/claude_review.md) and exact raw JSON are retained. It did not inspect code, browse or run experiments. Later masking additions require their own review and are not certified by that earlier call.

The critique improved the specification but did not establish efficacy or novelty. The revised instructions retain unsuccessful-outcome paths. New experimental claims still require real-data execution and a fresh evidence review.

## Review of the later masking additions

Three parallel primary-source reviews informed the U/J/A comparison. The initial leg-only mixture would have changed distal-joint and boundary-time masking frequencies. The selected width-balanced anatomical law removes that confound. A single-joint eight-token core is also a closer structural-budget comparator than hiding eight entire joint trajectories. These are design controls, not measured performance gains.

The [independent exact checker](mask_design_verification.md) verifies 64 hidden tokens, one-half marginal probabilities, bilateral symmetry, no wrap, and E_leg target accounting. The coordinator reran it and reproduced the saved report. The instructions additionally fix a numerical own-leg context rule, bank-specific parameters on the same reserved reference identities, and matched marginal probabilities rather than identical finite random counts. Full model and GAVD validity still require future execution.
